# Starting channel = search-anchored titles, every slot

**Rule:** Until the channel has audience momentum, **every title slot must contain a head-term keyword anchor** — not just the hero. Pure structural plays (two-sentence paradox, specificity bomb, mystery question) without keyword anchors work for channels that already have a watch-history footprint. They don't work at 515 subs.

**Why:**
- 515 subs / 47 videos = no meaningful browse-feed traffic. The "browse" lever assumes the algorithm is already pushing the channel to a known audience. It isn't.
- Suggested-feed traffic clusters off competitor watch-history — meaning a video gets recommended next to a competitor's video only when YouTube already knows the channel's topic affinity. Starting channels have weak topic-affinity signals.
- Search is the only acquisition path that doesn't require an audience. Search keys off the literal text of the title (and to a lesser extent description/tags).
- Channels like Atun-Shei (482K), Shaun (2.4M), Historia Civilis (7.2M), Knowing Better (6M) can drop the head-term and still get views because they have audiences. Their structural patterns (two-sentence declarative, specificity bomb in parens) work *on top of* their audience momentum, not in place of it.

User explicit signal: "for my case specifically, starting channel, no audience" (2026-04-25, project 51 Treaty of Tripoli title rotation, after rejecting "Article 11 Is In English. The Arabic Is A Letter." — a structurally strong but keyword-bare two-sentence title).

**How to apply:**
- Every title slot must contain at least one of the topic's primary search anchors. For project 51: "Treaty of Tripoli" or "Article 11" must appear in every title.
- Niche structural patterns (two-sentence paradox, specificity bomb, scale signal, hidden chapter) are still valid LEVERS — but they must be applied *with* the keyword anchor, not as a replacement for it.
- Reject any title proposal that drops the head term in favor of "structural elegance," even if the structural pattern has strong outlier data. The data is from channels with audiences.
- This rule is conditional on subscriber count. When the channel crosses meaningful audience-momentum threshold (TBD — likely 5K+ subs with consistent browse traffic in YouTube Studio), revisit and relax.

**Connection to existing rules:**
- Extends [Channel Data Not Actionable](feedback-channel-data-too-small.md) — that rule said don't apply channel-specific n<30 patterns. This rule says: even when applying niche-wide outlier patterns, the channel-state context (no audience) determines which levers are available.
- Reinforces operational principle: at this scale, packaging strategy = SEO + competitor-cluster matching, not pattern bets that assume audience momentum.
