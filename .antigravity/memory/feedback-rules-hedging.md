---
name: Rules Should Hedge, Not Prescribe
description: Competitor-derived script rules must be suggestive (ideas/options) not prescriptive (mandates) — HvH's uniqueness may come from deliberately breaking competitor patterns
type: feedback
---

Competitor-derived rules (34-46) should be framed as tools/options, not mandates. "Consider X" not "must do X."

**Why:** We're ingesting competitor videos for IDEAS, not to copy them. Sometimes NOT including something competitors always do, or INCLUDING something they never do, is what makes HvH unique. Prescriptive rules ("every script must have X") eliminate the creative space to differentiate.

**How to apply:**
- Frame patterns as "high-performing competitors tend to..." not "you must..."
- Replace "no exceptions" with "break this deliberately when it serves your voice"
- Checklists should use "consider" not mandatory checkboxes
- Flag which patterns are confirmed by HvH's own data vs just competitor observation
- Structure-checker should flag as INFO/SUGGESTION, not WARNING, for competitor-derived patterns
- When encoding new findings, always ask: "would deliberately NOT doing this make us unique?"
