**Why myth-first (Rule 33)?** Non-territorial topic → myth-first drives 30.3% retention vs 22.4% for chronological. Opens with "the medieval ruling you know today isn't in the Quran" — immediate promise.

**Why three packaging variants?** Channel is 515 subs; low n<30 sample size per title type. Native rotation validates which frame (gentle/forensic/high-intensity) resonates. Data informs future topic packaging.

**Why Cromer only in colonial bridge?** User decision to strip state montage (Atatürk, Reza Shah, Saudi, Khomeini, France, Taliban) — maintain focus on how colonial law-making mindset persists. Collective "secular and Islamist states" frame sufficient.

**Why "free woman" anchor (Rule 37)?** The thesis hinges on the medieval distinction (slave ≠ free woman). Anchoring this word throughout forces viewer to hold the distinction through modern context shifts. Prevents "hijab = oppression" oversimplification.
