## Title Formula — Own Channel (from: patterns.md)

**Source:** 38 long-form videos analyzed (full backfill), pattern tools regenerated 2026-03-05

### IMPORTANT: Full Dataset vs Partial Dataset Shift

With 23 videos (partial), colon titles showed +29% CTR. With 38 videos (full), colon titles show **-28% CTR**.
This means the earlier colon signal was driven by the Guatemala/Belize outlier skewing a small sample.

**The corrected pattern:** Colon titles have HIGHER retention (+17%) but LOWER CTR (-28%). They attract committed viewers but fewer clicks. This is actually consistent with the earlier observation that colons get "fewer clicks but more sustained viewers."

| Pattern | CTR | Retention | Avg Views | Notes |
|---------|-----|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Other/generic | 3.8% | 30.7% | 259 | Best CTR |
| `[Topic]: [Subtitle]` (colon) | 2.4% | 37.3% | 505 | Higher views despite lower CTR (retention drives algorithm) |
| `The [X] That [Verb]` | 1.3% | 35.5% | 58 | NEVER use — worst CTR |

### Title Attributes
| Attribute | Effect on CTR | Effect on Retention |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Year in title (1914, 2024) | **-45.6%** CTR | Neutral |
| Number in title | -42.0% CTR | -16.1% retention |
| Question (?) | -36.3% CTR | -6.6% retention |
| Colon (:) | -28.1% CTR | **+16.9% retention** |
| Country name | -12.1% CTR | Neutral |

**Takeaway:** Nothing consistently IMPROVES CTR. But several things kill it (years, numbers, questions). The safest bet is a clean, direct title without gimmicks. The KGB video (5.3% CTR, 44.8% retention) and Kosovo video (5.1% CTR, 37.9%) both use plain statement titles.

## Niche Title Benchmark (from: niche-benchmark-findings.md)

**Source:** 388 videos, 8 edu/history channels (2026-03-18)

| Pattern | Niche frequency | Own CTR | Own n | Confidence | Strategy |
|---------|----------------|---------|-------|------------|----------|
| Declarative | 27% (n=104+) | 3.8% | 19 | HIGH | **Safe bet.** Most reliable pattern in niche AND own data. Default choice. |
| How/Why | 16% (n=61+) | 3.3% | 5 | HIGH | **Solid.** Well-established in niche. Good for mechanism videos. |
| Question | 2% (n=8+) | 2.4% | 1 | MEDIUM | **Risky.** Low own-data. Niche uses sparingly. |
| Colon | 17% (n=65+) | 2.3% | 4+ | HIGH | **Avoid.** -28% CTR penalty confirmed by both own and niche data. |
| Versus | 0.26% (n=1) | ~3.7% | 2 | **VERY LOW** | **Differentiation bet.** Nobody else uses it. Could be why it works OR noise. Use when natural, don't force. |

**Why:** Versus was previously framed as "highest CTR pattern" — but n=2 own-channel and 1/388 in niche means this is unproven. Declarative is the foundation. Versus is the experiment.

**How to apply:** Default to declarative titles. Use versus only when topic has genuine two-entity conflict. Never force versus framing.

## Hook Patterns — Niche Benchmark (from: niche-benchmark-findings.md)

**Source:** 31 verified hooks, 8 channels (2026-03-18)

| Pattern | Frequency | Best for | Example |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| cold_fact | 26% (8) | Territorial, political | Opens with specific number/date/measurement |
| myth_contradiction | 26% (8) | Ideological, political | States belief then contradicts |
| contextual_opening | 26% (8) | Long-form, loyal audience | Broad frame before narrowing |
| specificity_bomb | 13% (4) | Document-based, long-form | Hyper-specific named detail |
| authority_challenge | **0% (0)** | NOT USED in this niche | Names authority, shows them wrong |

**Why:** authority_challenge (naming experts and showing them wrong) was a theoretical pattern we tried to find examples of. Across 388 videos and 31 hooks from 8 channels, zero examples. The edu/history niche uses evidence-first hooks, not authority-challenging hooks. This aligns perfectly with the "Calm Prosecutor" voice.

**How to apply:**
- Prioritize cold_fact for territorial topics (the Belize hook worked: specific number + geographic detail)
- Prioritize myth_contradiction for ideological topics (state what people believe, show the evidence says otherwise)
- Stop recommending authority_challenge — it's not this niche
- contextual_opening works for loyal audience but is weakest for new viewer acquisition (no information gap)

## Opening Hook Patterns — SRT Analysis (from: scientific-findings.md)

**High retention openings (35%+) start with:**
- Specific number + shocking consequence ("In three months, fourteen countries divided a continent")
- Document reveal ("This is a law that stripped French citizens...")
- Conspiracy tease + debunk promise ("Some people are convinced the CIA blew it up")

**Low retention openings (<25%) start with:**
- Abstract context-setting ("In 1996, the Russian parliament declared...")
- Anecdote that delays the topic ("A farmer from Virginia named Jeremiah Heaton planted a flag...")
- Book reference without stakes ("Open Anderson's Popular History from 1880...")

**Rule:** First sentence must contain either a SPECIFIC NUMBER or a SHOCKING VERB. Never start with context.

## Research Extraction Implication (from: niche-benchmark-findings.md)

When doing Phase 2 (NotebookLM research), actively extract:
1. **Cold fact material:** specific numbers, dates, measurements, percentages that can open a video
2. **Myth contradiction material:** common beliefs about the topic that primary sources disprove
3. **Specificity bombs:** named documents, places, people that signal primary source access

These are the raw ingredients for hooks that work in this niche.

## Debunking Psychology (from: scientific-findings.md)

**Truth Sandwich approach** outperforms myth-first framing:
- Lead with truth -> name myth -> explain error -> restate truth
- Myth-as-statement can **detach from refutation in memory** — viewer remembers the myth more than the debunk
- **Framing debunks as questions** works better than myth statements ("Did medieval people really never bathe?" > "The myth that medieval people never bathed")
- Already integrated into Style Guide Part 4

**AI Trust Erosion (validates document-first strategy):**
- 90% of viewers want to know if content is AI-generated
- 52% become less engaged when they suspect AI
- ~10% of YouTube's 100 fastest-growing channels are AI-only
- Your "show the actual document" approach is a growing moat, not just a differentiator

**CTA Placement:**
- Only ~16% of viewers reach the final 10 seconds
- CTA at 60-70% mark (after strong evidence reveal) > end of video
- 72% of viewers skip CTAs in first 15 seconds
- Already integrated into Style Guide Part 4
