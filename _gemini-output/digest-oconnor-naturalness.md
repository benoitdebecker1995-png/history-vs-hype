After the claim "religion causes Wars well actually only 7% of all human wars were caused by religion," he counters: "right off the bat this isn't really an argument for atheism just for the idea that religion is harmful" (00:00:00.040).

## 4. Attributing sources aloud
*   **"a book by [Author] called [Title]"**: Direct and clear attribution of a specific work. "Fear not we do have one source that was flashed on screen a book by Richard Gallagher called demonic foes" (00:06:07.360).
*   **"[Author] writes in [Publication]"**: Specifies the author and the context of their writing. "Dr Gallagher writes in the Washington Post of how she knew things" (00:07:07.399).
*   **"my friend and biblical scholar [Name]"**: Uses a personal connection and credential to introduce a source, sometimes adding context about their stance. "my friend and biblical scholar John Nelson just wrote an article on this contradiction he's a Christian who used to think it could be reconciled but changed his mind" (00:11:37.959).
*   **"I have a whole podcast about this with [Collaborator]"**: Refers to his own content and the expert he discussed the topic with. "I have a whole podcast about this with Bart Ehrman" (00:17:27.039).
*   **"[Concept name] is defined by one website as..."**: For general concepts, he attributes to common understanding or general web sources without naming a specific one. "sonder which is defined by one website as the profound feeling of realizing that everyone including strangers passing in the street has a life as complex as one's own" (00:00:24.480).
*   **"enter [Name] who popularized [concept]"**: Introduces key figures associated with philosophical ideas. "but enter Nick Bostrom who popularized what's known as the simulation argument" (00:02:11.280).
*   **"one biographer of [Subject] states that quote..."**: For secondary sources or interpretations of a person's views, he uses general attribution to a biographer. *   **"Wikipedia tells me [Name] says that..."**: Acknowledges using a widely accessible reference for information. "another biographer of Everett Wikipedia tells me Peter Byrne says that Everett also privately discussed Quantum suicide" (00:34:55.239).
