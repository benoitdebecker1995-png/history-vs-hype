### 🚧 v9.0 Lean Agent-Orchestrated Workflow (In Progress)

**Milestone Goal:** Main context = orchestrator only. Heavy reads + reasoning delegated to sub-agents. Conversational flow via AskUserQuestion checkpoints. Aggressive deletion with soft-delete safety net (`_GRAVEYARD/`, 7-day quarantine). Rules codified in `.claude/AGENT-ORCHESTRATION.md` so the lean state survives past v9.0 — this is the load-bearing defense against regression-by-accretion (root cause of v1.0 Phase 05 not sticking). Folds in v8.0's 5 unfinished quality-gate requirements (BRIDGE-01/02, NLM-01/02/03) into Phase 77, since those gates live inside refactored commands.

**Scope:** 10 sub-phases × ~18 effort-days total. 30 requirements, 30 mapped. Zero orphans. (Phase 75 split into 75a/b/c/d — one command per sub-phase to de-risk the refactor pattern.)

- [ ] **Phase 75c: /greenlight Refactor** — Apply established pattern to /greenlight
