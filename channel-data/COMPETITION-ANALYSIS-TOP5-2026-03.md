## 1. Manhattan Purchase Myth

### Competition: WIDE OPEN

**Channels checked — none have covered this:**
Johnny Harris, Vox, RealLifeLore, Knowing Better, Shaun, Kraut, Kings and Generals, History Matters, WonderWhy, CaspianReport, OverSimplified, Extra History, Weird History, Adam Ruins Everything

**What exists:** Blog posts (Mental Floss, Live Science, Smithsonian), PBS documentary segments, academic papers. NO dedicated YouTube explainer.

### 5 Stacked Myth Layers

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| "Sold for $24" | $24 figure from 1844 conversion error. 60 guilders = ~$1,150 today |
| "Beads and trinkets" | Schagen letter doesn't mention beads. Comparable deeds list kettles, axes, cloth |
| "The Lenape sold their land" | Lenape had no concept of permanent land sale. Matriarchal — men who signed had no authority |
| "Bought from the right people" | Dutch dealt with Canarsee (Brooklyn). Manhattan was Wappinger territory. Had to pay AGAIN |
| "There's a deed" | No deed exists. Only source is one-paragraph Schagen letter (1626) |

### Anniversary Tailwind

- NYC "Founded by NYC" campaign ($215M cultural spending)
- "Unceded: 400 Years of Lenape Survivance" exhibition at Museum of City of NY
- Smithsonian Magazine already published anniversary coverage
- NY400 organization coordinating commemorations through 2026
- **Media saturation building but YouTube hasn't caught up**

### Primary Sources
- Schagen Letter (1626) — one paragraph, Dutch National Archives
- Charles Gehring's 12,000-page Dutch colonial record translations
- Russell Shorto's "The Island at the Center of the World" (2004, definitive)
- Staten Island deed (surviving comparison document)
- Yale Avalon Project has Schagen letter text online

### Verdict: **FILM IN APRIL. Publish late April/early May to ride the wave.**

## 3. Sabah Dispute (Philippines/Malaysia)

### Competition: ZERO from international channels

**Channels checked — none have covered this:**
Same 15 channels + TLDR News, Geography Now, The Infographics Show, Cogito, Knowledgia. ZERO.

**What exists:**
- Filipino nationalist/news channels (Tagalog, emotional, no documents): 27K-90K views
- AI-generated fabricated video (Philippine military publicly condemned it)
- Al Jazeera/WION news clips (2-5 min)

### The Untold $14.9B Story

Nobody on YouTube has told this: a colonial-era rent agreement nearly cost Malaysia $15 billion in a French court.

- Feb 2022: Paris arbitrator awards Sulu heirs $14.92B
- July 2023: Paris Court of Appeal annuls award
- Nov 2024: French Supreme Court rejects final appeal
- Jan 2026: Formal annulment — 1878 clause deemed "inapplicable"

### The "One Word" Document Angle

The 1878 agreement uses Malay word **"pajak"**:
- Philippines translates as "lease" (temporary)
- Malaysia translates as "cession" (permanent)
- Competing translations published side-by-side on Philippine Official Gazette
- **Nobody has shown this on screen.** Perfect document-autopsy.

### Risk
- Search demand may spike rather than sustain (tied to diplomatic incidents)
- Title needs to avoid "Sabah dispute" (too niche for non-ASEAN). Use: "One Word" or "$15 Billion" framing
- Filipino comment section will be heated

### Verdict: **GO. Document angle is perfectly suited to the channel. Even low-quality Filipino videos get 27K-90K.**

## 4. Hamoodur Rahman Report (Pakistan/Bangladesh 1971)

### Competition: ZERO on the report itself

**What exists on the broader 1971 topic:**
- Dhruv Rathee (36M subs): Indian-perspective video, likely millions of views
- Various Pakistani/Bangladeshi channels: nationalist narratives in Urdu/Bengali
- Al Jazeera: "Bangladesh: The Ghosts of 1971"
- Traditional documentaries (BBC, CIA archive)

**What does NOT exist:** A neutral, document-first video that quotes the report page by page and lets Pakistan's own investigation speak for itself.

### The Suppression Story

- 213 witness testimonies recorded
- 12 copies made, 11 destroyed
- Report found war crimes including mass rape
- Suppressed for 30 years (1972-2000)
- Leaked 2000, partially declassified under Musharraf
- Key portions STILL classified as Top Secret

### News Hooks (2024-2026)
- Imran Khan tweeted from jail urging Pakistanis to read it — triggered FIA investigation
- Bangladesh demanding formal apology + $4.5B reparations (2025)
- First foreign secretary-level talks in 15 years
- May 2025 academic publication analyzing the report

### Risk
- **Three-way nationalist trap:** Indian, Pakistani, and Bangladeshi audiences all have strong narratives. Must frame as "Pakistan's own investigation" to stay neutral.
- "Hamoodur Rahman" keyword alone = niche. Need broader title: "Pakistan's Secret War Crimes Report"
- Comment section will be intense from all three sides

### Verdict: **GO with careful framing. Enormous audience, suppressed-document angle is unique. Let the report speak for itself.**

### Risk
- **"Operation Legacy" = too niche as keyword.** Nobody searches for it.
- **"Mau Mau" = unfamiliar** to Western audiences
- **Search demand may be below 1K/month** for specific terms
- **No obvious map angle** for thumbnail — needs document/classified stamp visual
- Must frame broadly: "Britain Destroyed Its Colonial Records" not "Operation Legacy"

### Verdict: **GO — but title/packaging is critical. Frame as Britain's cover-up, not Kenya's uprising. The destruction orders are the hook.**
