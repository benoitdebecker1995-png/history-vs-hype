# Claims-on-Trial — Next-Episode Brief (2026-07-10)

## SERIES ARC

*Claims-on-Trial* puts ONE weaponized I/P claim (or a claim-pair) in front of the Calm Prosecutor: steelman both sides, hold each to the evidence, split the verdict where the evidence splits, and let a primary document — shown on screen — carry the ruling rather than the narrator's opinion. Pilot #59 proved the format on the 1947 Partition Plan (Resolution 181): the edge wasn't a new fact, it was showing the institutional mechanics (UN Palestine Commission, non-enforcement, the "84% farmland" Sub-Committee 2 report) that every incumbent (Vox 27M, Britain in Palestine, Unpacked, RealLifeLore) skips in favor of a moral shorthand ("Arabs rejected a fair deal"). **Identity guard: method-first, procedural/evidentiary — never a regional geopolitics explainer, never a present-day policy argument.** A candidate that only reads because of 2026 headlines is a hook, not an episode.
