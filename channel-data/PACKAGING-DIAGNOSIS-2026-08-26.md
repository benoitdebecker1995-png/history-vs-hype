# Packaging diagnosis — 26 August 2026

The evidence behind the claim that packaging, not writing or frequency, is the channel's binding
constraint. Established by grilling the Cowork plan on 26 August 2026.

**Sources.** Impressions and CTR from `analytics-exports/Table data.csv`, the YouTube Studio export
covering 22 July 2025 – 23 July 2026. Retention and view duration from the YouTube Analytics API via
vidIQ, 12 months to 25 August 2026. Publish dates from the Data API.

---

## 1. Frequency is not the constraint

39 long-form videos published between 1 October 2025 and 5 July 2026 — roughly weekly for forty
weeks. One breakout in week four (Guatemala vs Belize, 30,762) and a median in the low hundreds.

Weekly cadence has been run. It did not produce growth. Dormancy since July is real and worth
fixing, but it is seven weeks old against a ten-month pattern that ran *through* weekly publishing.

Six videos under 120 views hold viewers **longer** than the 30,762-view breakout. Treaty of Tripoli
retains 51.5%, the channel's highest, on 106 views. People who press play stay.

**The failure is entirely upstream of the play button.** Do not spend effort optimising the script
in pursuit of views. (Voice work still matters for identity and for the creator's own standard — it
is simply not the growth lever.)

## 3. Packaging is the constraint

| Group | Videos | Impressions | Views |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Served, not clicked (≥2k impr, CTR <4%) | 27 | 159,017 | 5,898 |
| Served and clicked (≥2k impr, CTR ≥6%) | 4 | 351,422 | 37,712 |
| Never served (<2k impr) | 22 | 25,191 | 1,169 |

YouTube served 159,017 impressions to videos that could not convert them.

The controlled comparison: **JD Vance took 9,969 impressions at 9.41% and made 1,163 views.
Israel/Palestine took 10,925 at 1.60% and made 296.** Near-identical distribution, four times the
views, nothing between them but the click.

CTR range across the catalogue: **0.46% to 9.41%.** One channel, one voice, one research standard.

**CTR also gates distribution.** The four clicked videos hold 66% of every impression the channel has
received. The 22 never-served videos were most likely tested, ignored and dropped.

Agent + act + stake beats myth + category + method. "London's Stock Exchange Funded a Genocide" took
7.45% on only 980 impressions — an institution, an action, a consequence.

**This convicts the channel's own signature move.** The claim-provenance videos — the move
`CHANNEL.md` calls distinctly Benoit's — sit in the bottom band: Manhattan 0.46%, Spanish Inquisition
1.82%, Thermopylae 2.19%, Sol Invictus 2.48%, Flat Earth 3.65%.

But provenance is not unpackageable. **"JD Vance Claims Christians Found Child Sacrifice" is a
provenance video** — a claim traced to where it entered the record — packaged as a live conflict with
a named human being. It took 9.41%. The same method, packaged as a correction of an abstraction, took
0.46%.

Provenance has been titled like a seminar. That is a fixable problem.

## 5. Status of this finding

The **diagnosis** is solid: retention exonerates the writing, 159,017 unclicked impressions convict
the packaging.

The **rule is an UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS.** Not a finding, not guidance, and it must not be used as a
gate.

Sharpened 27 August after `title_scorer`'s composite was measured against the same data and found
non-predictive (ADR-0029: Spearman +0.173; its perfect scores average 2.67% CTR against a 3.05%
channel mean). **That tool was almost certainly built the way this rule was built** — by reading
patterns off this catalogue and never checking them against held-out outcomes. This rule was derived
by eyeballing the same 47 rows. It currently has exactly the standing of the thing it replaced.

The winners may also have clicked because their subjects were live conflicts, with actor-and-act
titles a symptom rather than the cause.

**What would settle it:** the three-video retitle test — Berlin Conference, Medieval Literacy, Sol
Invictus. Title only, thumbnails untouched, predicted CTR written down before the change, read at 28
days. It holds the subject fixed, which is the one thing that separates the rule from the confound.
Until it reads out, the honest position is that this channel has **no validated title instrument at
all.**

**Known gap.** These impressions figures end 23 July 2026. The CTR collector has been broken since
28 July and the analytics API surface in use does not expose impressions. Current per-video
impressions are unknown, so it cannot be said whether the quiet videos are being served-and-ignored
or not served at all.
