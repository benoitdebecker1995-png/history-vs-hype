## 1. NO-FACE is a durable break
Competitor shelves are face-heavy (south-china-sea 50%, stalin 88%, tariff 62%). A no-face thumbnail breaks the dominant pattern. Cross-validates the channel's existing 0%-face niche rule from the competitor side.

## 2. COMPRESSION is durable whitespace
The COMPRESSION operation (one number/word slammed large as the hero) is chronically absent across shelves (fully absent on dark-ages, gibraltar, south-china-sea; minimal elsewhere). A repeatable differentiation lever, not a one-off — reach for it when a thumbnail can lead with a single hard number/word.

## 3. Non-blue is a break on GEOPOLITICAL/TERRITORIAL shelves (narrowed)
Blue dominates the geopolitical/territorial shelves (gibraltar, south-china-sea, tariff). A high-contrast non-blue palette breaks the shelf on those topic types. NOT universal — myth (dark-ages: red/yellow) and 20th-c. history (stalin: brown/black/red) shelves are not blue, so apply this only to territorial/geopolitical topics.
