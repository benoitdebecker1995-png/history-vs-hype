## 5. Recommendations for Our Description Template

1. **Timestamps are optional in this niche** (29% usage), but still recommended for viewer experience and search snippets.
2. **Cite sources in description.** 43% of niche does this. This is our competitive advantage — lean into it harder than competitors.
3. **Target description length:** 2,030 chars (niche average). Comprehensive descriptions (1500+) correlate with educational channels.
4. **Hashtags are used by 26% of niche.** Include 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end for discoverability.
5. **First 2 lines are critical.** They appear in search results and above the fold. Lead with the thesis or a specific claim, NOT a generic topic summary.

## 6. Description Formula

Based on top-performing competitors and niche patterns:

```
LINE 1: Thesis statement or strongest specific claim (this shows in search)
LINE 2: What this video covers / unique angle (second search-visible line)

PARAGRAPH: 2-4 sentence summary expanding on the thesis.
Include the main keyword naturally 2-3 times.

TIMESTAMPS
0:00 - [Chapter title]
X:XX - [Chapter title]
...

SOURCES
[Academic citations with author, title, publisher, year, page numbers]
[Primary document references]

[1-line subscribe CTA]

#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #Hashtag3 #Hashtag4 #Hashtag5
```

**Key principles:**
- First 2 lines = SEO real estate. No wasted words.
- Timestamps = chapters in YouTube UI. Always include for videos >5 min.
- Sources section = trust signal AND keyword density (author names, book titles).
- Hashtags at END, not beginning (YouTube shows first 3 hashtags above title if in first lines).
- Target 1000-2000 chars total for comprehensive/educational content.
