## Interpreted Findings (Human Analysis)

### CAUTION: Intro confound
The `personal_authority` finding (-0.044 avg delta) is misleading. 86% of personal_authority moments occur in the first third (avg position 0.13), which is where ALL videos lose 15-25% of viewers regardless of content type. Mid-video personal_authority is essentially flat (-0.0001). **The "I read that treaty" pattern is NOT causing drops — it's happening during the natural intro hemorrhage.**

### What the data actually shows

1. **Statistics are retention gold.** Highest positive rate (61%) of any content type. Late-video statistics actually GAIN viewers (+0.001 delta). Numbers anchor attention. Use them throughout, especially in the closing third.

2. **Plain narration is the safest vehicle.** Lowest avg drop (-0.005), highest count (1,654 data points). Causal chain narration — the Kraut-style "consequently, thereby, which meant that" — is what holds viewers through the middle. Don't interrupt good narration with unnecessary pattern interrupts.

3. **Modern relevance bridges may disrupt flow.** At -0.010 avg delta, modern_relevance performs worse than narration, quotes, or primary sources. This doesn't mean "cut modern relevance" — it means the TRANSITION to/from modern relevance may be jarring. Use smoother bridges: weave modern stakes into the narration rather than interrupting with "and this still matters today."

4. **Quotes and primary sources perform similarly.** Both hold ~55% of the time. The key is pairing them with visual evidence on screen (which we can't measure from SRT data alone, but the pattern is consistent with STYLE-GUIDE.md recommendations).

5. **The intro is the only real danger zone.** All top-10 worst moments are in the first 2-4% of videos. After the intro drop, retention stabilizes. The mid-video is NOT where viewers leave — they leave in the first 30 seconds or they stay.

6. **Recovery happens during narration + statistics.** 7 of the top 10 best retention moments are narration or statistics in the 22-87% position range. The Serbia video gains viewers THREE times during narration sections — proving that a compelling causal chain can actually pull viewers back.

### Actionable Rules

| Rule | Evidence | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| Front-load a specific number | Stats have 61% positive rate | First 10 seconds should include a date, number, or measurement |
| Don't interrupt narrative momentum | Narration -0.005 vs modern_relevance -0.010 | Weave modern stakes INTO narration rather than using separate "this matters today" sections |
| Save strongest statistics for closing | Late stats: +0.001 (actually gains) | Put the most shocking number in the final 2 minutes |
| "I read..." is fine after minute 3 | Mid personal_authority: -0.0001 | Keep authority signals in the hook (first 60s) and mid-video, not in the 1-3 minute zone |
| The intro drop is not fixable | 10/10 worst moments are intro | Accept 15-20% intro loss. Focus on making the remaining 80% stay through strong evidence |
