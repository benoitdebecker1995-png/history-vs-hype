## 2. A3 (the Caribbean road) — OVER-CONCEDED. Do not put this in the pinned comment as written.

The draft pinned comment has you concede that *"the video makes it sound as though Guatemala has no
Caribbean access at all, and that is wrong."* **The video does not say that.** What it says is:

> "This wasn't just any road. Guatemala had Pacific ports with almost no Atlantic access. Their
> Caribbean coastline was tiny and underdeveloped."

Past tense, scoped to 1859. That is accurate for 1859 — the Carretera al Atlántico opened in **1959**,
a century later. The video never claims Guatemala lacks Caribbean access today. And "No road was ever
built, not even with Guatemala's own money" is scoped to the 1859 treaty road, which is also correct.

**The commenter was answering a claim about the present that the video makes about the past.**

**Verdict: not an error in the video.** Conceding it would be its own accuracy failure — on a channel
whose product is accuracy, over-correcting is not the safe direction. If you want to address it, add
context rather than a correction: the 1859 road was Guatemala City to Belize City through the disputed
zone, and Guatemala later built its own route to Puerto Barrios in 1959.

## 3. The ICJ error is IN THE VIDEO, not only in the replies. This is the serious one.

The 24 August file records this as *"confirmed error, in a reply."* It is worse than that. From the
video's own narration, in the first minute:

> "Right now the International Court of Justice is deciding whether Belize should even exist.
> **Oral hearings are in 2026. The ruling is expected for 2027.**"

Against the ICJ docket, case 177 (*Guatemala's Territorial, Insular and Maritime Claim*) has had **no
oral hearings held and none scheduled**; its last procedural step was the Order of 24 June 2022. The
November 2025 hearings belonged to case 185, the Sapodilla Cayes intervention, which the Court
resolved on 19 March 2026.

**This is stated as fact, in the narration, in the first sixty seconds, on the channel's most-watched
video — and it is the exact class of procedural claim the channel's authority rests on.** It should
lead the pinned comment, not sit at the bottom of it.

Two smaller items in the same passage, both confirmed in the narration:

- **"Guatemala finally emerged as an independent republic in 1839"** — the A4 error, verbatim.
