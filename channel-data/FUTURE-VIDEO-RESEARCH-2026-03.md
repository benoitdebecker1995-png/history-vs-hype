## Selection Criteria

Every topic below was evaluated against the channel's proven success formula:
1. **Search demand** — Are people searching for this? (>1K/mo minimum)
2. **Primary source angle** — Is there a document, treaty, court ruling, or law to show on screen?
3. **Myth vs reality gap** — Do people believe something wrong about this?
4. **Geographic monopoly** — Large English-speaking population with zero quality competition?
5. **News hook / anniversary** — Is there a reason to watch NOW?
6. **Competitor gap** — Has anyone done this well with primary sources?
7. **Map/thumbnail potential** — Can this make a compelling visual?

### 3. Manhattan Purchase Myth — The $24 Story Is Wrong in Every Way

**Anniversary hook: 400th anniversary in May 2026.**

- **Search demand:** Very high ("Peter Minuit Manhattan" + "Dutch purchase Manhattan" = steady evergreen + anniversary spike incoming)
- **Primary sources:** Peter Schagen's letter (1626, the only contemporary source), Staten Island deed (surviving comparison document), Dutch West India Company records
- **Myth vs reality:** MASSIVE gap.
  - Myth: "Manhattan was bought for $24 in beads"
  - Reality: 60 guilders (~$1,150 today), probably iron kettles/tools/cloth (NOT beads — no evidence), the Lenape didn't believe land could be "sold," the deed itself is LOST, and the $24 figure was a bad conversion from 1844
- **Geographic monopoly:** No — but MASSIVE American audience + 400th anniversary = guaranteed search spike
- **News hook:** **400th anniversary falls May 2026.** Media coverage will be everywhere. This is a time-sensitive opportunity.
- **Competitor gap:** Surprisingly low for primary-source treatment. Lots of pop history, nobody shows the actual Schagen letter or explains the cultural misunderstanding with documents.
- **Thumbnail:** Historical map of Manhattan + Schagen letter fragment, text overlay "$24?" or "THE LIE"
- **Draft titles:**
  - "Manhattan Was Never Sold for $24. The Real Story Is Worse." (myth contradiction, two-sentence)
  - "Everything You Know About the Manhattan Purchase Is Wrong." (declarative with scale word)
- **Why this is #3:** Anniversary timing makes this urgent. American audience is huge. Myth is universally believed. Document (Schagen letter) exists to show on screen.
- **DEADLINE: Film by April, publish late April/early May 2026 to ride the anniversary wave.**

- **Competitor gap:** Very low. Academic resources exist online but no YouTube channel has done a clause-by-clause reading with on-screen text.
- **Format:** Direct sequel to the Vichy Statut des Juifs video — "Untranslated Evidence" series.
- **Draft title:** "France Wrote a Law Making People Property. Here's What It Actually Says."
- **Estimated demand:** Medium (slavery content is evergreen, but "Code Noir" is niche search term)

### 7. The Requerimiento (1513) — Spain Read This Document Before Conquering You

- **Primary sources:** Full text survives. The document was read IN SPANISH to Indigenous peoples who didn't speak Spanish, sometimes to empty beaches, sometimes from ship decks.
- **Myth vs reality:** Spanish conquest is usually framed as military. The legal fiction — that Spain LEGALLY REQUIRED Indigenous peoples to submit — is far more absurd and revealing.
- **Competitor gap:** Very low for document-based treatment. History classes mention it but nobody has shown the text on screen and read it through.
- **Draft title:** "Spain Read This Document to Indigenous Peoples. In Spanish. Then Conquered Them."
- **Connects to:** Treaty of Tordesillas (already in production). Could be a companion video.

### 8. Herero and Nama Genocide — Germany's Extermination Order, Word by Word

- **Primary sources:** Von Trotha's extermination order (October 2, 1904): "Within the German boundaries, every Herero, with or without firearms, with or without cattle, will be shot. I won't accommodate women and children anymore." The Kaiser's withdrawal order (December 6). The second extermination order against the Nama (April 22, 1905).
- **Myth vs reality:** Often called "the first genocide of the 20th century" — predates the Holocaust by 40 years. Germany only formally acknowledged it in 2021. Many people have never heard of it.
- **Geographic monopoly:** Namibia is small (2.5M) but South Africa (60M English speakers) and global audience interested in genocide/colonial history.
- **Draft title:** "Germany Wrote an Extermination Order. In Africa. Before the Holocaust."
- **Connects to:** Berlin Conference (in production). Colonial Africa cluster.
