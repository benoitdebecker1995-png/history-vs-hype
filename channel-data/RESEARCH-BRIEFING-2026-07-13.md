  internal Iranian actors (the clergy, the army, the bazaar). Adjudicate *how much* Ajax caused vs
  catalyzed, with the declassified cable traffic on screen. That's the Calm-Prosecutor move.
- **Keyword ladder (channel formula):** famous parent `operation ajax` / `cia coups` buys the click →
  obscure document reveal (`the Wilber report`, `TPAJAX`, the specific cable) is the payoff.
- **Next step:** `/greenlight` (demand already clears; needs hook web-verification + whitespace SERP
  scan + competitor-gap — Kings & Generals and others have narrative versions, so the *document-forensic*
  angle is the whitespace to confirm).
