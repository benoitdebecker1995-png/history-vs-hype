## Cross-video bridges (THE MOST VALUABLE)
**Uti Possidetis Principle** — 1-somaliland-2025, 40-berlin-conference-1884-2026, 41-treaty-tordesillas-2026. The principle of respecting existing colonial borders, used by AU for Somaliland, by African states post-Berlin, and by Portugal in the Treaty of Madrid to abandon Tordesillas.
**Colonial Powers Drawing Lines on Maps They Haven't Seen** — 40-berlin-conference-1884-2026, 41-treaty-tordesillas-2026, 43-india-pakistan-partition-2026. Europeans divided territories with little to no on-the-ground knowledge, creating lasting geopolitical consequences.
**Historical Negligence in Treaty Making** — 1-somaliland-2025, 43-india-pakistan-partition-2026, 41-treaty-tordesillas-2026. Treaties made without proper ratification, with insufficient geographical knowledge, or under immense time pressure led to long-term conflict.
**Influence of International Law/Treaties on National Sovereignty and Borders** — 1-somaliland-2025, 14-chagos-islands-2025, 35-gibraltar-treaty-utrecht-2026, 41-treaty-tordesillas-2026. Formal legal instruments, even flawed or ignored, create enduring frameworks for state claims and identity.
**US Covert Actions and Foreign Intervention** — 24-iran-1953-coup-2025, 34-operation-condor-2025. The US used covert means to influence political outcomes in other nations, often with significant long-term negative consequences for democracy and human rights.
**The "Conflict Thesis" in Science and Religion** — 19-flat-earth-medieval-2025, 51-treaty-tripoli-article-11-2026. The 19th-century idea that science and religion are inherently at war shaped narratives like the flat earth myth and influenced readings of early American secularism.
**Persistence of Legal Fictions Despite Material Reality** — 19-flat-earth-medieval-2025, 31-bermeja-island-2025, 41-treaty-tordesillas-2026. Concepts or entities that never existed (flat earth, Bermeja Island) or were physically unmeasurable (Tordesillas line) continued to shape beliefs and physical borders.
**Secrecy and Documentation Fueling or Debunking Myths** — 37-untranslated-vichy-statut-juifs-2026, 51-treaty-tripoli-article-11-2026, 54-spanish-inquisition-torture-rules-2026. The absence or presence of specific documents (Vichy annotations, Treaty of Tripoli Arabic text, Inquisition instructions) can reveal hidden truths or expose propaganda.
