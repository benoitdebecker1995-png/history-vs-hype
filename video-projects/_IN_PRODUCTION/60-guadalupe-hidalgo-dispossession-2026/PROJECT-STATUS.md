## Stage C deep-research findings (2026-06-13, second pass — all NLM-anchored)
**Verified chronological spine:**
- 1848 Feb 2 treaty → Mar 10 Senate strikes Art. X → May 26 Protocol of Querétaro → **Feb 1849 Sec. Clayton disavows the Protocol** ("at no time regarded the Protocol as obligatory" — Miller vol.5, verbatim).
- **Surveyor General era 1854–1891** (Act July 22 1854, 10 Stat. 308): "passive agent," no due process / no witness funds. 1858–79 Congress confirmed 46 grants / 8,636,673 ac; by 1880 acted on only ~150 of ~1,000 claims; after 1879 stopped confirming. Santa Fe Ring (Catron, Elkins) exploited delays (Maxwell inflated 97k→1.7M ac).
- **CPLC 1891–1904:** NM heard 231 grants / 34,653,340 ac → confirmed **80 grants / 1,934,986 ac ≈ 6%**. (24% combined SG+CPLC; 6% CPLC-alone; distorted by 12.5M-ac Peralta-Reavis fraud.)
