## Tier 1 — Primary Sources [P]

#### [P1] Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo — ratified text, English + Spanish (Feb 2, 1848; ratifications exchanged May 30, 1848)
- **Type:** Treaty (primary) — incl. Senate-amended Article IX and the struck Article X
- **Access:** Avalon Project (Yale Law); National Archives (NARA) has the engrossed original — both digitized, free
- **Key content:** Articles VIII (property/citizenship default), IX (amended citizenship language), X (struck land-grant guarantee)
- **Provenance:** The instrument itself; English + Spanish executed as equally authentic (NO English-prevails clause — see killed claim)
- **On-screen:** YES — the document spine
- **Status:** Pending

#### [P2] Protocol of Querétaro (May 26, 1848)
- **Type:** Diplomatic clarification (primary)
- **Access:** Reproduced in Griswold del Castillo appendix; text online
- **Key content:** US negotiators' assurance that striking Article X did not impair existing grants; later **disavowed by Polk (1849)**
- **Provenance:** Post-ratification clarification; bias = US reassurance to secure Mexican ratification
- **Status:** Pending

#### [P3] U.S. Senate Executive Journal — ratification & amendments (Mar 10, 1848)
- **Type:** Government record (primary) — documents the 38–14 vote, Article X deletion, Article IX modification
- **Access:** Library of Congress / A Century of Lawmaking
