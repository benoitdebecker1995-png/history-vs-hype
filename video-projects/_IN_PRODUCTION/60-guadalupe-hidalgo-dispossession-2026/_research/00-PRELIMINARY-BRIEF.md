## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo (signed Feb 2, 1848) promised roughly 80,000 Mexicans in the ceded territories two things in writing: keep your property, and get U.S. citizenship. The legal machinery delivered neither at scale. Before ratifying (Mar 10, 1848), the U.S. Senate **weakened Article IX** (deferred citizenship to "the proper time, to be judged of by Congress") and **deleted Article X entirely** — the clause that would have honored *all* Spanish/Mexican land grants. The Protocol of Querétaro (May 26, 1848) tried to paper over this by insisting the deletion didn't annul grants — but President Polk **disavowed the Protocol in 1849**. Then a 40-year adjudication machine — the Surveyor General of New Mexico (1854) → Court of Private Land Claims (1891–1904) — applied alien "perfect title" standards that converted ~33 million claimed acres into the U.S. public domain. **The angle competitors miss: the dispossession was legal, procedural, and bureaucratic — not a battlefield event.** That HOW-not-WHY mechanism is pure channel DNA.

## ⚠ CRITICAL VERIFICATION TARGET: "English version prevails over Spanish"

**VERDICT: NOT-FOUND → leaning CONTRADICTED.**

- **Wikipedia (main article):** *"There is **no clause** in the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo that makes the English version prevail over the Spanish. Both versions were signed as equally authentic."*
- **JSTOR Daily annotated treaty:** No mention of any English-prevails clause; presents the bilingual text with Article X marked "[Stricken out]"; no language-hierarchy discussion.
- **Nuance to carry into Phase 2:** The *real* language story is the opposite direction — the **Protocol of Querétaro** was later invoked by *Mexico* to argue for the Spanish/pro-grant interpretation, which the **U.S. disavowed**. So the interpretive conflict existed, but it was a treaty-vs-protocol dispute, NOT an English-prevails-clause.

**Action:** Do NOT script the "English prevails" claim as fact. The podcast source appears unreliable. If the script needs a language angle, use the Protocol-disavowal interpretive fight instead, and confirm against Miller's *Treaties* Vol. 5 (the authoritative documentary text) in Phase 2.
