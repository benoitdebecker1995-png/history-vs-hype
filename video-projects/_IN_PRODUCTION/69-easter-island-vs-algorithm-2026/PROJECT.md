## The thesis

Facts are commoditised; the audit trail is not. **You cannot check a claim whose source has been
removed.** Video A proves it: right answers, real studies, and a viewer who tries to verify hits a
dead end because Bárbara Sousa da Mota became "Barbara Susa Damota."

The general "AI slop is ruining history" take is occupied (404 Media, several creators, YouTube's own
CEO) and every version argues from inaccuracy. Ours argues from provenance, on a specimen that is
mostly *correct*.

## Current unresolved problem

**Both script blockers are closed** (3 September, `_research/STAGE-B-PART2-ORIGINS.md`):

- **Width:** 2.83 m is the statue's overall width, 2.64 × 2.10 m its base footprint — same statue,
  same paper, never in conflict. **Use 2.83 m** (the figure most favourable to B): road 4.5 m ÷
  2.83 m = **1.59×**. Plus a third measurement nobody had noticed — **Heyerdahl**, excavating in the
  1950s, reported the roadway *"slightly wider than the moai base."*
- **Database pass:** B's 11-bearings claim **was located**. A self-published Blogspot post from

  author reports **42% of the bearings work**. B reports 100%. Beat 4 is rewritten around this. The
  0.4° claim also has a real anchor: Ahu Huri A Urenga, sub-degree, **one site**. Still unlocated:
  the 11 count itself, and the 0.3°→2.8° curve.

a **copy of a 27 June video** (The Silent Clue, 148,718 views) whose figures it reproduces exactly —

video quotes **four named experts who cannot be located**, and Specimen B **removed them** while
