## Why this is the angle and not the obvious one

The obvious video is "AI slop is wrong, here's the real Easter Island." That video is **taken** — 404
Media, several YouTubers, and YouTube's own CEO have all run the "AI is flooding history" argument, and
all of them argue from **inaccuracy**.

This one argues from **provenance**, across three centuries, on one island. It is the channel's
signature move — take a claim everyone repeats, find where it entered the record, watch it grow — aimed
at a subject with mass recognition and an active 2026 news hook. **Nobody has made it.**

It also converts the format's weakness into its strength. A debunk dates. **A 300-year pattern doesn't.**

## The spine — six documented inventions, one island

Every rung is sourced in `STAGE-B-VERIFICATION.md` and `_research/sources/`.

**1 · 1722 — misrepresentation begins at first contact.**
Roggeveen's expedition arrives. Captain Cornelius Bouman: *"The inhabitants did not have any weapons
whatsoever. They approached us in crowds with their empty hands to welcome us gamboling and jumping of
joy. Despite this, **9 or 10 of them were killed by shot**… This caused great offense."* Behrens' diary
regrets it; Roggeveen's own account calls it a crew's misunderstanding.
Then an anonymous Dutch sailor writes that the islanders are *"of the most gigantic size… they
measured, ones with others, **twelve feet high**… we could have passed between the legs of these sons
of GOLIAT without bending."*
Arthur (2015): these first writings *"were also responsible for **initiating a tradition of
misrepresentation on Rapa Nui**."*

**2 · The collectors' invention — a lie with a purpose.**
Later collectors justified removing moai by claiming the Rapanui *"showed a complete lack of interest
on their moai."* **The 1722 eyewitnesses recorded the exact opposite.** Behrens: *"these poor people
entrust themselves entirely to the help of their idols."* Roggeveen: they *"light bonfires before some
particularly tall stone images, and then sitting on their heels with their heads bended, they bring the
palms of their hands together."*
> **This is the template for everything that follows: invent a claim about the statues, contradicted by
> the actual record, because the invention pays.**

**3 · 1862 — the real catastrophe, and it comes from outside.**
Peruvian slavers take roughly **1,500 people, near half the island**; smallpox follows; ~110 alive by
the 1870s. Among the taken are the last people who could read rongorongo. *(Maude, "Slavers in
Paradise," Stanford UP 1981 — ch. 3, "The Easter Island Trinket Trade.")*

**4 · 2005 — the parable.**
Diamond: Easter Island is *"the clearest example of a society that destroyed itself by overexploiting
its own resources,"* and *"What did the Easter Islander who cut down the last palm tree say while he
was doing it?"*
**Treat him fairly — he is the most careful link in this chain.** He gives a range (*"6,000 to a high of
30,000"*), calls his own metaphor *"imperfect,"* and writes that on climate *"we have no evidence at
present, though it may emerge in the future."* The parable travelled. **The hedges did not.**

**5 · 2019–2025 — named humans, slowly, take the parable apart.**
DiNapoli 2019 (ahu on freshwater, *and explicitly only the island's eastern portion*); Davis 2024
(rock gardens **0.76 km²**, ceiling ~**3,901**); Moreno-Mayar, Sousa da Mota & Malaspinas 2024 (*"we
reject a scenario involving a severe population bottleneck during the 1600s"*); the 2025 drought study
— **supplying exactly the climate evidence Diamond said might emerge**; Lipo & Hunt 2025 (962 statues,
a 4.35-tonne replica walked **100 m in 40 minutes by 18 people**); Lipo, Hunt & **Pakarati** 2025 (30
quarry foci — decentralised, no central authority needed).

**6 · 2026 — the newest projector.**
Two videos, ~**956,000 views**, both claiming an AI was handed everything and found what humans missed.
**They contradict each other.** A tells you the roads are where statues walked; B tells you the roads
are too narrow for statues to have ever passed — while the paper says the roads run **~4.5 m** wide
(and Charlie Love measured ~5.5 m) against a **2.83 m** statue. B invents 11 navigational bearings, and
pukao "acoustic notation" — in a literature whose actual pukao study, **with a physicist on the author
list**, contains the words acoustic, resonance, frequency, sound and vibration **zero times**.
And A, the accurate one, still drops DiNapoli's own scope limit and garbles **Bárbara Sousa da Mota**
into "Barbara Susa Damota" — so a viewer who tries to check hits a dead end.

## The turn (the thing that makes it a History vs Hype video)

Every invention on that list **had a job to do.** Impress readers at home. Justify taking the statues.
Sell a parable about our own future. Sell clicks.

**Rapa Nui is never the subject. It is the surface.** And each retelling deletes the same thing — the
people. In 1722 they became giants. In the 1860s they became cargo. In 2005 they became a warning. In
2026 they became content, and even the *scientists* got deleted.

## The close — why a human channel is worth anything

The only thing that has ever corrected any of this is **someone going back to the documents and asking
who said that, when, and why they wanted it to be true.**

Facts are cheap now. **The audit trail is not.** A content farm cannot give you one — not because it is
badly made, but because it scraped the conclusion and threw the citation away. **It is not a source. It
is a break in the chain.**
