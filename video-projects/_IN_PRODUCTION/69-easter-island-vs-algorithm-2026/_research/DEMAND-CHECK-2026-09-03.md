# Demand check — #69, history-first version

**Run 3 September 2026, before rebuilding the beat sheet.** Two instruments: keyword demand, and the
small-channel outlier gate (the channel's pre-registered F3 — outliers beat keyword volume).

**Verdict: demand confirmed. Build it. Three conditions on packaging.**

---

## 1. KEYWORD DEMAND — rising sharply, and the growth is the story

vidIQ keyword research, metrics as of 3 Sep 2026.

| Term | Est. monthly | vs baseline | Competition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **easter island documentary** | **27,770** | **+111.5%** | **38.6 — low** |
| **easter island statues** | **15,049** | **+240.6%** | 45 |
| **moai statues** | **27,337** | **+202.7%** | 45.5 |
| rapa nui | 40,251 | +17.5% | 52.6 |
| easter island | 66,734 | −16.6% | 49 |
| moai | 69,409 | −25.3% | 54.5 |
| easter island mystery | 5,391 | −45.8% | 43 |
| **easter island history** | **<750** | — | 40 |

### Read this carefully — three things

**a) The clean signal is "easter island documentary": 27,770/month, up 111%, competition 38.6.**
High volume, low competition, doubling. That is the strongest single row in the table and the one to
build the package on.

**b) ⚠ "Easter island history" is below the 750/month floor.** Effectively nobody searches for it.
**People are not looking for the history of Easter Island. They are looking for the statues and the
documentary.** This does not argue against a history video — it argues against a history *title*.

**c) ⚠ Two figures must not be used.** "Moai" at 69,409 has Mexico as its top market at 27% — that
search volume is substantially the 🗿 emoji meme, not interest in Rapa Nui. And "easter island" at
66,734 shows top markets **Vietnam 27%, France 14%, Pakistan 9%** — a distribution that does not look
like an English-speaking history audience. "Rapa nui" is **67% Vietnam**. Do not build on those
three numbers; build on the documentary/statues rows.

### Where the growth is coming from — and the risk in it

A +240% on "easter island statues" and +203% on "moai statues" inside the window when five AI videos
put 1.3M views into this subject is **almost certainly demand created by the slop wave itself.**

That is good for us — the audience is assembled and looking right now — but it is **borrowed demand
and it can decay.** This is a reason to ship soon, and a reason not to treat these numbers as a
standing property of the topic.

## 2. THE OUTLIER GATE — small channels DO break out here

`vidiq_outliers`, keyword "easter island moai rapa nui", **maxSubscribers 100,000**, long-form, past
year. The relevant hits:

| Video | Channel subs | Views | Breakout |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Easter Island's Moai Were Reanalyzed by Grok AI" | **8,340** | 494,046 | **1,156** |
| "Easter Island Was Reanalyzed by AI" | **4,450** | 463,075 | **647** |
| **"The Hidden Megalithic Walls of Easter Island Almost Nobody Sees"** | **5,680** | **72,572** | **586** |
| "Rapa Nui: Where life and the ocean are one" | 92,900 | 73,295 | 685 |
| "Scientists Used AI to Reexamine Easter Island" | 43,600 | **10,693** | **3.1** |

**The gate passes.** Channels at 4,450 and 5,680 subscribers — the same order of magnitude as this
one — have taken 463,000 and 72,000 views on this subject in the last month. This is not a subject
that only large channels can win.

### ⚠ But read the composition honestly

- **Three of the four breakouts are AI slop.** The subject is proven, but proven *by the thing we are
  criticising*.
- **The one clean small-channel human breakout is a podcast clip** — Matt Beall, 5,680 subs, 72,572
  views on hidden megalithic walls. That is **fringe-leaning content**, not academic history. It
  establishes that a small human channel can take 70k+ views here; it does not establish that a
  rigorous treatment can.
- **The formula is already saturating.** "Scientists Used AI to Reexamine Easter Island" (43,600
  subs, published 28 Aug) took **10,693 views and a breakout score of 3.1.** The fifth and sixth
  entrants are dying. That cuts both ways: the wave is cresting, so ship — but also, the audience may
  be tiring of the frame.

⚠ **Absence discipline.** These are the results one outlier query returned. A subject not appearing
in this feed is not proof it has no audience, and this feed does not tell us how a *rigorous* history
treatment would perform, because nobody has published one in the window.

## 3. VERDICT — build it, under three packaging conditions

**Demand is real, large, rising, and reachable by a channel this size.** The history-first video is
approved by the evidence. But the evidence also says something specific about how it must be
packaged, and it is the constraint the channel already knows is binding.

**Condition 1 — do not put "history" in the title.** <750 searches a month. The door is *statues*,
*documentary*, and the *AI* frame. The room behind the door is the history.

**Condition 2 — the title must name someone doing something with a stake.** CHANNEL.md's untested
hypothesis convicts exactly the kind of title this video invites: provenance and method titles did
0.46% (Manhattan), 2.48% (Sol Invictus), 3.65% (Flat Earth), while "JD Vance Claims Christians Found
Child Sacrifice" — a provenance video packaged as a live conflict — took 9.41%. **"How AI Rewrote
Easter Island" is a method title. It is the losing pattern.** ⚠ The hypothesis is untested and is not
a rule; it is a reason to prefer an actor-and-stake title, not a scoring system.

**Condition 3 — the thumbnail carries recognition, the title carries curiosity.** A moai is one of
the most recognisable objects on earth, which is exactly what the channel's own breakout evidence
says a small channel needs: a stranger gets the subject in under a second with no title visible.

**Timing:** ship while the wave is up. The demand is borrowed from the slop videos and the sixth
entrant is already returning 10k views.
