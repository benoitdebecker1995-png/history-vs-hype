## 🛒 PLEASE SOURCE — ranked

### 1. Hunt & Lipo, "The walking moai hypothesis," *Journal of Archaeological Science* (2025)
ScienceDirect `S0305440325002328` · paywalled, per-article purchase
**This is the knockout source.** Specimen B's flagship claim is that the roads are *too narrow* to have
moved statues. This is the paper where they walked a 4.35-tonne replica 100 m in 40 minutes with 18
people along that road design. We cannot put the central contradiction on screen off a press release —
we need the paper's own words on road geometry.

### 2. Jared Diamond, *Collapse: How Societies Choose to Fail or Succeed* (Viking, 2005) — the Rapa Nui chapter
**The source of the myth we're correcting.** Both specimens attack "the ecocide story"; we must quote
what Diamond *actually wrote*, not the internet's version of it. Steelman-first requires his real
words. A used copy is fine — we need one chapter.

### 3. Lipo et al., pukao study, *Journal of Archaeological Science* (2018)
Paywalled. Photogrammetry / 3D models of the pukao; rolling from Puna Pau and **parbuckling** up ramps.
**This is what refutes B's "acoustic notation" claim positively** — there *is* pukao research, by Lipo,
and it's about transport mechanics. Far stronger than arguing from absence.

### 4. Hunt & Lipo, *The Statues That Walked: Unraveling the Mystery of Easter Island* (Free Press, 2011)
The revisionist case in its authors' own voice. Gives us the argument's shape and its caveats, and
protects us from overstating it. Trade book, cheap used.

### 5. William Liller — Rapa Nui archaeoastronomy
⚠ Exact title to confirm; likely *The Ancient Solar Observatories of Rapanui* (Easter Island
Foundation) plus papers in *Rapa Nui Journal* / *Archaeoastronomy*. He remeasured Mulloy's notes across
23 shore ahu.
**Only needed if we go hard at B's 0.4° astronomy claims.** Liller is the real archaeoastronomy of the
island; having him lets us say "here is what the actual alignment research found" instead of "I
couldn't find it." Buy if cheap; skip if it turns into a hunt.

### 6. ⏳ OPTIONAL — a Rapa Nui scholarly overview for historiographical baseline

reading the whole island through the Hunt–Lipo camp alone. Flag: the revisionist case is now
