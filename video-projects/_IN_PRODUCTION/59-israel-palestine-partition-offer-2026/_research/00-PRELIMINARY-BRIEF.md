## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**What is this topic?**  
On 29 November 1947, the UN General Assembly voted 33–13 (10 abstain, 1 absent) to adopt Resolution 181(II), a non-binding recommendation to partition Palestine into two independent states (Jewish and Arab) plus an internationally administered Jerusalem. The plan was never implemented.

**Why does it matter?**  
It is the foundational document invoked by both Israelis ("Arabs rejected a fair deal") and Palestinians ("colonial partition imposed on us") to justify their modern territorial and legal claims. The claim-pair saturates 2024–2026 comment sections and conflict discourse.

**What's the standard narrative?**  
Almost every explainer says one of: (a) the Jews "reluctantly accepted" and Arabs "rejected" a fair offer, or (b) partition was Western imperialism that ignored Palestinian rights and the Arab majority. Both narratives treat the resolution as a settled script rather than a document.

**What angle could this channel take that others miss?**  
Read the actual allocation map, population figures, economic-union clauses, and implementation mechanism ON SCREEN. Adjudicate both "fair offer" and "theft imposed" against what Res 181 *actually said and required* — not what each side claims it said. The channel's edge: **auditor reading the primary document**, not a geopolitics narrator.

**Scope note:** This is a mechanism audit (territory, viability, economics, governance), not a moral philosophy or full conflict history. The video is **8–10 min pilot** testing whether "show the document" works for this audience.

## STANDARD NARRATIVE (What competitors will say)

Every major YouTube explainer on this topic deploys one of these two frames:

1. **"Generous offer rejected"** (pro-Israel frame):
   - Jews accepted the partition plan; Arabs rejected it outright
   - Arabs had a chance for a state and chose war instead
   - This proves Arab intransigence / unwillingness to coexist
   - Used as: "See, the Arabs were never willing to negotiate; they wanted it all"

2. **"Partition was imposed colonialism"** (pro-Palestine frame):
   - UN gave away Palestinian land to a Jewish minority
   - Plan ignored Palestinian rights; Arabs were never genuinely asked
   - Western powers forced partition against Arab majority will
   - Used as: "See, Palestinians were colonized by the UN and the West"

**Both narratives treat Res 181 as a settled, obvious fact** — either obviously fair or obviously unjust — **without reading the actual document.** The script's job is to break that frame.
