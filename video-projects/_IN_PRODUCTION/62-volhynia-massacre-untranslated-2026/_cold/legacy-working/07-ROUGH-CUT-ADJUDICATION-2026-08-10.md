## Evidence-status rules used here

- **Primary-grounded:** the exact item is inspectable and supports the stated proposition.
- **Primary via testimony:** a real primary record exists, but it records testimony, often under custody or coercion; it proves that the statement was recorded, not automatically that the statement was true.
- **Secondary-grounded:** the claim is supported by raw-read scholarship, with exact page or passage.
- **Secondary-only:** historians reproduce or describe the item, but the underlying object has not been independently inspected.
- **Contested interpretation:** the underlying facts may be established while classification, intent, scope, or attribution remains debated.
- **Unsupported / contradicted:** the spoken wording goes beyond the best available evidence or conflicts with a current primary record.

## The documentary case after the Klymchak quotation is removed

The remaining record is sufficient for the video’s core conclusion. The defensible evidence ladder is:

1. **Settled event pattern:** the July 1943 attacks were geographically broad, temporally coordinated, heavily civilian-targeted, and followed by continued operations.
2. **Organizational mechanism:** police desertions supplied trained armed manpower; UPA structures supplied command, logistics, security enforcement, and political direction.
3. **Ideological preparation:** nationalist writings framed Poles as a territorial obstacle before the peak killings.
4. **Command proximity:** Stelmashchuk’s Soviet testimony attributes an oral directive to Klyachkivsky. Its coercive setting lowers its evidentiary weight but does not make it nonexistent.
5. **Administrative capacity:** an authentic signed Klyachkivsky order survives for logistics/defence. It is not a kill order, but it shows that the regional command generated signed paperwork.
6. **Scholarly judgment independent of the disputed order:** McBride explicitly concludes that, regardless of debates over the order’s date and provenance, the evidence demonstrates a clear OUN-UPA policy of ethnic cleansing in Volhynia.
7. **Open limit:** no authenticated signed extermination order has been independently inspected in the materials assembled for this project.

That is a stronger documentary argument than pretending one sensational quotation has clean custody. It demonstrates the mechanism, states the limit, and prevents a source-provenance dispute from swallowing the massacre itself.
