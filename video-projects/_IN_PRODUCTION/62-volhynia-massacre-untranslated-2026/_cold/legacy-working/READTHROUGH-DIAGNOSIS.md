# Read-through diagnosis — V1 new spine

## Scope and authority

This diagnosis uses the complete creator read-through of 17 August 2026 as voice and reasoning evidence. Spoken dates, names, paraphrases, and uncertain factual claims are not treated as historical corrections. The transcript's false starts, profanity, repetition, and recognition errors are diagnostic evidence, not prose to copy.

## Overall finding

V1 followed the approved macro-spine, but several paragraphs still behaved like compressed research notes. They supplied facts before establishing the viewer's question, used abstract shorthand after the concrete referent had faded, or introduced a source through its bibliographic identity rather than through its job in the investigation.

The read-through supports one central correction: referential and causal clarity are part of Benoit's voice. He sounds natural when each fact appears because the previous fact has made him need it. He stops when a sentence is locally grammatical but the viewer has not been given a reason to ask for it.
