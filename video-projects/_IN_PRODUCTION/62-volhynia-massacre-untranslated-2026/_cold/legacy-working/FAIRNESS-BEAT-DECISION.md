# Fairness-beat decision

## Options

### A. Polish-specific institutional dispute

Best fit. The 2024 Kuraś dispute shows Polish institutions disagreeing about whether crimes against civilians should cancel official honour for an independence fighter. It directly repairs the structural imbalance and can be explained briefly.

### B. Belgium and Leopold II

Valid as a general national-memory analogy but less relevant to a Poland–Ukraine dispute.

### C. No example

Possible, but merely asserting that every national memory is selective would leave the Polish side of the comparison undemonstrated.

## Decision

Use option A in no more than 15–25 seconds. Explicitly state that the cases are not equivalent. The comparison is between competing rules for commemoration, not between the nature or scale of crimes.

Place it after explaining why joint mourning does not answer Poland's objection and before the Russia/final synthesis.
