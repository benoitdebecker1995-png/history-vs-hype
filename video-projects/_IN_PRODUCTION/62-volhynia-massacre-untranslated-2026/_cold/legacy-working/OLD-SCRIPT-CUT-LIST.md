This list classifies material from `SCRIPT.md` and the failed `SCRIPT-REBUILT.md` according to whether it serves the new thought order. “Cut” does not mean historically unimportant. It means the material does not deserve narration time in this video. “Production notes” means it may remain available for fact-checking, edit decisions, description links, or a contingency answer, but should not shape the spoken architecture.
