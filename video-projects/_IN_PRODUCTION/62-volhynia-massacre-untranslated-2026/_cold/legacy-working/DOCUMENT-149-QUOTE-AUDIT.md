# Document 149 — quotation audit

## Exact Ukrainian

> Акція нищення поляків не принесла очікуваних наслідків.

## Best cautious translation

> “The action of destroying the Poles did not bring the expected results.”

Possible nominal alternative: “The action of destruction of the Poles did not bring the expected results.” Do not silently strengthen this to “extermination” without a specific Ukrainian-language review.

## Immediate context

The following sentence says that the “active Polish element” had largely survived. The report says this element was using German-Bolshevik occupation conditions to take revenge on Ukrainians and preparing for a larger independent action. The author then proposes revising “our attitude toward the Poles” and attempting negotiations with Polish leaders to detach them from Germans and Bolsheviks.

## Direct inference

- The author calls what happened an anti-Polish “action.”
- The action had expected results against which it was evaluated.
- In the surrounding paragraph, failure is connected to the survival of the active Polish element.

## The quotation cannot establish by itself

- central authorization or the first initiating authority;
- an instruction to kill every Pole;
- whether the intended end-state was killing, forced flight, territorial removal, neutralization of armed or “active” Poles, or a combination;
- Bandera's personal role.

Use the wider source base—not this sentence alone—for the campaign's territorial purpose.
