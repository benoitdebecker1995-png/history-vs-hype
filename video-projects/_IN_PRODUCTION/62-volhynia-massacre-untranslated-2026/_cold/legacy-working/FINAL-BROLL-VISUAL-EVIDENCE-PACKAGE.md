## 5. Document card plan

### Consistent card system

Use the document-first visual language already established under `assets/youtube-native/`, not the older presentation cards. Keep the host's lower-third safe area clear at bottom-left. For every major document:

1. **Entry shot:** full page or official webpage, large enough to recognize it as a real item; 2.5–4 seconds.
2. **Identity strip:** upper-left or upper-right, never bottom-left: document title; producer; date.
3. **Provenance strip:** one line: archive/publication plus page/folio. Keep it visible through the crop.
4. **Crop:** one clean keyframed move, no simulated handheld movement.
5. **Highlight:** one sentence at a time with a translucent box/underline; preserve surrounding grammar.
6. **Translation:** original on left/page; checked English in a separate right-side panel or lower-right box. Label `PUBLISHED TRANSLATION` or `EDITOR'S TRANSLATION` accurately.
7. **Maximum text:** one quotation of roughly 12–24 words at normal YouTube reading size. Longer clauses require two sequential highlights, not smaller type.
