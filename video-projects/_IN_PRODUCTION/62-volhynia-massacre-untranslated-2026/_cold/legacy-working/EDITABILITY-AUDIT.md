## Editing risks

1. **Foreign-language page overload.** Never show more than one document question at a time. Begin with source identity, then highlight one passage, then state its limitation.
2. **Middle-act sameness.** Alternate full-screen document, talking head and map/registry. Do not stack four beige pages consecutively.
3. **Tiny text.** All source pages need a wide establishing view followed by a legible crop and short translated side-card. Translation must be labelled.
4. **Timeline abstraction.** Keep the memory section to five dates. No animated network or ten-stage chronology.
5. **Rights/provenance.** Prefer official pages, project-held scholarly excerpts used for commentary, and verified reproductions. Maintain the source lower-third already established by the asset pack.
