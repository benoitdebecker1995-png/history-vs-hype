# Document 149 — context

## Status: VERIFIED

The primary edition is *Litopys UPA*, New Series, volume 2, *Volyn and Polissia: UPA and its Rear Line, 1943–1944* (Kyiv–Toronto, 1999).

The edition places Zahrava under UPA-North and describes it as an operational-territorial group or military region operating in northern Rivne oblast and adjacent districts of Belarus.

Document 149 is an internal report by the Zahrava military region's Security Service section head, who signed with the pseudonym “Tretiy.” It covers Security Service work from 15 September to 15 October 1943. The document bears an erroneous 3 September date; the editors consider 30 October the probable date. Narration should use the reporting period, not litigate the dating problem.

In this report the Security Service handles intelligence networks, investigations, identification of enemies and punishments, including death sentences. It also complains that district Security Service punishment decisions were often not effectively controlled from above.

## Spoken introduction ceiling

> This is an internal report from the Security Service of Zahrava, one of UPA-North's military regions. It covers the section's work from mid-September to mid-October 1943 and is signed by the official using the pseudonym “Tretiy.”

If a further definition is needed:

> The Security Service was the movement's internal security apparatus. It handled intelligence, investigations and punishments.
