**Anglophone consensus ("ethnic cleansing"):**
- **McBride**, *Peasants into Perpetrators: The OUN-UPA and the Ethnic Cleansing of Volhynia, 1943–1944* — *Slavic Review* (2016). **Spine source.**
- **Snyder**, *The Reconstruction of Nations* (Yale UP, 2003) · *Bloodlands* (2010/2017) · working paper *"To Resolve
  the Ukrainian Question Once and For All": The Ethnic Cleansing of Ukrainians in Poland, 1943–1947* (MIT-Mellon WP #9,
  2001) — the reciprocal-cleansing / Operation Vistula cycle (ACT 5).
- **Rossoliński-Liebe**, *Stepan Bandera: The Life and Afterlife of a Ukrainian Nationalist* (ibidem/Columbia UP, 2014).
- **Himka**, *Ukrainian Nationalists and the Holocaust* (ibidem, 2021).
- **Plokhy**, *The Gates of Europe: A History of Ukraine* (Basic Books, 2015) — anchor context.

**Polish / genocide camp (affirmative defenders):**

- **Piotrowski**, *Poland's Holocaust* (McFarland, 1998).

- **IPN** collections: *Zbrodnia wołyńska* t. 1 · *Wołyń 1943* (`Wolyn_foldermini`).
