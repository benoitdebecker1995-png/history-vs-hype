## Confidence ladder

- Formal UPA participation: **very strong**
- Organizational reporting of anti-Polish actions: **very strong**
- Organized regional anti-Polish campaign: **strong**
- Local variation and imperfect control: **strong**
- Regional-command involvement: **strong**
- Exact regional-versus-central initiation: **unresolved**
- Bandera's operational responsibility while detained: **not established**

## Narrator-safe ceiling

Several contemporary records show anti-Polish actions being assigned, implemented, discussed and evaluated through OUN/UPA structures. This makes a purely spontaneous local-uprising explanation difficult to sustain. The evidence does not reconstruct the exact first decision or establish Bandera's personal direction.
