## CORRECTIONS MADE THIS PASS (applied to SCRIPT.md directly, tagged `[FACT-CHECK FIX]`)

### 1. CH2 — "in Stetsko's own hand" → "signed by Stetsko"
**Was:** "the text of this proclamation survives — in Stetsko's own hand, it pledges…"
**Problem:** Armstrong (p.83) describes the Act of Restoration as "an early typewritten copy," signed by Stetsko as "Head of the National Congress" — the body text was NOT handwritten.
**Fix applied:** "the text of this proclamation survives, signed by Stetsko — it pledges…" The on-screen "autograph" visual note stays accurate (the signature is genuine); only the VO's over-claim is corrected.

### 2. CH7 — SB-purge citation + precision (the most serious finding of this pass)
**Was:** "shot Ukrainians who refused to take part — thousands of them," carded to "RL p.255."
**Problem (mis-citation):** Raw full-text search of Rossoliński-Liebe's actual PDF (637 pages, extracted this session) found **no occurrence** of "4,000," "Gestapo," or "NKVD" anywhere near a discussion of the SB purging Ukrainians. The figure exists — but in a **different book**: Piotrowski p.254 (citing Zynovii Knysh), not RL. Card corrected.
**Problem (conflation):** The script implied "thousands" specifically refused to kill Poles. Raw-verified sourcing shows two distinct populations: (a) the SB's broad purge of suspected "unreliable"/rival-faction Ukrainians — genuinely thousands (Piotrowski/Knysh ~4,000; Snyder "tens of thousands" p.166; RL/Motyka 889 executed of 938 investigated in just Jan–Apr 1945 in Volhynia alone); (b) Ukrainians specifically executed for refusing to kill Poles — documented at low hundreds (Siemaszko p.1079: "at least 846… murdered by the SB," including 146 who "did not accept the genocidal activity" and 167 who helped Poles).
**Fix applied:** "shot Ukrainians it considered unreliable — thousands of them, including men who refused to kill Poles." "Thousands" now modifies the correctly-sourced broader category; the refusal detail is an included documented case (concrete example available if wanted: Slavko Hulub, "hung and perforated with bayonets… for refusing to participate in the killing of Poles," Piotrowski p.254).
**Process lesson (logged in `SOURCE-GENEALOGY.md` addendum):** an NLM-synthesis answer citing a page number is not the same as having raw-read that page. This is the second time an unverified RL/Piotrowski citation nearly rode into the script this project (see Row 5c footnote-laundering catch, 2026-07-18). Both books are now fully extracted and raw-searchable in the session scratchpad; use that before citing either again.

## SIMPLIFICATION FLAGS (9-rule scan)

### 🟢 RECOMMENDED (not blocking)

**CH1, line 29 — "the powers that had won the war sat around a table and assigned the land [Volhynia AND Galicia]"**
- Rule 7 (oversimplified causation): this compresses TWO separate diplomatic processes into one image — Volhynia went to Poland via the bilateral Treaty of Riga (Poland–Soviet Russia/Ukraine SSR, 1921); Eastern Galicia's status was decided separately by the Allied Council of Ambassadors (1923). Soviet Ukraine was technically a co-signatory of Riga, though the West Ukrainian People's Republic (the actual claimant to Galicia) was not represented in either process.
- Not fixing: the compression doesn't misstate the outcome (Ukraine's nationalist movement excluded from both), and un-compressing it would cost ~20 words on a Tier-2 scene-setting beat for no change to the thesis. Flagged for awareness only.

**No other simplification-rule violations found.** The script does not use unqualified territorial claims (Rule 1), present-tense-for-past-position errors (Rule 2), unearned absolutist language (Rule 3 — every "never"/"no one" in the script is backed by the specific claim it makes), unsourced statistics (Rule 4 — every number carries a card citation), single-source-laundered citations presented as multi-source (Rule 9 — the one genuine single-citation-chain figure, the SB "4,000," is now correctly hedged as "according to"/single-sourced in the genealogy note, though the VO itself doesn't over-claim consensus).
