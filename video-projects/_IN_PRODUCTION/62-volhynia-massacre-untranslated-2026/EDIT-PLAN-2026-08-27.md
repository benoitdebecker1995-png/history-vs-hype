### The polling passage, 10:10–10:37

**What you said on camera** (from `SCRIPT-V6-FILMING-LOCK.md`, the version filmed):

> "In April 2022, the Rating Group asked… Eighty-one percent supported that. **Five months later**, the
> Kyiv International Institute of Sociology asked… **Forty-three percent** answered positively, eight
> percent negatively, and **thirty-seven percent** found it difficult to judge."

**What `RESEARCH.md` E16 — the file `SCRIPT.md` names as factual authority — says:**

> Rating Group, 27 April 2022: 81/10, n=1000 CATI. **KIIS 2025**, on a WWII-activity question: **37/8/50**.

| | Filmed narration | RESEARCH.md E16 |
|---|---|---|
| Rating Group | April 2022, 81% | 27 April 2022, 81/10 — agrees |
| KIIS date | "five months later" (Sept 2022) | **2025** |
| KIIS positive | **43%** | **37%** |
| KIIS negative | 8% | 8% — agrees |
| KIIS difficult | **37%** | **50%** |

**I cannot resolve which is right from this repository.** `RESEARCH.md` carries no URL, no fieldwork
dates, no sample size and no question wording for the KIIS poll — its only trace anywhere in the
project is that one line. It is genuinely possible KIIS polled this twice and both figures are real.

**But the beat is blocked either way**, because `PROJECT.md` already requires:

> Poll cards must show exact question wording, sample and fieldwork before any number is spoken, and
> must say the 2022 and 2025 questions are different — not a trend.

That rule says **2022 and 2025**. The filmed narration says *"five months later."* The project's own
exhibit rule and the recorded audio disagree about which years these polls are from.

**Three ways out, cheapest first:**

1. **Cut the KIIS sentence.** Keep the Rating Group 81%, keep the "these are different questions"
   reasoning that follows it. Costs about 12 seconds, needs no re-record, removes the risk entirely.
2. **Verify KIIS directly** (kiis.com.ua press releases), then keep whichever is true and build the
   card with wording, sample and fieldwork as the rule requires.
3. **Re-record the passage.** One pickup, same setup, about 25 seconds.

This is an exact number and an exact date, spoken on camera, on a politically live subject. It is
precisely the class of claim `AGENTS.md` says to escalate rather than wave through.
