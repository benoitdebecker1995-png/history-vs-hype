# Volhynia visual style — draft for human approval

This is a test, not a locked channel template. It responds to the creator references Lindybeige, Metatron, and Matt Easton.

## Working visual grammar

- The presenter remains the visual anchor. Aim for roughly 75–85% presenter screen time.
- Cut away only when the viewer needs to inspect evidence, understand geography, or distinguish competing claims.
- Let evidence remain legible long enough to read. Do not manufacture motion every few seconds.
- Use ordinary cuts and occasional short dissolves. No swooshes, fake film damage, parallax photo collages, or template-like kinetic typography.
- Source labels stay small and factual. One label at a time; no decorative panels.
- Historical documents remain visibly real documents. Crops and simple highlights are allowed; generated facsimiles are not.
- Maps solve a spatial problem. They do not exist merely to add visual variety.
- No generated historical imagery, reenactments, fake documents, or synthetic eyewitness scenes.
- Music is optional, not a default bed. Voice, room tone, and deliberate silence can carry the argument.
- Preserve human timing and personality. Do not cut every pause or hand movement simply to increase edit density.
