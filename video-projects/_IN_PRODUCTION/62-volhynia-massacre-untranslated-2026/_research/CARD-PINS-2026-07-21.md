**File:** `library/by-topic/general-history/UkrainaModerna-20-2013-Zaitsev-KolodzinskyiDoctrine.pdf` — a legitimate UTP-Publishing-watermarked journal PDF that (unlike the Motyka rip) **carries print page numbers directly in the extracted text** (confirmed: running headers alternate "Архів" / author name with the print page number inline, e.g. PDF idx 244 → "245", idx 265 → "266" — verified consistent across 10+ sampled pages; mapping is **print page = PDF index + 1**).

Zaitsev's article reproduces Kolodzinskyi's own manuscript chapter "«Націоналістичне повстання»" ("Nationalist Uprising," a chapter of his *Military Doctrine of Ukrainian Nationalists*) starting at print p.257 (PDF idx 256). The target passage is on:

### PRINT PAGE 266 (PDF idx 265)

Ukrainian verbatim, char-exact off the rendered text:
> «Тільки під час повстання буде нагода вимести буквально до останньої ноги польський елємент <із>* З.У.З. і в цей спосіб закінчити польські претенсії про польський характер цих земель. Польський елємент, що буде чинно ставити спротив, мусить улягти в боротьбі, а решту треба зтероризувати й примусити до втечі за Вислу.»

### English rendering — do NOT re-translate; use Himka's own published translation

`library/by-topic/general-history/UkrainianNationalistsAndTheHolocaust-Himka-2021-ibidem.pdf`, **print p.154** (confirmed via the book's own embedded running-header page numbers, e.g. "154 UKRAINIAN NATIONALISTS AND THE HOLOCAUST"), footnote 404:

It must purge Ukraine from the alien, hostile element and from what is not good in our own people. **Only during the insurrection will it be possible to sweep away literally to the last man the Polish element from the Western Ukrainian Lands and thus to end Polish pretensions with regard to the Polish character of these lands. The Polish element that actively resists must fall in battle, and the rest must be terrorized and forced to flee beyond the Vistula....**' The more of the hostile element that [...]"

### ⚠ CORRECTION — the ledger's "Himka p.389" pin is WRONG in our owned copy; it's p.154

Checked p.389 of this exact file directly: it is mid-chapter on **Jewish** testimonies from Ternopil oblast in early 1944 (Skala-Podilska, Korolivka, Tovste) — an entirely different topic, no Kolodzinskyi content at all. The "sweep away literally to the last man" passage is at **p.154**, not p.389. `01-VERIFIED-RESEARCH.md`'s round-trip note ("earlier cite 'pp.790–91' = e-book location; use p.389 on cards") appears to have mis-converted an e-reader location number to a print page for a *different* Himka edition/location than the one in this file — both numbers are wrong for THIS owned PDF. **Recommend correcting every card/ledger reference from "Himka p.389" to "Himka p.154 (fn.404)."** Also note: Himka's own footnote cites the Ukrainian original as "260–61" — that is NOT the Ukraina Moderna (2013) page number (266); it's almost certainly a citation to the manuscript's own internal typescript foliation or an earlier standalone printing (Toronto, 1957) of the same chapter, not the journal reprint. This does not affect the pin above — p.266 is independently confirmed by direct raw-read of the actual reproduced facsimile text — it just means Himka's own footnote number cannot be used to cross-check the Ukraina Moderna page.

### Predicate check — VO's "cleared of the Polish population" vs. the primary

The primary text says **"польський елємент"** ("the Polish element"), not "population," and it distinguishes two groups within that element: those who **"actively resist must fall in battle"** (violence) and the rest, who **"must be terrorized and forced to flee beyond the Vistula"** (expulsion). The VO's plain-English "cleared of the Polish population" is a fair, non-overshooting summary of that combined kill-resisters/expel-the-rest program — but **the ON-SCREEN CARD must show the primary's own words ("the Polish element"), not silently substitute "population."** If the card wants to show the full mechanism (not just "sweep away… to the last man"), the two follow-on sentences (fall in battle / forced to flee beyond the Vistula) are worth including — they're what keeps "cleared" honest rather than reading as blanket extermination.

## U7 — the Motyka number guard — PINNED (content); binding card rule below

### (a) RL p.269 fn.1297 — CONTENT CONFIRMED; exact page NOT independently verifiable from our file

`library/by-topic/general-history/StepanBanderaLifeAndAfterlife-RossolinskiLiebe-2014-ibidem.pdf` is a **Calibre-generated ebook conversion** (confirmed via PDF metadata: `creator: calibre 2.20.0`) with **no print page numbers anywhere** — footnotes appear as bracketed inline markers (`[1297]`), not page-anchored. So "p.269" cannot be mechanically verified from this file. **The content, however, is an exact match** for what the ledger claims. Raw-read, body text (PDF idx 239):

> "During the period 1943–1945, the OUN and UPA, together with other mobilized Ukrainians, killed a total of between 70,000 and 100,000 Poles. **Grzegorz Motyka, a specialist on this subject, estimated that the number might be closer to 100,000 than to 70,000.** In different circumstances, Poles killed between 10,000 and 20,000 Ukrainians..."

Footnote [1297] (PDF idx 596, endnotes):
> "In Volhynia, Ukrainians killed 50,000 to 60,000 Poles, and Poles killed 2,000 to 3,000 Ukrainians. In eastern Galicia, Ukrainians killed 20,000 to 25,000 Poles, and Poles killed 1,000 to 5,000. In the territories of today's Poland, Poles murdered 8,000 to 12,000 Ukrainians, and Ukrainians killed 6,000 to 8,000 Poles. **Cf. Motyka, *Ukraińska partyzantka*, 410–12.**"

Note: RL's source for Motyka's own regional-breakdown figures is a **different Motyka book** — *Ukraińska partyzantka 1942–1960* (2006) — which we do **not** own (only *Od rzezi wołyńskiej…* 2011 and *Wołyń '43* 2016 are in the library). Not blocking (see (b) below, which pins the same conclusion directly from an owned Motyka book in Motyka's own words), but flagged for completeness.

### (b) Motyka's own published figure — FOUND, raw-read, in the owned 2011 book (page pin same rip-limitation as U5)

`OdRzeziWolynskiejDoAkcjiWisla-Motyka-2011.pdf`, chapter "Chronologia i bilans ofiar" ("Chronology and Balance of Victims," TOC/rip pg.375–377, PDF idx 375):

> "W świetle przytoczonych liczb wydaje się, że straty polskie w wyniku akcji UPA wyniosły prawdopodobnie **około 100 tysięcy zabitych** (przy czym raczej nieco mniej, a nie więcej niż 100 tysięcy). Wszelkie podawane wyższe liczby – 150 czy wręcz 200 tysięcy ofiar – nie znajdują potwierdzenia w żadnych poważnych badaniach naukowych…"
> *("In light of the figures cited, it appears that Polish losses from UPA action probably amounted to around 100 thousand killed (though rather somewhat less, not more, than 100 thousand). Any higher figures cited — 150 or even 200 thousand victims — find no confirmation in any serious scholarly research…")*

This is Motyka's own stated conclusion, in his own book, in his own words — **directly consistent** with RL's paraphrase ("closer to 100,000 than to 70,000") and with an independent web-search hit describing "Grzegorz Motyka and Jan Kęsik" as both citing "approximately 100 thousand" Polish dead. **Exact print page not establishable** (same Scribd-rip problem as U5 — no page numbers in this file, and the print/rip page-count mismatch rules out a fixed offset). If a card needs a page cite, treat it as "Motyka 2011, in the book's closing 'Chronologia i bilans ofiar' chapter" pending a paginated copy (same acquisition route as U5).

### (c) Binding card rule — paste-ready for `SOURCES.md`

> **CARD RULE — 70K:20K attribution.** No on-screen card may present "seventy thousand" or the 70K:20K ratio as **Grzegorz Motyka's own number.** The ratio is **Snyder's** (*The Reconstruction of Nations*, p.204: "about seventy thousand Poles to perhaps twenty thousand Ukrainians"), built on Motyka's research but not Motyka's own stated conclusion. Motyka's own published estimate is **higher** — "probably around 100 thousand… rather somewhat less, not more, than 100 thousand" (*Od rzezi wołyńskiej do akcji "Wisła"*, 2011, "Chronologia i bilans ofiar") — independently consistent with Rossoliński-Liebe's characterization that Motyka thinks the true toll is "closer to 100,000 than to 70,000" (*Stepan Bandera*, 2014, fn.1297). **If a card names Motyka, it must either (i) carry no number in his mouth, or (ii) use his own ~100,000 figure with citation to his own book — never the Snyder-sourced 70,000.**
