## THE FORENSIC-CONTRAST FRAMING — ✅ CONFIRMED (with one precision guard)

The claim's framing holds, and the retrieved scan **strengthens** it:
- Klyachkivsky signed his real name/pseudonym ("Клим Савур") to a dated, countersigned, archivally-filed **defensive** order (No. 11), and to sibling logistics/mobilization orders (No. 5, No. 8, No. 28). **The administrative machinery is on paper and signed.**
- **No signed order to liquidate the Polish population is in this record** — the initiating anti-Polish directive routes only through **Stelmashchuk's NKVD testimony** ("the secret directive… about the complete physical destruction of the Polish population," McBride p.642) and Congress endorsement kept out of the written resolutions (Motyka). *They kept the kill-order deniable; the defensive/logistics orders they signed openly.*

### ⚠ PREDICATE-DRIFT LANDMINE (must be guarded on screen)
**Point 2 of Order No. 11 contains the word "liquidate"** — but about the **German / Soviet** enemy, NOT Poles:
> `Воїнами, самообороною, розвідкою заблокувати ворога у його опірних пунктах… Якщо можна — його зліквідувати. Це відноситься так до німців як і до большевицьких партизан.`
> (*"…block the enemy in his strongpoints… if possible, liquidate him. This applies to the Germans as well as to the Bolshevik partisans."*)

If the editor zooms/highlights **`зліквідувати`** ("liquidate") without its object, the shot will **falsely read as the massacre order** — the exact drift this exhibit is meant to disprove. **On-screen rule:** show the header + signature + the "defence against the Germans" subject; if the translation card renders point 2, keep the **full object** ("liquidate *him* — this applies to the Germans as well as the Bolshevik partisans"). Do **not** crop "liquidate."
