## ATTRIBUTION MISMATCHES
1. **PROVENANCE OVERSTATEMENT — "a *signed archival document*, not duress testimony" (FINDING 1) is too strong.** The report survives *inside a Soviet secret-police investigative case file* (spr. = *sprava*), and **no scholar in the corpus — including McBride — has examined the original folio** (restricted). The "not duress testimony" contrast is fair in KIND (it reads as a captured perpetrator report, not an answer extracted under interrogation — and that is its evidentiary strength over the Stel'mashchuk NKVD confession), but "signed archival document" claims an autopsy of an original we do not have. **On-screen framing:** *"a UPA after-action report, preserved in a Soviet security-service case file and reproduced by Polish and Ukrainian historians"* — NOT "a signed original report."
2. **SAME-FOLIO COMPRESSION (minor).** McBride cites the identical locator (spr.11315, t.1, ch.2, ark.16) at fn 63 (the 24 June 1943 Rudyi→Lebed directive), fn 64, and fn 102 (the late-Aug Klymchak report) — two documents of different dates at one folio reference. Consistent with a relayed/compressed citation drawn from others' work (reinforcing #1), not a precise folio autopsy. Not load-bearing for the quote, but do not present ark.16 as a single self-contained "the Klymchak order" page.
3. **TRANSLATION VARIANT — do NOT blend renderings.** A widely web-circulated English version reads *"On September 29, 1943, I carried out the action in the villages of Wola Ostrowiecka and Ostrivky. I have liquidated all Poles, starting from the youngest ones"* (Wikipedia-lineage). It differs from McBride in **wording AND date**. The corpus consensus dates the massacre to **30 Aug 1943** (McBride p.648; Piotrowski; Katchanovski "end of August"; Siemaszko) — the "September 29" rendering is anomalous and unreliable. **Use McBride's translation; keep the date as 30 Aug 1943; never card the punchier "youngest ones / Sept 29" version.** Because the Ukrainian original is restricted, exact original wording cannot be pinned — different translators of the reproduction diverge.
4. **Claim-wording itself: NO drift.** "UPA battalion commander Klymchak ('Lysyi')" ✓ (McBride p.642); "reported to his superiors" ✓ (exact); "After the Ostrivky / Wola Ostrowiecka massacre" ✓ ("Following the operation," 30 Aug 1943). The quote is char-exact. The claim's attribution (Klymchak issued/reported this) is what the source shows — not merely that the massacre happened.
