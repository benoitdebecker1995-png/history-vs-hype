## PART 2 — TRANSLATION DRAFTS

### ① ORDER No. 11 — "Клим Савур" (Klyachkivsky), 4 September 1943
**Source:** `_research/exhibits/klym-savur-order11/order11-avr7498-p1.png` (HDA SBU f.13 spr.376 vol.60 folio 200).
Typewritten Ukrainian. Draft below is my read of the scan — **🔴 the two landmine lines must be native-confirmed.**

**Title:** НАКАЗ Ч. 11 — **Order No. 11**
**To:** *Командирам, командантам і всім сільським самооборонам* — "To commanders, commandants, and all village
self-defence units."

**Preamble (context — the GERMANS):** 🟢
> The Germans are retreating on all fronts… They have now gone entirely over to plunder — robbing whatever and
> wherever they can. Lately they have hit our villages harder still, for plunder: in the collective farms they thresh
> in haste and take all the grain, cart it away, leave the peasants almost nothing… driving the Ukrainian population to
> total economic ruin… and now they have begun attacking the forest areas too.

**НАКАЗУЮ / I ORDER:**

- **§1 🟢** — Immediately fortify the edges of the larger forest tracts and all forest belts with barricades, impassable
  ditches and "wolf-pits"; block every passage into the forests; cut all roads leading in, at their edges; secure the
  blocked points with pickets and permanent guards; develop good reconnaissance at the forest edge with direct
  communication to the larger centres. However many times the enemy clears the barricades, that many times the roads
  must be barricaded again.

- **§2 ✅ VERIFIED at 7× (friend cross-check, 2026-07-11) · THE LANDMINE LINE** — *"Воїнами, самообороною, розвідкою заблокувати ворога у його опірних пунктах. Здержати
  його вихід в терени на грабеж. **Якщо можна — його зліквідувати. Це відноситься так до німців як і до большевицьких
  партизан.**"* (crop: `klym-savur-order11/verify-s2-liquidate.png`)
  > With soldiers, self-defence and reconnaissance, **block the enemy** in his strongpoints; hold back his sortie into
  > the countryside for plunder. **If possible — liquidate him. This applies to the Germans as well as to the Bolshevik
  > partisans.**
  - **⚠ NEVER crop "зліквідувати" / "liquidate" from its object.** The object is **the enemy = the Germans and the
    Bolshevik partisans** — stated in the very next sentence. Cropped alone it falsely reads as an order to kill Poles.
    On screen, the "liquidate" clause and "this applies to the Germans and the Bolshevik partisans" must appear together.

- **§3 🟢** — Immediately put fully into effect **Order No. 8, dated 30 August 1943.** *(confirms the sibling order the
  genealogy flagged — locatable at avr.org.ua the same way.)*

- **§4 🟢** — On receipt, change all current quartering sites (they are known to the enemy); pick new ones in deep
  secrecy and fortify them; build layered reconnaissance and small forward combat/self-defence posts for ~10-odd km
  around each camp.

- **§5 🟢** — Store all combat and food supplies in the safest places so they can't be plundered.

- **§6 ✅ VERIFIED at 7× (friend cross-check, 2026-07-11) · SECOND CROP-LANDMINE** (crop: `klym-savur-order11/verify-s6-houses.png`) — Get the peasants to hide grain, food, linen and clothing — anything the enemy could
  seize in a surprise raid — and clear the houses of valuables. *"**Хати можуть бути попалені.**"* → **"The houses may
  be burned."**
  - **⚠** This is about **their OWN houses** — scorched-earth / denying the enemy in a raid, immediately followed by
    "before every predatory enemy raid we will defend ourselves…". It is **not** an order to burn Polish villages.
    Cropped, "houses may be burned" is weaponizable the same way as §2. If shown, keep the self-defence context.

- **§7 🟢** — Every peasant should build a good dugout (землянка) besides his house, to live in over winter — "if the
  house burns down, the dugout will replace it"; prepare before winter, when building one is impossible.

**Close 🟢:** "…we must all defend ourselves against the predatory enemy raid, against our physical and material
extermination. Act very fast… the last stage of the struggle is the hardest, and it is coming…" — **Слава Україні /
Glory to Ukraine.**
**Signed:** *Chief of the military staff — [signed] Col. Honcharenko* · *Commander of the Ukrainian Insurgent Army —
[signed] Klym Savur.* Dated **4 September 1943.**

**🔎 THE "SURPRISE" (why this document is the spine):** the one signed, named order we can put on screen is a mundane
**anti-German village-defence order** — barricades, hidden grain, winter dugouts. His name is on the logistics; it is
**not** on any order to kill Poles. That contrast *is* the forensic point — and the two crop-landmines (§2 "liquidate",
§6 "houses may be burned") are exactly what a hostile clipper would lift out of context.
