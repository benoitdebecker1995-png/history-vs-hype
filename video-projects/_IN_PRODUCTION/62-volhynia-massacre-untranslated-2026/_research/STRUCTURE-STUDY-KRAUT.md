# Structure study — exemplars vs our script (2026-07-16)

## PART 2 (added later same day): vidIQ storytelling-coach analysis of the top English competitor ("Ukraine's Darkest Secret," A Day In History, 9uJvZZ1NT08 — vidiq_video_watch, 25cr)

**Their double hook works:** visceral micro-scene (child begging a killer he knew, in the first 30s) + relevancy pivot (today they're allies — how?). **Then the video dies:** minutes 2–14 = "the Wikipedia trap" — macro history recitation, zero characters after the opening boy, atrocity delivered as voiceover LISTS (numbing, not moving), blunt date-transitions, an ending that pays off intellectually but not emotionally "because the tragedy was never grounded in a specific human narrative."

**The coach's core law: MICRO/MACRO BREATHING.** Zoom out for politics, but continually zoom back in to ground level (a family in a barn, a neighbor turning on a neighbor) or emotional engagement flatlines. Borrow: visceral specificity early · relevancy pivot · active simple cartography for border shifts. Avoid: over-contextualizing (give only the context the current scene needs) · telling atrocity as lists · abandoning micro for macro.

**Scorecard of our v5.2 against this rubric:** relevancy pivot ✓ native (the 2026 medal scene) · Wikipedia trap ✓ avoided (1918 in ~90s vs their 12 min) · show-don't-tell ✓ (Poryck scene, the neighbors clause, Gaj barn, Werba — we independently added the exact two examples the coach names: "a family hiding in a barn, a specific neighbor turning on a neighbor") · ending ✓ (mechanism + mirror vs their flat recount) · cartography → production note for VISUAL-ASSETS (simple animated maps at 1918 carve-up, 1943 Volhynia strike map, postwar border shift) · micro/macro breathing: our only macro-only stretch is CH1 (1918 + doctrine — no human at ground level; candidate touch: Dontsov as a person, one clause) · the visceral-scene-first hook = open FORK vs our data-validated news-lead open (T4 consideration, creator's call).

## His skeleton (from full open/middle/close reads)

1. **Hook = pattern → one exception.** Two minutes of delightful examples (nomads "turned into what they conquered": Magyars→Hungary, Ottomans→"the new Romans"…) then "there is however ONE BIG EXCEPTION" → the subject. Curiosity trap, zero stated controversy.
2. **One continuous chronological causal story.** Medieval Rus → "Then came the Mongols" → Muscovy → tsars → … The thesis EMERGES from the story; it is never announced and defended.
3. **Contrast is the recurring engine.** Russia vs the rest of Europe (repeatedly), Russia vs China at the close — every mechanism made visible by a comparison, and the comparisons are where the "aha"/joy lives.
4. **Analysis is embodied.** Even pure mechanism beats are micro-stories of actors trying and failing ("the French monarchs drove the state into bankruptcy… the Stuarts ended with their expulsion"). Enumerations exist ("three main factors") but each factor is walked as a concrete story.
5. **Zero meta-narration.** Never "the story," "the sources show," "what we have/don't have." Total narrative confidence; sourcing lives off-VO.
6. Plain CTA + next-video tease at the end.

## Implication for #62 (v5 blueprint) — merge his skeleton with OUR moat
