# Visual Source Audit — Volhynia rough cut

Status: working draft. Recommendations below are limited to institutional/primary records or repositories with a stated public-domain/Creative Commons policy. No graphic massacre imagery is recommended.

## Headline finding

The cut can be built around document facsimiles, maps, and restrained memorial/context footage. The strongest evidence visuals are already acquired in `_research/genealogy/` and `_research/exhibits/`; they are safer than sourcing atrocity photographs of uncertain provenance. The only currently verified 2026 visual for the cold open is the Ukrainian presidential decree. Do not use random Wikimedia/YouTube massacre photographs, colorized portraits, exhumation images, or screenshots of uncited social posts.

## Safe context sources (useful, but not proof of a particular event)

1. **Institute of National Remembrance (IPN), “Neighbors’ Blood: Genocide in Volhynia and Galicia 1943–1945.”** Institutional exhibition page lists photographs/documents from Leon Popek, Tomaszów Lubelski Regional Museum, KARTA, National Digital Archive, and IPN Archive: <https://eng.ipn.gov.pl/en/digital-resources/exhibitions/4756,NEIGHBORS-BLOOD-GENOCIDE-IN-VOLHYNIA-AND-GALICIA-1943-1945.html>. Use as a lead to a specific item, not a blanket licence; avoid graphic panels.
2. **USHMM Collections.** Search interface is authoritative for catalogue metadata but individual images have item-level rights and warnings: <https://collections.ushmm.org/search/catalog>. Do not recommend an image until the exact record, creator/date, rights line, and content warning are captured.
3. **Verkhovna Rada legal records.** The English notice and official law text are stable, showable government records: <https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/News/107333.html>; <https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/314-viii?lang=en>.

## Explicit exclusions / not verified

- `photo-lipniki-1943.jpg`: content-check and provenance/rights incomplete; do not use until reviewed for graphic content and exact archive credit.
- `photo-bandera-colour.jpg`: colourized derivative; creator/licence not confirmed. Use the black-and-white portrait only with a PD-likely caveat, or replace with a rights-cleared institutional portrait.
- Generic “Volhynia massacre” images from Wikimedia, Reddit, blogs, or YouTube thumbnails: frequently miscaptioned (wrong place/date/event) and rights unclear.
- USHMM search-result thumbnails without an opened catalogue record: not archive-ready.
- Mass-grave/exhumation photographs from 2025–26 news: modern sensitive imagery, often editorially licensed and not evidence for the 1943 event narrated here.
- Any graphic body, burning-village, or weapon image: unnecessary to the mechanism argument and risks sensationalizing documented suffering.

## Pre-publish checks

- Confirm every local exhibit’s acquisition record, creator/date, and licence in `EXHIBITS-INDEX.md`; a file existing in the project is not proof of public-domain status.
- For Ukrainian/Russian/Polish documents, show the original page and run `/translate` + Check-A before locking a translated side-card.
- Keep evidence labels (“primary,” “reproduced copy,” “scholar’s estimate,” “testimony under Soviet interrogation”) on screen; do not let illustrative maps or portraits carry factual claims.
- Re-check the 2026 decree URL and Polish award-withdrawal source on upload week; current political documents can change or be superseded.
