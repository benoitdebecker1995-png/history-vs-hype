### Exhibit 1 — Makar, *Litopys UPA* Vol.5, p.15 (RENDERED)
- **Document:** Volodymyr Makar ("Vadym"), «Північно-західні українські землі — збройна самооборона українського народу». Underground essay; per the *Litopys UPA* editorial note (idx8/idx14) it is *"the earliest known underground sketch of the rise and struggle of the UPA in Volhynia and Polissia,"* written late-May/early-June 1943, printed in *Вісник Української інформативної служби* no.11; a compilation of reports of the OUN-SD political referentura and the UPA information-intelligence service, compiled by Makar, then working in the OUN-SD information/propaganda dept under M. Palidovych ("Karpatsky") and M. Prokop ("Volodymyr").
- **Citation:** *Litopys UPA*, main series, Vol. 5 (*Волинь і Полісся: німецька окупація*, кн. 3; Toronto 1984), **p. 15**.
- **Original-language verbatim (char-exact, off the rendered page — Check-A PENDING):**
  «…український народ на згаданих теренах ухопив за зброю, щоб чинно боронити своєї чести, свого життя та майна від **організованого наступу ворогів України: німців, москвинів-більшовиків та поляків.**»
  (working gloss, NOT verified: "…the Ukrainian people in those territories took up arms to actively defend their honour, life and property from the organized onslaught of the enemies of Ukraine: the Germans, the Muscovite-Bolsheviks and the Poles." — flagged for `/translate` + Check-A)

### Exhibit 2 — "Chas ne zhde," *Litopys UPA* Vol.1, pp.105–110 (RENDERED)

- **Citation:** *Litopys UPA*, main series, Vol. 1 (*Волинь і Полісся: німецька окупація*, кн. 1; Toronto 1978), **pp. 105–110** (title/section header «Військово-вишкільні, політичні й інформативні публікації», PDF idx 104–109).

## ATTRIBUTION MISMATCHES

1. **SOURCE-DRIFT (Exhibit 2 — flag, do not launder):** "Chas ne zhde" is a genuine OUN-UPA own-press document and is now showable, but its content names the post-German enemy as **Muscovite imperialism (Russia)**, not a resurrected Poland. If a card captions it as evidence of the "resurrected Poland / Poland is the final enemy" belief, that is source-drift. **Fix:** use "Chas ne zhde" only for the general preemptive-strike mindset ("build the army now; the occupier will collapse; another enemy comes — prepare"), with an accurate caption. Note Snyder himself cites it under "political aims" generally, and footnotes the specifically anti-Polish attitude to Motyka/Armstrong/*Eksterminacja* p.85, not to this article.
2. **On-point exhibit for the card = Makar p.15.** It carries the OUN-UPA's own grouping of Poles among "the enemies of Ukraine," which is the documentary anchor beneath Snyder's "resurrected Poland" synthesis. The "final enemy = *resurrected* Poland" framing itself remains Snyder's reading (Row 15, named-scholar T2) — p.15 shows Poles listed as one of the three *current* enemies, not the word "resurrected."
3. **Identity error corrected:** the ledger's "Vasyl' Makar / 'Do pochatkiv UPA'" is the wrong article/author-forename (see Ledger corrections above). Correct = Volodymyr Makar, «Північно-західні українські землі».
4. **Name note:** author of "Chas ne zhde" is given only as initials **"В.Д."** in the *Litopys UPA* edition — do not attribute it to a named person on screen.
