# Solo Asset-Production Plan — Volhynia (#62)

**Cut reference:** `rough cut.srt` (timecodes are offset +01:00:00; this guide reports real video time). **Runtime:** ~13:11. **Purpose:** a $0, evidence-led build plan for one creator. The existing `ON-SCREEN-CARD-MATCH-2026-07-24.md` remains the source-of-truth for document identity, provenance, and card wording.

## Tool reality and safety gate

**Available locally:** PowerPoint/Google Slides/Canva-style workflows, basic NLE keyframes, screen capture, local image files, and ordinary audio editing. Use these first. **Not confirmed installed:** Canva, Figma, Remotion, QGIS, Google Earth Studio, visualization plugin, browser-research connector, and image-generation plugin. They are recommended options only; do not block the edit on installing them. If a plugin is later installed, use it as an accelerator, never as a source of historical facts.

**Non-negotiable:** never fabricate an archival document, signature, casualty photograph, order, map border, or witness scene. Never generate a “realistic” massacre image. AI imagery is permitted only when visibly labeled **SYMBOLIC / RECONSTRUCTION / MODERN ATMOSPHERE** and must not resemble evidence. Do not show an authenticated signed massacre order: the project explicitly says none is available. Do not crop the word `зліквідувати` from Klym Savur Order No. 11; its object is German/Bolshevik enemies, not Poles. Do not caption the published 24 June 1943 letter as signed; it reports an oral directive. Stelmashchuk material requires a legible Soviet-interrogation/duress caption.

## Asset recipes by category

### Maps and animation

1. **Static orientation map (03:37–04:00):** Start from the project’s `map-volhynia-1943-44.png`; in PowerPoint add a muted legend, approximate border disclaimer, and 3–5 labels. Export PNG. If QGIS is installed, georeference a public-domain base and save the project alongside a flattened PNG; do not redraw borders from memory.
2. **11 July strike/coordination map (06:55–07:20 or 08:50 transition):** MapChart is sufficient for a static base; add village dots only from the verified card ledger. For an animated version, duplicate slides with 3–5 highlighted waves and export as PNG sequence, then keyframe opacity in the NLE. Google Earth Studio is optional and unnecessary unless a licensed, non-dramatic geography flyover is desired.
3. **Route/location diagram (Poryck):** Make a schematic, not a faux satellite reconstruction: road, church, settlement, arrow, date, and “diagrammatic—not to scale.” Use PowerPoint shapes. No generated terrain or invented troop paths.

### Timelines and data visuals

- **Timeline slide:** 1918 border settlement → 1941 Act → 1943 killings → 1945–50s Soviet fighting → 2015 law → 2026 decree. Make each date a separate card; use verified dates only. PowerPoint SmartArt or Figma (if installed) is enough.
- **70K:20K bars (07:48):** two horizontal bars, labels “about” and “perhaps,” Snyder citation under chart. Animate width from zero over 0.8 s; keep a visible source line.
- **Named-dead wall (07:20):** scrolling scan, not a fabricated aggregate chart. A count of “over 36,000 named” must retain its scope (“named in this registry”).

### Documents, quote cards, and lower thirds

Use one 1920×1080 PowerPoint master: off-white paper for scholarship; charcoal for modern politics; 48–60 px body text at 1080p; source/provenance strip always visible. Keep full document on screen and put translation/highlight in a separate overlay. Every card gets an ID matching `ON-SCREEN-CARD-MATCH` and `SOURCE-GENEALOGY.md`.

Lower thirds should identify **person + role + date**, not make claims: “Yaroslav Stetsko · OUN faction · 1941”; “Gregor Motyka · historian”; “Stelmashchuk · testimony under Soviet interrogation.” Avoid “genocide expert” or “UPA commander ordered…” unless the cited exhibit literally supports it.

### Archival-photo treatment

Use original scans in grayscale/neutral grade; preserve margins, seals, handwriting, and page numbers. Add a slow 3–5% push, never a “burn” effect. Public-domain stills from Wikimedia, Internet Archive, Gallica, or British Library need a source/credit slate. Do not colorize evidence, use AI upscaling that changes marks, or juxtapose unrelated atrocity photos as if they depict Volhynia.

### Title, thumbnail, and promotional assets

- **Title card (00:00 and 12:55):** “Zelensky Honored These WWII ‘Heroes’ — Poland Calls Them Nazis” (locked project title); subtitle “What the documents support.”
- **Thumbnail (16:9, mobile test):** use a real decree/header or neutral UPA portrait on one side and a Poland–Ukraine map seam on the other; 2–4 words max: **“HEROES? / MASSACRE?”** No generated corpses, Nazi uniforms, or fake document signatures. Keep a small “DOCUMENTS” label if legible.
- **Promo stills:** export 3 frames: Klymchak report, Order No.11 full scan, Snyder 70K:20K chart. Add “source card” watermark only if it does not obscure the document.

### Sound design

- 00:00–00:14: silence/low, non-melodic drone; let the quote land.
- 03:29 dead gap: tighten picture/audio, do not fill with a dramatic hit.
- 06:55–07:20: remove music or use near-silence under Poryck numbers.
- 09:21 Order No.11: quiet paper/room tone; no “horror” sting.
- 12:45–13:11: restrained lift into outro after “in the Congo.”

Record source-page rustle yourself if desired; do not synthesize gunfire, screams, or battlefield ambience as if archival. Normalize final mix to about −14 LUFS integrated with −1 dBTP true-peak limiter (editing guide measured the rough cut at −31.9 LUFS).
