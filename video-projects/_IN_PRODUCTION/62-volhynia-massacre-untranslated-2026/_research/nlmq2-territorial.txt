  "answer": "The sources explicitly state that the OUN-UPA carried out the ethnic cleansing of Poles in Volhynia to create \"accomplished facts\" on the ground, securing the territory for a future Ukrainian state and preventing a repetition of the post-WWI scenario where a Polish minority served as a pretext for Polish territorial claims.\n\n### Pre-empting Post-War Claims and Border Settlements\nScholars characterize the massacres as a strategic attempt to ensure that, by the time of a post-war peace conference, there would be no Polish population left to justify Polish rule.\n\n*   **Timothy Snyder:** \"OUN-Bandera leaders apparently believed that the Second World War, like the First World War, would end with the exhaustion of both Germany and Russia, and that **Ukraine’s final enemy would be a resurrected Poland**. ... On their view, Ukrainians had to form an army and strike during the war, before a revived Poland could once again, as after 1918, direct forces and settlers from central Poland.\" [9, p. 166-167]\n*   **Witold Szabłowski:** \"Nacjonaliści chcą uwolnić od Polaków Wołyń, a z czasem również Galicję, by **nikt nie miał wątpliwości, że ziemie te po wojnie powinny przypaść niepodległej Ukrainie**.\" (Nationalists want to free Volhynia from Poles... so that no one has any doubt that these lands should belong to an independent Ukraine after the war). [13, p. 1289]\n*   **Jared McBride:** \"With the front rapidly closing in on them in spring 1943, OUN-UPA leaders believed there was **no better or more practical time to solve the ‘Polish question’ once and for all**. Poles, they argued, had to be removed as potential fifth columnists for the coming occupation.\" [7, p. 637]\n\n### Avoiding a Repeat of 1918–1921\nThe OUN leadership was deeply influenced by the \"trauma\" of the failed struggle for independence after WWI and was determined to use more radical, \"accomplished\" methods this time.\n\n*   **Witold Szabłowski:** \"Po I wojnie światowej... Stosunkowo nieduża grupa żyjących tu Polaków wystarczyła władzom z Warszawy jako pretekst... by przyłączyć Ukrainę Zachodnią do Rzeczypospolitej. **OUN... nie chce, by taka sytuacja powtórzyła się po II wojnie światowej. Tym razem to oni mają działać metodą faktów dokonanych**.\" (After WWI... A relatively small group of Poles living here was enough for the Warsaw authorities as a pretext... to annex Western Ukraine to the Republic. OUN... does not want such a situation to repeat itself after WWII. This time they are to act by the method of accomplished facts). [13, p. 1290]\n*   **Timothy Snyder:** \"The OUN had been founded by veterans of the West Ukrainian–Polish war... Both generations knew that Poland could be expected to send its armies to Galicia and Volhynia as soon as it had the chance. ... **preemptive strikes against Poles envisioned by the OUN-Bandera in early 1943 were not military operations but ethnic cleansing**.\" [9, p. 166, 167]\n\n### The Doctrine of Ethnic Purity as a State-Building Requirement\nThe goal was not just political, but grounded in an ideology that saw a monoethnic space as the only stable foundation for a state.\n\n*   **Jared McBride:** \"the goal remained the same for Ukrainian nationalists: to **create a 'nationally pure space' in western Ukraine through cleansing policies**. ... whether Poles left on their own accord... or they died at the hands of the OUN-UPA.\" [7, p. 632]\n*   **Timothy Snyder:** \"The OUN (and thus the UPA) accepted a totalistic form of integral nationalism, according to which **Ukrainian freedom required ethnic homogeneity**. ... the Polish 'occupier' could be defeated only by the removal of Poles from Ukrainian lands.\" [9, p. 156, 167]\n*   **Grzegorz Rossoliński-Liebe:** Internal UPA documents specified that the state was to be **\"purified\" of non-Ukrainian inhabitants** and often ended with the greeting: \"*Zbroia na verkh—Smert’ poliakam*\" (Weapons ready—Death to Poles).
