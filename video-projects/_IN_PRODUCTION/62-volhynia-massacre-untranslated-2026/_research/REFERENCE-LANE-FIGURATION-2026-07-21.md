## 8. Honest limits

- **n is small by your own standard.** 12 videos, 6 channels, 30,810 words. Your repo treats n<30 as noise. These rates are *directional*, not measurements you should defend to anyone.
- **Category assignment is my judgment.** "Dead metaphor" vs "live metaphor" vs "personification" has fuzzy borders; another reader would move each cell by roughly ±20%. The *ordering* of the table is more robust than any individual number. The two zero rows (scale comparison, sinister adverb) are the most robust findings, because they're regex-checkable and absolute.
- **Auto-captions have no punctuation** for fig tree, Atun-Shei and ReligionForBreakfast. So sentence-boundary tells — colon-reveal density (your T8), period-stab vs em-dash, fragment counts — are **not measurable** on those three and I have not reported numbers for them. Historia Civilis, Premodernist and Stefan Milo have properly punctuated captions.
- **Word counts are transcript words**, which drop filler and occasionally mis-transcribe; treat them as ±5%.
- **Atun-Shei is a register outlier.** It's in the sample for the argumentative/source-quoting structure that matches yours; its comedy-sketch voice should not be read as lane licence for heat.
- **fig tree is a register outlier too**, despite you naming it — profanity, vlog asides, chatty self-interruption. Its *epistemics* match you (it says "I'm telling you what's been argued, I'm not arguing it"); its *delivery* is the least like yours of the six.
- **Survivorship, not causation.** Every channel here is 2–5× your size. Nothing in this study shows figuration rate *causes* performance. It shows what a viewer in this lane is habituated to hearing. Your own bottleneck is packaging, not prose.
- **Not measured:** retention, CTR, or whether any of this moves a number. This is a register study only.
