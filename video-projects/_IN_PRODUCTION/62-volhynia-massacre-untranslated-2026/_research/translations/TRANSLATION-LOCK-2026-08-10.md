# Translation Lock — Volhynia On-Screen Evidence

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Scope:** Ukrainian, Russian and Polish wording proposed for on-screen cards in video #62.  
**Editorial verdict:** **READY WITH THE GUARDS BELOW.** No unresolved translation issue blocks the edit.

## Method and status

This pass compared the legible source text or diplomatic transcription against an independent literal translation, checked grammar and referents in the surrounding clause, and used a published scholarly translation where one exists. It also checked whether the translated words support the narration's attributed claim.

This is an editorial AI-assisted translation audit, **not certification by a native human translator**. On screen, retain the original language and label our wording `editor's translation` unless the English is explicitly credited to a published translator or scholar.

## Attribution locks

1. A translated primary text establishes what that text says; it does not automatically establish that its claims are true.
2. Kolodzinskyi and the 1941 Act use published scholarly English and should credit the scholar/edition.
3. Stelmashchuk remains primary testimony created under Soviet interrogation. Translation accuracy does not remove the duress and custody problem.
4. The Klymchak and Lebed English cards are reproduced through McBride. Label them `REPRODUCED PRIMARY` or `SCHOLAR-PUBLISHED TRANSCRIPTION`, not `ORIGINAL SCAN`.
5. The Sejm clause should use the official Polish record as the visible source and our separately labelled English translation.

## Stop flags for the edit

- Stop if “liquidate” is cropped away from “Germans” and “Bolshevik partisans.”
- Stop if Litopys p.441 is labelled a kill order.
- Stop if the p.442 testimony is attributed to Klym Savur rather than “Oleh.”
- Stop if *Chas ne zhde* is used to prove that Poland was named as the next enemy.
- Stop if a reconstructed Cyrillic slogan or fake facsimile is created.
- Stop if any editor's translation is presented as an official or native-certified translation.
