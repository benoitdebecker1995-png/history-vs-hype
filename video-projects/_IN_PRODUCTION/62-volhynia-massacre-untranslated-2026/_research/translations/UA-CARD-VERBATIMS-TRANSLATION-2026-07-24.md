## ① KOLODZINSKYI — "Military Doctrine" / «Націоналістичне повстання», print p.266

- **Card:** #11 (CH2 doctrine beat). **Showable:** `_research/exhibits/kolodzinskyi-doctrine-um20-p266.png`.
- **Original artefact:** Mykhailo Kolodzinskyi, *Voienna doktryna ukraïns'kykh natsionalistiv*, chapter «Націоналістичне повстання», **late-1930s manuscript** (OUN archive, Kyiv — **undigitized**). Showable reproduction = *Ukraina Moderna* 20 (2013), Zaitsev's edition, **print p.266** (facsimile of the MS text).

**Original (UA), char-exact:**
> «Тільки під час повстання буде нагода вимести буквально до останньої ноги польський елємент із З.У.З. і в цей спосіб закінчити польські претенсії про польський характер цих земель. Польський елємент, що буде чинно ставити спротив, мусить улягти в боротьбі, а решту треба зтероризувати й примусити до втечі за Вислу.»

**Translation (clause-by-clause):**
> "Only during the uprising will there be the opportunity to sweep away, literally to the last man, the **Polish element** from the W.U.L. [Western Ukrainian Lands], and in this way to end Polish claims to the Polish character of these lands. The Polish element that actively puts up resistance must fall in the struggle, and the rest must be terrorized and forced to flee beyond the Vistula."

**Annotations (no-direct-equivalent terms):**
1. **«польський елємент» → "the Polish element"** — NOT "population." A collective, dehumanizing category term. ⚠ Commonly softened to "population" in loose renderings; the card must keep "the Polish element."
2. **«до останньої ноги» → "to the last man"** — idiom; literally "to the last leg/foot" («нога» = leg). The set phrase means "utterly, to the last one." Himka renders it "literally to the last man."
3. **«З.У.З.» → "W.U.L."** = *Західні Українські Землі* / Western Ukrainian Lands (author's abbreviation).
4. **«зтероризувати» → "to terrorize"** · **«примусити до втечі за Вислу» → "force to flee beyond the Vistula"** (i.e., expel westward into ethnic Poland).

**Cross-check (dual-pass):** ✅ no semantic discrepancy. Matches **Himka's own published English (print p.154, fn.404)** word-for-word — the grounded scholar rendering. This is the strongest possible cross-check (an independent professional translation agrees).

**Surprise / predicate flag — NOTABLE:**
- The narrative "OUN doctrine = remove the Polish **population**" is imprecise on two counts the primary makes explicit: (a) the word is **«елємент» / "element,"** not "population"; (b) it is a **two-track program** — resisters *"must fall in the struggle"* (killed) and the rest *"terrorized and forced to flee beyond the Vistula"* (expelled). The VO's "cleared of the Polish population" is a fair non-overshooting *summary*, but the **on-screen card must show the source's own words: "the Polish element,"** and ideally the kill-resisters/expel-the-rest split that keeps "cleared" honest rather than reading as blanket extermination. **Confirms `SOURCE-GENEALOGY.md` Row 6 predicate guard.**

**Check-A status:** 🟡 CONFIRM — meaning secure (Himka corroborates); native eyes sign off the card wording of «польський елємент» and «до останньої ноги».

## ② MAKAR — «Північно-західні українські землі», *Litopys UPA* Vol.5 p.15

- **Card:** #17 (CH4 "restored Poland" beat — the on-point own-press primary). **Showable:** `_research/genealogy/litopys-mainser-vol5-p15-makar-nwlands.png`.
- **Original artefact:** Volodymyr Makar ("Vadym"), underground essay in ***Вісник УІС* no.11**, late-May/early-June 1943 (**not digitized**). Showable reproduction = *Litopys UPA* main-series Vol.5 (Toronto 1984), p.15 (typeset reproduction).

**Original (UA), char-exact:**
> «…український народ на згаданих теренах ухопив за зброю, щоб чинно боронити своєї чести, свого життя та майна від організованого наступу ворогів України: німців, москвинів-більшовиків та поляків.»

**Translation:**
> "…the Ukrainian people in the aforementioned territories took up arms in order to actively defend their honour, their life and property against the organized onslaught of the **enemies of Ukraine: the Germans, the Muscovite-Bolsheviks, and the Poles**."

**Annotations:**
1. **«москвини-більшовики» → "Muscovite-Bolsheviks"** — «москвини» is a nationalist/pejorative ethnonym for Russians (lit. "Muscovites"); pairs the ethnic slur with the ideological label.
2. **«чинно боронити» → "actively defend"** — the framing is defensive ("took up arms to defend against an onslaught").
3. **«ухопив за зброю» → "took up / seized arms."**

**Cross-check (dual-pass):** ✅ no discrepancy.

**Surprise / attribution flag — NOTABLE:**
- The document lists Poles as **one of three *current* enemies** (Germans, Muscovite-Bolsheviks, Poles), in a **defensive** frame — it does **not** say "resurrected Poland" or "final enemy." The **"resurrected Poland as *final* enemy" framing is Snyder's synthesis** (Row 15, named-scholar T2), not this document's words. **Card caption must say what p.15 says** ("OUN-UPA grouped Poles among *the enemies of Ukraine*, Volhynia, May 1943"), and the "resurrected Poland" reading stays attributed to Snyder. Confirms the hunter's on-point verdict.

**Check-A status:** 🟡 CONFIRM — legible typeset; native eyes confirm the three-enemies clause + the «москвини» gloss.

## ③ "CHAS NE ZHDE" — «Час не жде», *Litopys UPA* Vol.1 p.109

- **Card:** ⚠ **OFF-POINT for #17** (see flag). **Showable:** `_research/genealogy/litopys-mainser-vol1-p109-chasnezhde-secondenemy.png` (+ `-p105-...-title.png`).
- **Original artefact:** «ЧАС НЕ ЖДЕ — Кілька завваг на тему збройної сили», by "В.Д.", in UPA organ ***Самостійність***, 22–29 Jan 1944 (**not digitized**). Reproduction = *Litopys UPA* main-series Vol.1 (Toronto 1978), pp.105–110.

**Original (UA), char-exact:**
> «Німеччина стоїть напередодні розвалу. Вона є силою, яка з українських земель відплине. Це правда. Але рівночасно правда, що за нею йде друга ворожа нам сила — московський імперіялізм. До оборони перед ним ми мусимо готовитися.»

**Translation:**
> "Germany stands on the eve of collapse. It is a force that will ebb away from the Ukrainian lands. That is true. But it is equally true that behind it comes **a second force hostile to us — Muscovite imperialism**. We must prepare to defend against it."

**Annotations:**
1. **«московський імперіялізм» → "Muscovite imperialism"** = Russian imperialism (the named post-German enemy).
2. **«напередодні розвалу» → "on the eve of collapse"** · **«відплине» → "will ebb / flow away."**

**Cross-check (dual-pass):** ✅ no discrepancy.

**Surprise / SOURCE-DRIFT flag — 🔴 MAJOR:**
- This **directly contradicts** the "resurrected Poland" narrative. The document's named "second hostile force" after Germany is **«московський імперіялізм» — MUSCOVITE IMPERIALISM (Russia), NOT Poland.** A scan of pp.105–110 found no «поляк/Польща». **Captioning this as "resurrected Poland" evidence = a source-drift error on screen.** Use it **only** for the general preemptive-strike mindset ("build the army now; when the occupier collapses, the next enemy comes — prepare"), captioned accurately (the next enemy = Russia). **Hardens the hunter's SOURCE-DRIFT flag; card #17 uses Makar p.15, not this.**

**Check-A status:** 🟡 CONFIRM — verifies the «московський імперіялізм» reading (the whole point of the drift flag).
