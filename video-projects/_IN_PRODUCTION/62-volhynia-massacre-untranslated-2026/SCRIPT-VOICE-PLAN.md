## The build loop — per beat (needs your PC + mic)
1. **Claude hands you the beat as BULLETS** — what must land + the fixed quotes/dates + the on-screen doc + the question
   it opens. No candidate lines.
2. **You talk it** — voice memo, rough, rambling, hedge, restart, whatever. Say your *reaction* too — what *you* find
   damning or interesting. (That reaction is where "unique" comes from.) Or type it messy.
3. **You paste the transcript / type it.**
4. **Claude subtracts ONLY** — cut repetition, fix a genuine error, tighten, mark where the document lands. Never adds a
   sentence. If a line drifts un-like you, Claude *flags it and hands it back* — it does not "fix" it into prose.
5. **Flow check** — Claude stitches the beat to the one before and after (no cold pronouns, no dead seams — your standing rule).
6. **You read it aloud.** If it snags, adjust. Read-aloud is the lock gate.

- Document-led: the verdict rides the paper on screen, not the narration (your moat).
- The diagnostic catches generic-AI / other-channel tropes before they ship.

## AD-LIB FINGERPRINT — the sharpened diagnostic (mined 2026-07-13 from 8 scripts vs their delivered SRTs)
Real signal from diffing what was written vs what you actually said on camera. Use as the tell-detector when cutting.
- **You cut, you don't pad.** Delivered runs well under the script every time — your live instinct strips it down. → Claude
  writes LEAN and expects you to trim; never pad to hit a length.
- **Your live glue = "and" + "but"** (you chain conjunctively when talking) — not "so" (you actually *cut* "so" on camera).
- **You point: "here's" / "that's" / "here"** go UP live — deictic, conversational.
- **You talk TO "you"** — second-person address rises in delivery. Lean direct.
- **Light spoken texture you add, sparingly:** "kind of", "you know", "see", "well", "let me".
- 🚫 **SCRIPT-TELLS you STRIP on camera — so don't write them:** "**So…**" openers, "**Look,**", "**actually**", "**really**",
  "**right**". These are writer's residue; every one is a line you'd delete. (Confirms: writerly connectors + over-explain = the tells.)
- ⚠️ Limits: exact cut-% noisy (script files carry cue text); phrase-level n-grams surfaced topic words, not clean ad-libs.
