## ⚠ THE EDITORIAL-APPARATUS RULE

**Coleman's 1922 notes are NOT Grade A**, even though they sit inside a Grade A volume. The list of
datable tells (`synclitus`, `inluminator`, `censura`, `largitas`) is **Coleman's**, and Coleman says
so — C10e. The same applies to **Sigmund's** note in P3 (C0): it is strong **B-grade corroboration**
from the edition's editor, not the primary itself.

**Rule for the script:** inside a primary volume, the *text* is A and the *notes* are B.
