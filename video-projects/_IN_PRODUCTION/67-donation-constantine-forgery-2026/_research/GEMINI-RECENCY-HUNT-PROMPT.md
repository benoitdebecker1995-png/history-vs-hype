## THE QUESTIONS, IN PRIORITY ORDER

**1. If the answer is "still unresolved," say so plainly — that is the answer I expect and it is usable.

**2. The Byzantine/Eastern-origin thesis (model 6 above).** I have nothing on this and it may be a blind spot. What is the state of that argument? **3. John Whethamstede and his *Granarium*.** Hiatt's 2023 article is paywalled and I cannot reach it. Find me (a) any open-access or repository copy of it, (b) any other scholarship on Whethamstede's treatment of the Donation, and (c) **whether the *Granarium* manuscripts are digitised and viewable online** — British Library Cotton Nero C.VI is said to hold the first part, and Gonville & Caius (Cambridge) MS 230/116 reportedly contains excerpts. **Direct links to page images if they exist.**

**4. Reginald Pecock.** Levine 1973 is my only comparative study of Pecock versus Valla and it is over fifty years old. Is there newer work on Pecock's chapter against the Donation, or on his method?

**5. Did Nicholas of Cusa protect Valla?** Fried (p. 31) claims Cusa and Bessarion shielded Valla from the Inquisition but cites no source for it. Has anyone since documented this, or documented the Cusa–Valla relationship more generally? **6. New editions, translations, or computational work** on the *Constitutum Constantini* since Fuhrmann 1968 — including stylometry, authorship attribution, or newly identified manuscripts.

**7. Forgery detection as a discipline.** Anything recent that updates or challenges Grafton — history of diplomatics, the Mabillon–Papebroch controversy, or the origins of document authentication as a method.
