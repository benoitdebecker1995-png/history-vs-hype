## 5. THE GAP — what nobody is doing

Every treatment found, in every language, tells the story as **the exposure of a fraud**. The
document was fake; Valla caught it; the Church was embarrassed.

**Nobody tells it as a contest between two methods.** The research (`CUSA-VS-VALLA-priority-and-method.md`)
establishes from both primary texts that Cusa proved it false from the **registry** — absence in the
conciliar acts, the papal correspondence, the Fathers — and never examined the Latin, while Valla ran
those same arguments *and then* convicted the document out of its own sentences.

**That distinction is the video, and it is absent from the entire competitive field.**

Two supporting gaps, also unused anywhere found:
- ***Palea*** — the Donation's absence from the oldest copies of Gratian's *Decretum* and the
  marginal name meaning "straw beside grain." The Church's own canon lawyers flagged the text.
- **Otto III's chancery called it a forgery c. 1001** — which means the competing videos' implicit
  "nobody noticed for centuries" is false, and ours must not repeat it.

## 6. Our edge, stated plainly

Primary documents on screen, in the original, with locators — and in this case **the creator reads
the languages the primaries are in.** The *Constitutum* survives in a free MGH critical edition and
Valla's treatise in a free 1520 Latin print. Nobody in the field is showing either.
