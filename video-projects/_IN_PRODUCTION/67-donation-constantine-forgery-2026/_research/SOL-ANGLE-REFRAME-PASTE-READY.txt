## THE RESEARCH THAT EXISTS

Eleven months of archival work. Load-bearing findings, all sourced with page locators:

SURVIVAL / RECEPTION
- A man who argued FOR the Donation in 1436 absorbed the disproof, became Pope Pius II, and refounded papal temporal power on other grounds. The specialist's verbatim verdict: recognising it as a forgery "has no further consequences."
- In 1447 a Venetian humanist sent the Pope a Greek "older, independent witness" to the Donation — having silently deleted every single word the critic had convicted it on.
- In the 1530s-40s its most serious defender printed a Greek colophon showing his "ancient independent" copy was made in December 1206 for a Roman cardinal. He published the evidence against himself.
- A jurist argued the Donation was not a gift at all but a restitutio — Constantine handing back what Rome had wrongly taken — which settles the validity question in advance. A real legal argument, not a smear.
- The device underneath the defence: histories are silent, so the emperors suppressed them; Eusebius contradicts it, so his text was corrupted; the Latin is bad, because "a real forger would have written it more elegantly." The clumsiness of the forgery becomes proof of its authenticity.
- One defender had to hold the Donation valid because his own university's founding charter (itself forged) collapsed if Constantine had ceded the West.
- Nobody suppressed it. The Index listing is 1559, a century after the critic died, and the "forbidden book" story traces to his own publishers.
- Deployment history runs from a papal letter of 1053 to a defence printed in 1547.

METHOD / DETECTION
- The critic's proof included a numismatic argument: emperors mint coins, Constantine's gold survives in Latin, and no papal coinage from the period exists anywhere.
- Anachronistic vocabulary with exact line locators in the critical edition.
- A second critic, seven years earlier, used a different instrument: the popes needed Pepin and then Charlemagne to reconquer land they supposedly already owned.
- The critic then ran the method on himself and failed it once — he suppressed an inconvenient inscription. His greatest admirer is the scholar who says so.

ORIGIN
- Six live scholarly models for the forgery's date, spanning 750-850. The dating is contested; one prominent thesis is a strong minority, not consensus. An honest video cannot assert a date.
