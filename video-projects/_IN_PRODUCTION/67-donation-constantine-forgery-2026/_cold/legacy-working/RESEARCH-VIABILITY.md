## ✅ THE OPEN QUESTION — RESOLVED 2026-08-01

Full working: `_research/CUSA-VS-VALLA-priority-and-method.md`. Both primary texts read directly —
Cusa's Latin via the Cusanus-Portal, Valla via Coleman's 1922 facing Latin/English (now held at
`_research/sources/`).

**Cusa's method was different in kind.** *De concordantia catholica* III.2 §307 argues from the
**documentary record**: nothing in the conciliar acts, the papal correspondence or the Fathers; and a
later papal synodal decree restoring holdings to Otto I enumerates every place and never mentions
Constantine. He files the Donation with the Pseudo-Clementine decretals. **He never examines the
document's Latin.** Pecock (*Repressor* III.13, vol. 2 pp. 357–366) uses Cusa's method, not Valla's.

**Valla runs all of Cusa's arguments and then adds the one nobody had: he convicts the document out
of its own sentences.** Coleman's text breaks into six movements; §§1–4 are Cusa's ground, and §5
(pp. ~79–155, the long central movement) is Valla's own. **That is where the video lives.**

### 🔨 THE THESIS — Option A′

Not Valla-as-hero, and not "three people caught it at once." **The two methods are the subject:**

> **Cusa proved it false with the registry. Valla proved it false with the sentence. Only the second
> still works when the registry is gone.**

Mechanism-first, honest about Cusa, and it explains why Valla matters without claiming he was first.
It converts the Cusa problem into the cold open. **The packaging lock stands verbatim.**

## ⛔ THREE CLAIMS OVERTURNED — do not carry these forward

1. **"Fief" is fabricated.** Zero hits for `fief` / `feud*` / `vassal*` across Coleman's Latin **and**
   English. This came from the vidIQ-generated structure draft, and it confirms the zero-trust rule
   was right. **Never cite it.**
2. **The Gallicanus consulate proof is broken.** Coleman's note 500: Valla was attacking a **corrupt
   reading**; the best text does not say it. The date is impossible on other grounds anyway, but
   *Valla's* version of the proof does not stand. **Struck from the specificity-bomb list below.**
3. **"Nobody noticed for 700 years" is FALSE.** Otto III's chancery called it a forgery **c. 1001**
   (MGH DD O III n. 389). The script must not say or imply this, and the cold open must not lean on it.

## ⭐ THE EXHIBIT THIS FOUND — *palea*

Valla, Coleman pp. 75–77: the Donation is absent from the oldest copies of Gratian's *Decretum*, and
was added by a figure the manuscripts call **Palea** — *"because what he added of his own, compared
with Gratian, is as **straw [palea] beside grain**."*

**A physical, filmable object: the medieval Church's own canon lawyers flagging the text in the
margin, centuries before Valla.** It is the same species of argument as Cusa's, which is exactly why
it belongs — it shows Valla stacking the new method on top of the old one rather than replacing it.
