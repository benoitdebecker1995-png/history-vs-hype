## Promise

Valla showed that the Donation of Constantine failed tests of language, records and physical
evidence. The important story is what happened next: defenders changed what counted as proof,
rerouted the legal claim around the forged charter, or accepted the forgery while preserving the
power it had supported.

This is a video about how a claim survives disproof, not a standard “Valla exposed a medieval
forgery” biography.
