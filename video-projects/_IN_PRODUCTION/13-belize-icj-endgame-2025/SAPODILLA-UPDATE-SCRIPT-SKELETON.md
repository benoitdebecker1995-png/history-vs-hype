## WHAT'S BEING DECIDED (Background for You)

This is NOT a ruling on who owns the Sapodilla Cayes. It's a ruling on whether Guatemala can JOIN the case as a third party.

**Three countries, two simultaneous ICJ cases:**

| Case | Parties | Subject | Stage |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| No. 185 | Belize v. Honduras | Sovereignty over Sapodilla Cayes | Intervention ruling March 19 |
| No. 177 | Guatemala v. Belize | Guatemala's claim to 53% of Belize | Oral hearings Nov 2025, ruling ~2027 |

Guatemala applied to intervene in Case 185 (Dec 2023) because the Sapodilla Cayes are ALSO part of their broader claim against Belize in Case 177. If the ICJ rules on the Cayes without Guatemala present, it could prejudice their bigger case.

**Positions:**
- **Guatemala:** Wants in. Says its interests would be prejudiced by any bilateral ruling.
- **Belize:** Does NOT object. Actually wants both cases coordinated.
- **Honduras:** Strongly opposes. Calls it "redundant and an abuse of the institution of intervention."

**The legal wrinkle:** Guatemala may be an "indispensable party" (Monetary Gold doctrine) — meaning the Court CANNOT rule on the Cayes without Guatemala, because doing so would require ruling on Guatemala's rights without its consent.

**Four possible outcomes:**
1. **Intervention granted as party** — Guatemala joins fully. Three-way case. Unprecedented.
2. **Intervention granted as non-party** — Guatemala submits observations but isn't bound by ruling.
3. **Intervention denied** — Case proceeds Belize v. Honduras only.
4. **Case declared inadmissible** — Court says it can't proceed without Guatemala (Monetary Gold). Freezes everything.

## SECTION 1: WHAT ARE THE SAPODILLA CAYES? (0:50 – 1:50)

`[MAP: Zoom into Sapodilla Cayes — 14 islands at the southern tip of the Belize Barrier Reef]`

The Sapodilla Cayes are fourteen small islands — sand cays and mangrove islands — at the southern end of the Belize Barrier Reef. About 36 miles east of Punta Gorda, in Belize's Toledo District.

They sit inside the Sapodilla Cayes Marine Reserve — coral gardens, endangered sea turtles, manatees. But this isn't about conservation. It's about who controls the maritime zones around them. Fishing rights. Seabed resources. Where three countries' exclusive economic zones meet.

`[TALKING HEAD]`

For about 160 years, only Guatemala and Britain claimed these islands. Honduras made no formal claim. Then in 1982, Honduras wrote them into its constitution — Article 11 explicitly lists the "Cayos Zapotillos" as Honduran territory.

`[TEXT ON SCREEN: Honduras Constitution 1982, Article 11 — "Cayos Zapotillos"]`

That turned a two-way dispute into a three-way dispute. And in November 2022, Belize took Honduras to the ICJ to settle it.
