**[P4] Convention of London (14 July 1786)**
- Search: Full text of the 1786 Anglo-Spanish Convention
- Try: Avalon Project (Yale), Google Books, Archive.org
- **Why:** Defined British settlement rights in Belize. Did it address offshore islands? The southern boundary was the Sibun River — well north of the Sapodilla Cayes. This matters for establishing when/how British control extended south.
- **Extract:** Exact boundary language, any mention of islands or maritime rights

**[P5] 1859 Wyke-Aycinena Treaty (Full text)**
- Already in your existing NotebookLM from video #13

- **Extract:** All six articles + Article 7, any mention of "islands" (only river islands, not offshore)

**[P6] Honduras 1982 Constitution — Article 11**
- Source: https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Honduras_2013
- **Why:** Exact text listing "Cayos Zapotillos" as Honduran territory. The moment a two-way dispute became three-way.
- **Extract:** Full text of Article 11, surrounding articles on territorial definition, any UNCLOS-aligned provisions (12nm territorial sea, 200nm EEZ)

**[A1] Heather McKillop — Maya maritime trade in southern Belize**
- Book: *In Search of Maya Sea Traders* (Texas A&M University Press, 2005)

- **Why:** Shoman is the leading Belizean historian. Covers British Honduras colonial period, the 1859 treaty, and the territorial disputes. Will have context on British administration of offshore islands.
- **Extract:** Colonial administration of the Cayes, how British Honduras expanded south beyond the 1786 boundary

**[A3] UNCLOS III context — 1982 Law of the Sea**
