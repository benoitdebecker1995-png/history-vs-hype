## Flagged Issues

### 1. OVERSTATEMENT (Lines 72-73)
**Claim:** "Every maritime boundary dispute you hear about today — South China Sea, Arctic sovereignty, EEZ claims — traces back to this basic problem."
**Issue:** These disputes have distinct origins. The longitude/measurement problem is analogous but not causative.
**Recommendation:** Soften to: "The same basic problem — drawing a line and measuring where it is — still drives maritime boundary disputes today."
**Severity:** MEDIUM — could draw factual challenges in comments

### 2. HEDGE NEEDED (Line 1 / Claim #1)
**Claim:** "Every country speaks Spanish. Except one."
**Issue:** Suriname (Dutch), Guyana (English), French Guiana (French) also don't speak Spanish.
**Mitigation:** Script says "open a language map" — in context, viewers see the map. But the spoken claim "every country speaks Spanish" is technically wrong.
**Recommendation:** Consider "Almost every country speaks Spanish" or "The entire continent is Spanish-speaking — except for one massive exception."
**Severity:** LOW — visual context mitigates, but pedantic commenters will flag it
