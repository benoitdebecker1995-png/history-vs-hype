## Filming Notes

1. **Single setup:** Standard talking head, no set changes needed
2. **Delivery shifts:** Mark shots #14 (lean forward), #17 (deadpan), #24 (slight smile), #28 (slow), #30 (surprise setup)
3. **B-roll cutaway points are clean:** Every B-roll moment has a natural sentence break for editing
4. **Teleprompter:** ~1,060 words at 150 wpm. Budget 8-9 minutes total filming for pickups
5. **Energy arc:** Start neutral (Act 1-2) → build through reveals → firm/conclusive at close
