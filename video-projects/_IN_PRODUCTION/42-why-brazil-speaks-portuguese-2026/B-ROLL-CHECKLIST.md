# B-Roll Checklist — Why Does Brazil Speak Portuguese?

**Total assets needed:** 10 (6 maps, 2 text overlays, 1 map animation, 1 end screen)
**Tools:** Canva (free), MapChart.net, PowerPoint/Keynote, Wikimedia Commons, DaVinci Resolve

---

## CRITICAL PRIORITY (Must have before filming)

### Asset 1: Language Map of South America
**Timestamp:** 0:00-0:07 (Shot #1-2)
**Description:** South America with all Spanish-speaking countries in muted tan/sand, Brazil in deep green. Clean, no clutter.

**DIY Steps (MapChart.net + Canva):**
1. Go to MapChart.net → South America map
2. Color scheme:
   - Brazil: #006400 (deep green)
   - Spanish-speaking countries: #D4A574 (warm tan)
   - Guyana/Suriname/Fr. Guiana: #808080 (grey — not the focus)
   - Ocean: #1B2838 (dark navy)
3. Export as PNG (high-res)
4. Import to Canva → add country labels if needed
5. Export 1920x1080

**DaVinci Resolve animation:**
- Static map for 5s with slow 105% zoom
- At "Except one" — Brazil pulses (opacity flash 100→80→100 over 0.5s)

### Asset 2: Volta do Mar Arc
**Timestamp:** 1:40-1:55 (Shot #10)
**Description:** Atlantic Ocean with animated ship route showing the Volta do Mar. Ship icon traces: African coast → west into mid-Atlantic → catches westerlies → sweeping arc back to Lisbon.

**DIY Steps (Canva + DaVinci Resolve):**
1. Canva: Create 1920x1080 canvas
   - Background: Dark navy ocean (#1B2838)
   - Add simplified coastlines: Western Africa (left), Portugal/Lisbon (top-right), Brazil coast (far left, faintly visible)
   - Draw the arc: Use Canva's curved line tool
     - Start point: West African coast (~10°N)
     - Arc west to roughly 30°W longitude
     - Curve north catching westerlies (~35-40°N)
     - End at Lisbon
   - Add wind direction arrows:
     - Trade winds (NE→SW) at low latitudes
     - Westerlies (W→E) at high latitudes
   - Label: "Volta do Mar" along the arc
   - Label: "Trade Winds" and "Westerlies" with arrows
2. Export as PNG
3. DaVinci Resolve: Animate a dot/ship icon along the arc path over 10-15 seconds
   - Use keyframed position on a ship emoji or simple triangle
   - Dotted trail appears behind the ship as it moves

**Alternative (simpler):** Static map with the arc pre-drawn + arrow direction indicators. Pan slowly across in post.

### Asset 3: Inter Caetera → Tordesillas Line Animation
**Timestamp:** 2:05-2:15 (Shot #13)
**Description:** Map showing the Inter caetera line (100 leagues) shifting westward to the Tordesillas line (370 leagues). Brazil's eastern bulge appears on the Portuguese side.

**DIY Steps (PowerPoint/Keynote animation):**
1. Slide 1 background: Dark navy ocean + Atlantic coastlines (Africa right, Americas left)
2. Draw vertical line at 100 leagues (~38°W) — thin, white, labeled "Inter caetera (1493) — 100 leagues"
3. Add Cape Verde Islands as reference point
4. Animate: Line slides left to 370 leagues (~48.7°W) position
5. New label fades in: "Treaty of Tordesillas (1494) — 370 leagues"
6. Brazil's eastern bulge (Natal/Recife area) is now clearly EAST of the line
7. Color the east side green tint, west side tan tint
8. Export as video (MP4) from PowerPoint

**Key visual:** The moment the line shifts and Brazil's bulge appears on the Portuguese side is the payoff.

### Asset 4: Modern Brazil vs Tordesillas Line
**Timestamp:** 3:35-3:40 (Shot #22)
**Description:** Modern Brazil's full outline overlaid with the Tordesillas line. The vast majority of Brazil sits WEST of the line — stark contrast.

**DIY Steps (Canva):**
1. Start with MapChart South America map (Brazil highlighted)
2. Import to Canva
3. Draw vertical line at approximately 48.7°W (the Tordesillas line)
4. Label line: "Treaty of Tordesillas (1494)"
5. Color scheme:
   - East of line (original Portuguese claim): Deep green
   - West of line (expansion beyond treaty): Light green or green with diagonal stripes
6. Add text: "Original claim" (east) and "Expansion beyond treaty" (west)
7. The visual should make it immediately obvious that the original claim was tiny

**This is the "wait, that doesn't add up" moment.** Make the contrast stark.

### Asset 7: Bandeirante Expansion Routes
**Timestamp:** 4:05-4:20 (Shot #25)

**DIY Steps (Canva):**

2. Tordesillas line drawn (for reference)
