## Pacing Rules

- **Never more than 10 seconds** of talking head without a visual cut or lower-third
- **Primary source on screen:** minimum 5 seconds per quote (reading time)
- **Map transitions:** at 0:03, 1:48, 3:42, 5:54, 6:55, 10:38 — highest drop-risk moments
- **Beat gaps (4 total):** after treaty text (~2:35), after "paper reality" (~4:59), after "citizens of nowhere" (~10:13), after Odinkalu quote (~10:19). Hold 2-3 sec, no music
- **Cut on SRT pauses** — the gaps between subtitle blocks are your natural cut points
- **Audio continuity** — voice runs continuous under all B-roll. Never cut audio because you cut video
