## Four recurring deflections we must address in script

Cross-referenced across all three videos' top comments:

### Deflection 1 — "Arab / Islamic slavery was earlier, longer, and worse"

Frequency: Highest. Surfaces in Walsh comments [2], [5], [15], [17], [51], [56], [66], [67]. Also in Lee comments [5], [17], [34].

> *[Walsh, 3800 likes]* "Islam slavery started earlier and has never stopped"
> *[Walsh, 756 likes]* "My white ancestors were enslaved by the Muslims."
> *[Lee, 69 likes]* "The Arabs started the slave trade then the Europeans jumped in. Then after the pope told the europeans that Africans didn't have souls they really turned up"

**Counter we already have:** Atlantic peak intensity 65,000/year vs trans-Saharan 1,000/year per route. Atlantic moved more people in 366 years than all other routes combined in 1,250 years. **Plus** the Lee commenter accidentally provides our framing: "the pope told them" — that's *Romanus Pontifex*.

### Deflection 2 — "Thomas Sowell said everyone did it"

Frequency: High. Walsh comments [9], [11], [21], [26], [68]. Also recurs in Lee comments via "everyone enslaved everyone" framing.

> *[Walsh, 1400 likes]* "'History is what happened not what you think happened' — Thomas Sowell"
> *[Walsh, 595 likes]* "I'm already familiar with 95% of this information thanks to the great Thomas Sowell"

**Counter we have:** Sowell's universalizing claim is technically true for *the existence* of slavery and *technically false* for the *kind* (chattel/racial) and *scale* (industrial). The Smallwood "saltwater slavery" distinction + the Pieter de Marees Dutch trader admission addresses this directly. **Don't argue with Sowell** — concede his "everyone did it" then deploy the "but not like this" with documents.

### Deflection 3 — "Dahomey / Queen Nzinga / Woman King"

Frequency: Medium. Walsh [29], [30]. Lee [73].

> *[Walsh, 423 likes]* "Dahomey tribe was the basis for 'The Woman King' which tried to reverse the role of the Dahomey's"
> *[Lee, 2 likes]* "queen nzinga sold her ppl to gain the throne. The woman king is a feminist retelling of the dahomey tribe."

**Counter we have:** The Oba of Benin's 1530s ban on male slave exports + Allada's 1670 refusal of the French fort + Afonso I of Kongo 1526 letter. African kingdoms split — some sold, some refused, some begged Europe to stop. The Hollywood-Dahomey-flat-narrative is the same flattening Lee's top commenter calls out: *"White supremacists do not acknowledge the distinct cultural and tribal differences between African civilisations and kingdoms."* Use this exact framing.

### Deflection 4 — "Schools lied / I knew this for years from [Sowell / Walsh / homeschool]"

Frequency: Pervasive in Walsh comments. Establishes the audience's *credibility frame*: they value "real history" over "what schools teach."

> *[Walsh, 764 likes]* "I knew I had been lied but I didn't know I had been lied to this degree."
> *[Walsh, 2000 likes]* "I was homeschooled and knew all of this for years. Get your kids out of public schools."

**Implication for our framing:** Don't position as "the OPPOSITE of Walsh" — position as "the LEVEL ABOVE Walsh." The audience that found Walsh because they distrust schools is the *same audience* that should find us because Walsh stopped at the rhetorical layer and didn't show the documents. *"Walsh got the existence right. He missed the documents."*

## Script-structure implications

The four-beat document deployment locked in PROJECT-STATUS is structurally right but needs two additions:

### Addition 1 — Sowell-acknowledge beat (30-45 sec, early)

Before deploying Zurara, name-check Sowell's universal claim and *concede it*. This buys credibility with the Walsh audience instead of triggering their distrust filter. Then immediately pivot to the distinction Sowell doesn't make: existence ≠ scale ≠ chattel framework.

### Addition 2 — Trans-Saharan intensity beat (30 sec, mid-video)

Between Document Beat 2 and Beat 3, drop the intensity comparison: *"The trans-Saharan trade lasted 1,250 years and moved 6 million people. The Atlantic moved 12.5 million in 366. At peak, 65 times faster."* This pre-empts Deflection 1 before the comment section can.

These two additions push runtime from 8 min toward 9-10 min. Still within Format A/B + 12-min cap.
