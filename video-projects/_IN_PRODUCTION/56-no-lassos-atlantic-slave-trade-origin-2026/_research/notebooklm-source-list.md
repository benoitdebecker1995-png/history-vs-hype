## TIER 1 — Primary-document editions (mandatory; exhibits live here)

### 1. Zurara — Beazley & Prestage translation

Beazley, C. Raymond, and Edgar Prestage, trans. and eds. *The Chronicle of the Discovery and Conquest of Guinea by Gomes Eannes de Azurara*. 2 vols. Hakluyt Society, 1st series, nos. 95 and 100. London: Hakluyt Society, 1896-1899.

- **Status:** [ ]
- **Accessibility:** Public domain, Internet Archive — direct links (verified 2026-05-12):
  - Vol. 1 (Ch. I-XL + Prestage intro): https://archive.org/details/chronicleofdisco01zura — also https://archive.org/details/chroniclediscov01presgoog
  - Vol. 2 (Ch. XLI-XCVII + Beazley intro): https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.181648 — also https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/35764
- **Brings:** Zurara primary text in English translation; raid-period eyewitness; the buyer's-court chronicle
- **Used for:** primary exhibit candidate (front-runner for "no lassos" refutation)
- **Key passage reconnaissance (web search, NotebookLM-verify before script):**
  - 1441 Antam Gonçalvez raid at Cap Blanc (Mauritania) — kidnapped 2 Berbers, told crew the prize would be "the first captives before the presence of our Prince [Henry]" — buyer's-court framing baked into the source itself
  - 1442 Antam Gonçalvez return to Cabo Branco / Bay of Arguin
  - Lançarote's raid (1444) — Christian-conquest framing with battle cries "St. James! St. George! Portugal!"
  - 1444 Lagos (Portugal) — first major slave auction in Europe

### 2. Newitt — Portuguese in West Africa documentary history

Newitt, Malyn, ed. *The Portuguese in West Africa, 1415-1670: A Documentary History*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2010. ISBN 978-0521769358.

- **Status:** [ ]

### 3. Davenport — European Treaties (papal bulls)

Davenport, Frances Gardiner, ed. *European Treaties Bearing on the History of the United States and Its Dependencies to 1648*. Washington, DC: Carnegie Institution of Washington, 1917.

- **Status:** [ ]
- **Accessibility:** Public domain, Internet Archive — direct links (verified 2026-05-12):
