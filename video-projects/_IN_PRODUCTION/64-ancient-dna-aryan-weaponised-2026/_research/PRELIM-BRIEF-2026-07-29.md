## THE STEELMAN: What Each Side Gets Right (Before It Collapses)

### Hindutva Reading (Steelmanned)

**Correct premise:** The Rakhigarhi skeleton (2600 BCE) lacks steppe ancestry. This is a **fact proven by the Shinde et al. 2019 paper itself.** This fact does indeed prove that Indus Valley Civilization populations *at that time* were not genetically influenced by steppe pastoralists.

**Why it then fails:** 

The logical leap — "Rakhigarhi has no steppe DNA, therefore there was never Aryan migration" — commits a category error. Here's why:

1. **One skeleton ≠ one population.** Nick Patterson explicitly: "The Indus Valley Civilization itself was genetically extremely diverse."

2. **Absence in 2600 BCE ≠ absence in all times.** The very same papers (Narasimhan et al.) show steppe DNA is ubiquitous in modern South Asians and in later samples from 1200-800 BCE (Swat Valley).

3. **Timing direction matters.** If steppe ancestry arrived in 2600 BCE, we'd find it in Rakhigarhi. We don't. If it arrived after 2600 BCE, we *wouldn't* expect to find it in Rakhigarhi. We don't. The papers therefore support **later arrival**, not no arrival.

The Hindutva reading mistakes "not in 2600 BCE" for "never," but the data proves the opposite direction.

### Western Nationalist Reading (Steelmanned)

**Correct premise:** Steppe pastoralists (Yamnaya culture) did migrate into Europe and toward South Asia around 2000 BCE. Genetic evidence supports this historical reality.

**Why it then fails:**

The logical leap — "Yamnaya/steppe ancestry = Aryan racial purity = modern legitimacy" — commits three errors:

1. **Ancestry ≠ identity.** A modern individual with 20% steppe ancestry is not "Aryan." They are a mixed population. David Reich: "The mixture of highly differentiated populations is a recurrent process in our history."

2. **Archaeological culture ≠ ethnicity.** The Yamnaya were a Bronze Age pastoral people with a material culture. They did not self-identify as "Aryans" or possess racial ideology. Applying modern racial/ethnic categories to ancient archaeological groups reifies categories that didn't exist.

3. **Genetic descent ≠ political claim.** Even if modern Europeans *did* descend genetically from Yamnaya, that ancestry does not establish territorial rights, cultural supremacy, or political legitimacy in 2026.

The Western nationalist reading mistakes a historical fact (Yamnaya existed and migrated) for a political conclusion (therefore moderns can claim racial continuity and dominion).
