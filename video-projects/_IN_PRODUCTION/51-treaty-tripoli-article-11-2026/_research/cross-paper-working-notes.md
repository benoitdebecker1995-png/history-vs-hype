### S3 — SAME-EVIDENCE-MULTIPLE-READINGS upgrades from n=1 to n=3, with three sub-variants
- **Variant A — DIACHRONIC RECEPTION** (Crane): one document, era-by-era reading. Sequential structure.
- **Variant B — SYNCHRONIC INTERPRETIVE CONTEST** (Cronon): same evidence, contemporary historians, opposite conclusions. Pairs two specific scholars/books. > "it remains possible to narrate the same evidence in radically different ways"
- **Variant C — INSTRUMENTAL APPROPRIATION** (Trouillot): same event, every group uses it as pretext for *their* argument. > "apologists and detractors alike, abolitionists and avowed racists, liberal intellectuals, economists, and slave owners used the events of Saint-Domingue to make their case... Haiti mattered to all of them, but only as pretext to talk about something else"
- **Crane is A+C merger** — Article 11 read differently across eras AND each era using it for its own fight. That's why the article works — two patterns on the same document.
- **Channel mapping:** treaty/decree/document videos = A+C; "consensus-is-more-complicated" videos = B; "this event got weaponized" videos = C.
- **Status:** TIER 2 (validated), no longer Tier 3 (single-source).

### S4 — Said's STACKED DEFINITIONS framework establishment
- 3 layers, each more concrete than the last: (1) Academic (the field/scholars), (2) Epistemological (a style of thought), (3) Institutional/material (Western style for dominating the Orient).
- Explicit signaling: "I mean several things, all of them, in my opinion, interdependent... a more general meaning... the third meaning..."
- **Channel verdict:** ADAPT — works compressed (200 words, not 2,000) when the central concept needs unpacking. Move from abstract to concrete in sequence so reader latches onto the most accessible layer even if they miss the more theoretical ones.

### S5 — Ginzburg's THREE-CASE BRAID + SHARED-ORIGIN REVEAL
- Surface: three unrelated cases (Morelli/art attribution, Sherlock Holmes/detection, Freud/psychoanalysis).
- Hidden link: > "In all three cases, infinitesimal traces permit the comprehension of a deeper, otherwise unattainable reality"
- Origin reveal: > "Freud was a physician; Morelli had a medical degree; Conan Doyle had practiced medicine before turning to literature... the model of medical semiotics is evident"
- **Channel verdict:** DIRECT TRANSFER. This is HOW>WHY at structure level — the channel's growth axis. Use for any video covering 3+ cases that share an underlying mechanism (e.g., "three different border disputes that hinged on the same bad map projection"). The mechanism reveal is the payoff that makes comparative content worth more than its parts.
- **Cross-format:** Also viable for script-writer-v2 — "Three X That Failed for the Same Reason" titles map directly.

### S6 — Hobsbawm's WORD-ZOOM DECODE (sub-pattern under Rule 13b)
- After the Swiss Federal Song Festival statute quote, Hobsbawm decodes ONE WORD: > "(The word 'improvement' introduces the characteristic note of nineteenth-century progress.)"
- A parenthetical word-level decode that extracts the era's ideology from one lexical choice.
- **Toolkit move:** when the speaker's word choice betrays the larger ideology, zoom on the word rather than the gestalt. Not the default — use when one word is doing disproportionate work.
- **Routine confirmation:** Hobsbawm's overall pattern (setup → quote → re-entry) holds Rules 13a + 13b. Adds a refinement, not a contradiction.

### S7 — Darnton's QUESTIONING RE-ENTRY (sub-pattern under Rule 13b)
- After the grim peasant version of Little Red Riding Hood: > "What is the moral of this story? For little girls, clearly: stay away from wolves. For historians, it seems to be saying something about the mental world of the early modern peasantry. But what? How can one begin to interpret such a text?"
- Darnton names the interpretive PROBLEM before resolving it. Reader gets a shared-puzzlement beat before the analysis.
- **Toolkit move:** re-enter with questions instead of answers when (a) the quote is genuinely puzzling, (b) you're setting up a standard reading to demolish, (c) the audience needs to feel the difficulty before resolution.
- **Channel relevance:** strong fit for myth-busting articles where the surface reading is the wrong one. The questioning beat earns the right to overturn it.
- **Routine confirmation:** Darnton uses fairy tale as primary source — opens a small category note: myths/folk-stories/pop-cultural artifacts as legitimate primary sources for the channel (most channel videos use archival documents but folk/oral sources are valid).

### S8 — Grandin's THREAD-AND-REFRAME closing (refines Closing Type 4 / FULL-CIRCLE)
- Grandin opens with Bolton's 1932 AHA address. Bolton's question — "who has written on a Western Hemisphere scale...?" — frames the entire inquiry. The article evaluates whether Bolton's hope for hemispheric unity panned out.
- Closing: > "suggesting that Bolton's remarks should be read not as an illustration of a movement toward unity, as he meant them, but as an opening to thinking about the history of Greater America as an ideological contest, an ongoing rivalry to define its exceptionalism."
- **Distinction:**
  - **Crane = FULL-CIRCLE BRACKET** — anchor opens + closes; body goes elsewhere. Anchor and body don't share spine.
  - **Grandin = THREAD-AND-REFRAME** — anchor is also the spine running through the article. Closing re-reads what the spine MEANT.
- **Channel implication:** when a topic has one primary source that can carry the whole article (single treaty article, one ICJ paragraph, one founding speech), thread-and-reframe is stronger than bracket. Three uses of the same source for the price of one.
- **Sub-pattern under Rule 13b:** Grandin's re-entry style after quotes is **NAMED-EXAMPLE DECODE** — > "Here, then, is a remarkably clear example of [thesis-relevant pattern]..." Use when the source illustrates a principle the article is establishing.
- **Routine confirmation:** Rules 13a + 13b hold (setup → quote → re-entry). Closing is a refined sub-variant of synthesis.

### S9 — Subheading divergence: corpus mostly skips them; Crane's pattern is the channel-fit exception
- Academic norm in corpus = NO subheadings (Davis = Roman numerals; Grandin = CAPITALIZED FIRST-WORD section breaks; Wolfe = typographical dingbats only). 3 of 4 sampled.
- **Crane is the exception**, and his pattern is the only one Substack-fit:
  - "The Secular Opposition" — thematic phrase, names the era's actor
  - "Trampling upon the Cross" — primary-source quote fragment (Cobbett)
  - "Secular Afterlives" — thematic phrase, names the era's mood
  - "Not Formally unlike the Mohammedans" — primary-source quote fragment
  - "A Bold Text" — primary-source quote fragment
- **The Crane move:** alternation between thematic phrases and primary-source-quote fragments. Each rhetorical-quote subheading is a hook — reader sees "Trampling upon the Cross" and wants to know what that means; the section then unpacks the quote.
- **Channel rule (new):** article-writer MUST use subheadings (Substack format / mobile reader accessibility — academic no-subheading norm doesn't transfer). Crane's mixed pattern (thematic + rhetorical-quote) is the model. Avoid flat descriptive labels ("The Barbary Crises").
- **Format-driven divergence to log:** Substack demands a feature the academic corpus mostly avoids. We follow Crane, not the academic majority. This becomes a STRUCTURAL rule in article-writer.

### S10 — Macro-structure taxonomy + concede-and-pivot SCALES from sentence to architecture
- Three macro-structures across 4 sampled papers:
  - **CHRONOLOGICAL** (Crane) — era-by-era reception
  - **ANALYTICAL** (Wolfe, Snyder) — concept-driven, jumps eras/geographies
  - **HYBRID** (Grandin) — concept threading through narrative chronology, with explicit two-layer signaling: > "often narrated as X. But threading through this narrative is Y."
- **Channel-decision guide:**
  - Treaty/document/event reception → CHRONOLOGICAL
  - Comparative mechanism videos → ANALYTICAL
  - Myth-busting "told X, actually Y" → ANALYTICAL dismantle-and-rebuild
  - Surface-narrative + deeper-thread videos → HYBRID
- **Most important finding:** Snyder's article structure IS the concede-and-pivot move at architecture scale. Opening sentence ("Auschwitz... is in fact only the beginning of knowledge") = whole article shape (dismantle the conventional, rebuild around the bloodlands).
- **Channel rule:** if the opening hook is a concede-and-pivot at sentence level, the macro structure must mirror it. Unity of rhetoric and architecture. When scripts feel jumpy, check: does the body do what the hook promised?

### S11 — Storyboardable scenes: cinematic moments embedded in academic prose
- 5 scenes surfaced (Wolfe Cherokee dinner-table-to-burning-homes; Davis Montauban priest-on-donkey-backwards; Trouillot classroom interruption; Crane 1892 Central Music Hall banner; Mamdani Tecumseh refusing the chair).
- Three properties shared by all:
  1. **Single iconic visual element** anchors the scene (one image you could thumbnail).
  2. **Primary-source dialogue embedded inside the scene**, not cut to as separate testimony.
  3. **Analytical claim left for the viewer/reader to infer.** Wolfe never says "this illustrates the logic of elimination" — he shows bayonets in the doorway.
- **Channel rule for script-writer-v2:** when an evidence beat needs energy, build it as a STAGEABLE SCENE not as quoted documentary cutaway. Test: could a director storyboard this in 3-5 shots from these words alone? If yes = scene. If no = quoted evidence (probably less retentive).
- **Direct project hit:** Crane's 1892 Central Music Hall banner-over-Stars-and-Stripes is a B-roll moment for project 51 (current Treaty of Tripoli video). Single hero shot.

### S12 — Four sentence-rhythm patterns map to four video VO+cut moments
- **Snyder CHAIN REPETITION** (logical propulsion): clause-end = clause-start. > "we know about Auschwitz because there were survivors, and there were survivors because Auschwitz was a labour camp..." → Video: inescapable-conclusion beat with silence at the comma.
- **Trouillot ACCUMULATING PARALLELS** (temporal propulsion): "from X to Y, from X to Y, from X to Y" compressing time. → Video: time-compression montage. Each clause = date stamp + 1 image + 1 second.
- **Davis STACCATO-TO-CASCADE** (action mimesis): short blunt anchor → release into long cascade whose clauses mimic the action. → Video: hard-cut + short VO → release into longer montage as VO cascades. Short sentence = beat before wave.
- **Darnton LIST PROPULSION** (rapid-cut velocity): rapid action verbs in a string → unconjoined noun-list closer. → Video: each verb = one shot. Noun-list = 4 snap-cuts on the closer.
- **Channel rule for script-writer-v2:** when a beat needs energy, name the rhythm (CHAIN / ACCUMULATION / ANCHOR-THEN-WAVE / LIST) and pre-bake the cut pattern into the VO. Don't write "vary sentence length" — pick the rhythm that matches the action.

### S13 — Three paragraph-handoff mechanisms (micro-transitions that prevent jumpiness)
- **Cronon RAISED-QUESTION-THEN-ANSWERED:** P-N ends "Why is this?" / P-N+1 opens "Two possible answers to this question emerge..." → Video: VO ends with question on screen, transitions to "the answer" — open loop / close loop retention beat.
- **Trouillot ECHO WORD:** P-N ends "...were 'unthinkable' facts in the framework of Western thought." / P-N+1 opens "Pierre Bourdieu defines the unthinkable as..." → Video: VO ends on key term (also displayed as chyron), next beat picks up the same word. Word-level continuity = audio-visual continuity at the cut.
- **Wolfe THEMATIC INTERROGATION:** P-N ends with primary-source quote ("Kill the Indian in him and save the man") / P-N+1 opens "But just what kind of death is it that is involved in assimilation?" → Video: source on screen → analytical question pulled from the source's metaphor → analytical answer. Built-in retention move.
- **Channel rule for script-writer-v2:** every section transition should pick a mechanism — don't default to "Now let's turn to..." or section-header-only breaks. The handoff IS the engagement.

### S14 — Three recurring-motif types for video coherence
- **Trouillot COINED-TERM motif** ("unthinkable"): author seizes/coins a term that becomes the analytical anchor. Returns in opening, body definition, close.
- **Snyder PROPER-NOUN-AS-SYMBOL motif** ("Auschwitz"): named real thing that comes to stand for a category of received misunderstanding. Each return reinforces what it OBSCURES.
- **Crane SPECIFIC-EVIDENCE motif** ("Article 11"): the actual document/event is the spine. Discipline = use the EXACT same name every time, never paraphrase. "Article 11" not "the secularism clause."
- **Cross-cutting principle:** coherence in video comes from a NAMED RECURRING ANCHOR. Pick ONE named thing (concept / proper-noun symbol / specific evidence), use the same wording every return, ensure it appears in every major beat.
- **Channel rule for script-writer-v2:** before approving a script, identify the ONE named thing that appears in cold open + every act + close. If absent, add one or accept the script will feel jumpier than necessary.
- **Pairs with S10:** the recurring anchor and the macro structure should be doing the same work. Snyder's "Auschwitz" recurs *because* the structure is dismantle-Auschwitz / rebuild-bloodlands. Motif and architecture unified.

### S15 — Turn Pattern Taxonomy: four landing techniques for the 15-25% pivot
- All four turns share ONE structural property: **concession FIRST, claim SECOND** (macro-scale concede-and-pivot, validates S10 again).
- Four signal devices:
  - **Crane LENS-SHIFTING ROADMAP:** "It is through this lens, rather than the triumphalist secular narratives... that we can best understand..." — best for myth-busting that also needs a trajectory map.
  - **Trouillot PARADOX-FRAMING QUESTIONS:** "If some events cannot be accepted even as they occur, how can they be assessed later? ... How does one write a history of the impossible?" — best for high-level conceptual challenges. Use 2-3 escalating questions.
  - **Davis HISTORIOGRAPHIC "BUT" PIVOT:** "There is no doubt some violence has this character... but is this the only kind of social meaning?" — best when challenging a specific scholarly position.
  - **Wolfe "I CONTEND" THESIS DECLARATION:** "I contend that, though the two have converged... they should be distinguished." — best when the claim is sharp enough to land naked.
- **Channel rule for script-writer-v2:** when scripting the 15-25% turn, pick ONE of these four. Default = PARADOX-QUESTIONS for video (creates open-loop retention beat). Always concede first.
- **Channel rule for article-writer:** opening section must end with a turn (any of the four). Don't drift from setup into body — make the pivot visible.
