## 3. ✅ H3(c) — active dispute. Re-verified 2026-07-28, and it has ESCALATED since the June mine.

The June report's hook was the Feb 2026 court ruling. It has since become a running legal war:
- Panama Ports Company (CK Hutchison) filed **ICC arbitration**; claim **now above US$2 billion**
- **Panama missed the ICC response deadline** (13 March) and disputes the timetable
- Maersk and MSC hold Balboa and Cristóbal in interim control; COSCO suspended operations
- Beijing warned Panama would pay a "heavy price"
- Investment arbitration of this kind **runs for years** → the hook has runway well past publication

⚠ **Identity guard:** this is the closing rhyme, not the video. A 2026 tribunal voiding a canal
concession on sovereignty grounds ↔ a 1903 treaty no Panamanian signed. **If a beat explains present-day
US–China politics for its own sake, cut it.**
