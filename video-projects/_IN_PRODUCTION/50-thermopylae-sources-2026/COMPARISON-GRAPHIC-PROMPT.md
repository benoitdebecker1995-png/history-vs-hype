## PROMPT (copy-paste into Gemini with NannoBanana)

Create a clean, dark-themed comparison table graphic for a YouTube history video (1920x1080). The graphic compares three ancient accounts of the Battle of Thermopylae. Style: minimal, academic, dark background (#1a1a2e or similar), white/cream text, with subtle color-coding per column.

**Layout:** 4 rows × 3 columns, with row labels on the left. Column headers are the three ancient authors with brief identifiers underneath.

### COLUMN HEADERS

| HERODOTUS | DIODORUS SICULUS | CTESIAS OF CNIDUS |
|---|---|---|
| *Histories, c. 440 BCE* | *Library, c. 50 BCE* | *Persica, c. 400 BCE* |
| *Written ~40 years after* | *Written ~430 years after* | *Written ~80 years after* |

Color-code each column subtly: Herodotus = warm gold/amber, Diodorus = cool blue, Ctesias = muted green.

### DESIGN NOTES

- Italic for quoted text, bold for the key numbers/names
- Each cell should have: bold summary → italic quote → small citation reference
- Make the BETRAYER and LAST STAND rows visually pop (these are the most dramatic disagreements)
- Consider subtle red highlight or "≠" symbols where accounts directly contradict
- Bottom text: "All three accounts are Greek. No Persian narrative of Thermopylae survives."
- Keep it readable — this will be on screen for ~10-15 seconds
- NO decorative elements, borders, or clip art — clean data visualization only
- Font: sans-serif, modern (not faux-ancient)
