## M1 · WHY natives fought with Spain (set up BEFORE the cold open pays off)
NOT dupes, NOT "thought they were gods." Rational self-interest in existing local wars.
- **Aztec:** central Mexico = rival city-states (*altepetl*) with fierce "micropatriotic" identities. Mexica were "intolerable tyrants who gave very little for the high tribute" (Totonacs, via Cervantes); Tlaxcala economically blockaded (no salt/cotton/gold); sacrifice = "a gruesome public declaration of power." Cortés saw "an irresistible opportunity to divide and conquer." Allies = "agents of native ambition" (Restall); Ixtlilxóchitl wanted Tetzcoco to become the new imperial capital (Townsend).
- **Inca:** Atahualpa–Huáscar dynastic war had JUST ended when Pizarro landed 1532 (Atahualpa "savoring his recent victory," 80,000-man army at Cajamarca, D'Altroy). Atahualpa's "ghastly revenge" on the Cañari (kill every male incl. children → 15:1 women-to-men 15 yrs later, Cieza) → Cañari/Chachapoya "more than willing" to help Spain; Xauxa/Huanca "celebrated the Spaniards as authentic liberators," supplied them 20 years.
- **THE CLARITY DEVICE (use this):** Lockhart's **"Double Mistaken Identity"** — Spaniards thought they were imposing "the lordship of the king"; natives thought they were forming "an alliance of equals" to settle old scores. Both right, both wrong. Restall: "The Indian empire was in a manner conquered by Indians." Townsend's correction: not gods, not blind hatred — recognition of "a great imbalance of technological power… many wanted to be on the winning side entering a new era."

## M2 · ENCOMIENDA ≠ MITA ≠ SLAVERY (define each separately; never blur — the audience fights over this)
- **ENCOMIENDA** (Gibson): a private grant of the RIGHT to extract **tribute + labor** from a designated group of Indians "consigned" to an encomendero. **NOT land. NOT ownership of people** ("no landed property, dominium, or señorío"). Indians = "free vassals," could **not be bought or sold** (the legal line vs slavery). Reciprocal fiction: encomendero owed them Christianization + protection. Gibson: "in legal principle a benign agency… an instrument of authority for Spanish colonists and a source of terror for native peoples"; a "possession, not a property." First generation = "generalized abuse"; "slavery with extra steps" critique = the formal distinction "had a distant and impractical aspect" — careful historians keep both: legally-not-slavery AND practically-brutal.
- **MITA** (Cole/Stern): a STATE rotational labor **draft** (Quechua *mit'a* = "a turn"), built by Viceroy Toledo **1573** for Potosí from the pre-Inca communal rotation. Up to **1/7** of a province's adult men; 1 year, 1 week on / 2 off; mustered by **kurakas** (native brokers = "the means of compulsion"). The dangerous mine-haul work (apiris carrying ~25kg up 300m rawhide ladders, perpetual dark — Acosta's eyewitness) that paid wage-labor (mingas) refused. By 1600s morphed into a **cash tax** (*indios de faltriquera*). Cole's nuance: it persisted NOT from labor shortage but because Indians refused the deadly work.
- One-line distinction: encomienda = private tribute grant (early, Mexico-centered); mita = state mining draft (later, Potosí); slavery = chattel ownership. Three different things.

## M4 · KHIPU as an information system (frame as data tech, not a trinket)
Semasiographic = "meaning signs" readable in any language (like numerals), "akin to writing" (Urton). **Base-10 positional**: figure-8 knot = 1, long knots = 2–9, single knots = powers of ten; place-value by height on the cord; did +, −, ×, ÷ incl. fractions (Ascher). **Binary coding** (S/Z spin-ply + S/Z knot direction) signed categories e.g. moiety (Hanan/Hurin); **color** banding/seriation = labels for ayllus/tribute types. Held **census** (10 age-grades; pachaka = 100) + **tribute** ("all taxes and tributes, both paid and due," Garcilaso) + 1/3 "narrative" khipus (history/song, undeciphered). Proof it was real recording: the **1670 Santa Valley "Rosetta khipu"** matched to a Spanish document; survived at Tupicocha into living memory. **1583 Third Council of Lima**: declared khipus "idolatrous objects… ordered destroyed" → lost court validation, went clandestine ("alphabetic prejudice": non-alphabetic = "pagan," threatening "the right order of things"). NOT erased — criminalized.
