## COLD OPEN · Exhibit A′ — "Not 900 Spaniards. A native civil war." ✅ BULLETPROOF · [P] on screen
**Demand:** #1 "changed how I see it" beat (K&G 2,100 "5% Spanish 95% locals"; Armchair 3,300+4,400). **Gap:** military story told; the *both-legends-crop-it* frame unowned.

- **BLACK CROP:** "ruthless Spaniards slaughtered the natives" — erases that natives did most of the fighting.

## ACT 3 · Exhibit K — The Requerimiento ✅ (the single best laws-vs-reality artifact) · [P]
