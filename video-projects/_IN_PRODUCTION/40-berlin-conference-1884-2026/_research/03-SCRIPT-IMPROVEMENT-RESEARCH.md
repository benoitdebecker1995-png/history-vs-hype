### DRC / M23 / Goma — MAJOR NEW DEVELOPMENT (not in script)

**What happened:** On **January 27, 2025**, Rwanda-backed M23 rebels captured **Goma** — the capital of North Kivu province (population ~1 million). 900–2,000 killed in the offensive alone. 7+ million displaced across DRC.

**Why it matters for the script:** This is a LIVE example of Berlin Conference borders causing war RIGHT NOW:
- M23 is primarily **ethnic Tutsi** fighters (colonial identity category)
- Rwanda's president Kagame has **publicly questioned the borders** between Rwanda and DRC — claiming parts of eastern DRC were "historical Rwandan land taken away during the colonial period"
- The Kivu populations the script's verified research discusses (claim 11.6-11.8) are EXACTLY the groups driving this conflict
- US sanctioned Rwanda's military and top officials (March 3, 2026 — **today**)
- Rwanda explicitly uses "Greater Rwanda" narrative = questioning colonial borders drawn at Berlin

**Script integration option:** Add 1-2 sentences to the "Still Here" section:
> "In January 2025, Rwanda-backed rebels captured Goma, the capital of eastern Congo — a region whose ethnic composition was shaped by Belgian colonial administration. Rwanda's president has publicly questioned the colonial borders between the two countries. A hundred and forty-one years later, and those borders are still being fought over."

### Sahel Coups — ECOWAS Breakup (not in script)

**What happened:** Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger **formally left ECOWAS on January 29, 2025** — a seismic break from the 15-member West African bloc. The three junta-led states formed the **Alliance of Sahel States (AES)**, explicitly framing their withdrawal as anti-colonial.

**Why it matters:** These military leaders are *directly invoking colonial borders and French neocolonialism* as justification for their actions. This is Berlin Conference legacy playing out in real-time institutional politics.

**Script relevance:** Probably too much to add to a 10-minute video, but worth mentioning in the description or as a card. The borders drawn at Berlin created the states that are now breaking apart from ECOWAS.

**Key stat:** Violence fatalities in Burkina Faso have **tripled** since the 2022 coup — 17,775 deaths in 3 years.

**Sources:**
- [Crisis Group: Sahel military-led states](https://www.crisisgroup.org/cmt/africa/sahel/burkina-faso-mali-niger/defining-new-approach-sahels-military-led-states)
