# Substack Notes — Haiti Article

**Article:** "Haiti Paid for Its Own Freedom"
**Strategy:** Post 2/day starting 2-3 days before publishing. Don't link article until Note 3-4. After publishing, post one with the link.

---

## Note 1 (the comparison)

In 1947, prosecutors at Nuremberg sentenced Nazi war criminals for the use of slave labor. The same year, Haiti made its final payment to an American bank — settling a 122-year bill for having freed itself from slavery.

## Note 2 (the number)

France charged Haiti 150 million francs for independence. Two decades earlier, France sold the Louisiana Territory — 77 times the size of Haiti — to the US for 80 million francs. Freedom cost more than land.

## Note 3 (the mechanism)

Haiti borrowed 30 million francs from a French bank to pay France. After fees, Haiti received 24 million. The money traveled from the bank's vaults to the French treasury. It never left Paris.

## Note 4 (the returns)

French banks clearing Haiti's debt payments earned 15-24% annual returns. Those profits helped build the bank that financed the Eiffel Tower. By 1914, 80% of Haiti's entire budget went to France and French banks.

## Note 5 (the steelman)

The Duvaliers stole $1.65 billion from Haiti — three times what France extracted directly. Haiti's poverty is overdetermined. But no country can lose 80% of its budget to foreign creditors for generations and emerge with functioning infrastructure. The Duvaliers robbed a house that had already been stripped to the studs.

## Note 6 (primary source)

Marine General Smedley Butler, years later: "I helped make Haiti and Cuba a decent place for the National City Bank boys to collect revenues in." The creditor changed from France to America. The debt did not.

## Note 7 (the scene)

After signing the ransom, a Haitian senator toasted a French bishop who had defended Haiti's independence. The French officers set down their glasses with "visible disapproval." The Haitians drank with "noisy enthusiasm." France had just delivered a ransom note at gunpoint and was offended someone toasted the wrong Frenchman.

## Note 8 (verdict)

Haiti is the only country in history where the formerly enslaved compensated their former enslavers. Not symbolically. In cash. Over 122 years.
