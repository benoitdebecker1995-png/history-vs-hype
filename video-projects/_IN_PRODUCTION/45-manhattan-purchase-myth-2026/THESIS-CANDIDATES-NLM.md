## WHAT THIS NOTEBOOK DOES NOT SUPPORT

The forensic evidence in these sources legally precludes the following common YouTube framings:

- **Framing 1 — "The Dutch were uniquely evil conquerors who deliberately swindled the Lenape out of their land in 1626."**
  The record shows the Dutch operated under strict corporate protocol. The West India Company's 1625 instructions explicitly ordered colonists to *"induce them to give up ownership and possession to us, without however forcing them thereto in the least"*.

- **Framing 2 — "The Lenape were guileless dupes easily distracted by shiny trinkets."**
  The record proves the Lenape were executing a calculated, negative-reciprocity geopolitical strategy against the Mohawks. As Russell Shorto concludes: *"the Indians were far from guileless dupes, and in the short term, which was what mattered at the time, they got considerably more out of a simple land transaction than the amount of the purchase indicates."*
