## 3 Surgical Fixes (data-backed)

### FIX 1 — Hook: lead with a NUMBER, not a sentence

**Data:** "First sentence must contain either a SPECIFIC NUMBER or a SHOCKING VERB. Never start with context." — analytics-findings.md, retention-script-correlation analysis (4,200 data points)

**Current opening (line 40):**
> "This is the only document from 1626 that mentions the purchase of Manhattan."

→ No number, no shocking verb. Number ("60 guilders") arrives 30s in.

**Recommended opening:**
> "Sixty guilders. That is the entire 1626 record of the Manhattan Purchase. One sentence. In a cargo manifest. Surrounded by a list of beaver skins."

**Why this wins on data:**
- Cold_fact hook: 3.7x normalized views (n=11, MEDIUM confidence)
- Specific number as first words (matches top retention pattern)
- "Manhattan" still in first 10 seconds (failure pattern #5: bury the lede)
- Specificity bomb backup: "60 guilders" + "beaver skins" = named-document signal (5.4x normalized views, n=5)
- Same 8 lines of script, ~3 seconds shorter

### FIX 2 — Add escalation phrase at 50% mark

**Data:** Wave 4 mid-video recovery analysis (55-source NLM corpus): "Escalation phrases at 30-70% zone — 'And it gets worse' / 'But here's where the evidence gets damning'. Force viewer to actively process."

**Current 50% mark (line 124, ACT 4):**

→ Already uses an escalation marker ("even stranger") but soft.

**Recommended insertion (replace line 124 lead):**

**Why:** "damning" = Calm Prosecutor register match. Current "even stranger" is ranked weaker per Wave 4 escalation phrase library.
