## PRIMARY SOURCES [P]

### [P1] Pieter Schagen Letter (November 5, 1626)
- **Type:** Official correspondence — ONLY primary source for the transaction
- **Original language:** Dutch
- **Translation used:** Yale Avalon Project (standard English translation)
- **Repository:** Dutch National Archives (Rijksarchief), The Hague
- **Access:** FREE — https://avalon.law.yale.edu/17th_century/charter_015.asp
- **Alt translation:** NYSL — https://www.nysl.nysed.gov/newnetherland/schaghentrans.htm
- **Facsimile:** http://www.nysl.nysed.gov/newnetherland/schaghenletter.htm
- **Key Content:** "They have purchased the island Manhattes from the Indians for the value of 60 guilders; 'tis 11,000 morgens in size." — the ENTIRE evidentiary basis
- **On-screen potential:** HIGH — facsimile image available, one-paragraph letter surrounded by cargo manifest
- **Status:** ✅ Downloaded (text confirmed)

### [P2] Staten Island Deed (August 10, 1626)
- **Type:** Land transaction deed — surviving comparison document from same colony, same year, same Director-General
- **Original language:** Dutch
- **Translation used:** NYSL transcription / Gehring translation (verify which)
- **Repository:** New York State Library, Albany
- **Access:** NYSL online transcription
- **Key Content:** Trade goods list — 10 muskets, 30 kettles, 50 axes, 30 pounds gunpowder, 10 boxes shirts, 10 ells red cloth, etc. NO BEADS.
- **On-screen potential:** HIGH — the goods list demolishes the "beads and trinkets" myth visually
- **Status:** ⏳ Pending — verify Gehring translation vs secondary transcriptions

### [P3] Kieft's War Primary Records (1640-1645)
- **Type:** Colonial administrative records / conflict documentation
- **Original language:** Dutch
- **Translation used:** Charles T. Gehring (trans.), *New Netherland Documents* series
- **Publisher:** Syracuse University Press / Holland Society, 1974-ongoing
- **Repository:** New York State Archives, Albany (originals); New Netherland Research Center
- **Key Content:** Wappinger attacks on Dutch New Netherland — proves 1626 transaction was NOT accepted by actual Manhattan territory holders
- **On-screen potential:** MEDIUM — less visual than the Schagen letter, but provides the "war" evidence
- **Status:** ⏳ Pending — may be in Gehring's *Council Minutes of New Netherland* or *Correspondence* volumes

### [P4] William Ranney — *The Purchase of Manhattan* (1855 painting)
- **Type:** Visual primary source (19th-century myth construction)
- **Original:** Museum or private collection (verify location)
- **Access:** Public domain image (pre-1923)
- **Key Content:** The painting that CREATED the visual template of "beads and trinkets" — artistic invention, not historical record
- **On-screen potential:** VERY HIGH — show the painting, then contrast with the actual Schagen letter
- **Status:** ⏳ Pending — locate high-res public domain image

- **Publisher:** University of Oklahoma Press, 2009
