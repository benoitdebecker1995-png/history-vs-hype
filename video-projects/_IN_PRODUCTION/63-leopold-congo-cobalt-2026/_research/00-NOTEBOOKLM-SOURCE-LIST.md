## Tier 2 — Academic Monographs [A] (the best works on the topic)

**[A1] Hochschild, Adam. *King Leopold's Ghost*.** Houghton Mifflin, 1998. `[OWNED → …/KingLeopoldsGhost-Hochschild-1998-Houghton-Mifflin.pdf]` — the definitive narrative history.

**[A2] Vansina, Jan. *Paths in the Rainforests* (Wisconsin, 1990) / *Being Colonized: The Kuba Experience* (Wisconsin, 2010).** `[ACQUIRE]` — the foremost historian of Central-African societies; demographic/anthropological authority behind "population cut by half."

**[A3] Nzongola-Ntalaja, Georges. *The Congo from Leopold to Kabila: A People's History*.** Zed Books, 2002. `[ACQUIRE]` — the leading **Congolese** political scientist; the 1908→present arc. Non-Western academic voice.

**[A4] Vanthemsche, Guy. *Belgium and the Congo, 1885–1980*.** Cambridge UP, 2012. `[ACQUIRE]` — the essential Belgian scholarship and **the death-toll skeptic camp** (required for a real referee, not a pile-on).

**[A5] Ewans, Martin. *European Atrocity, African Catastrophe: Leopold II, the Congo Free State and its Aftermath*.** RoutledgeCurzon, 2002. `[OWNED → …/LeopoldIICongoAtrocity-Ewans-2002-RoutledgeCurzon.pdf]`.

**[A6] Grant, Kevin. *A Civilised Savagery: Britain and the New Slaveries in Africa, 1884–1926*.** Routledge, 2005. `[ACQUIRE]` — the definitive study of the CRA / "first modern human-rights campaign."

**[A7] Stengers, Jean & Vansina, Jan. "King Leopold's Congo, 1886–1908," in *The Cambridge History of Africa*, Vol. 6.** CUP. `[ACQUIRE]` — the demographic-debate anchor.

**[A8] Roes, Aldwin. "Towards a History of Mass Violence in the État Indépendant du Congo," *South African Historical Journal* (2010).** `[ACQUIRE: JSTOR]` — modern reassessment of the demographic methodology (steelmans the skeptic).

**[A9] Yabili, Marcel** — Congolese jurist (Lubumbashi); oral tradition + Lumumba on Leopold. Vol. 1 EN, rest FR (creator-verifiable). `[ACQUIRE]` — the Congolese voice surfaced in competitor comments.

*(Optional foundational: Slade, Ruth. *King Leopold's Congo*, Oxford, 1962.)*
