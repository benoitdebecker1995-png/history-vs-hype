**The mechanism (sourced):** Morel, a bilingual clerk at **Elder Dempster** (Liverpool, held the Congo cargo monopoly), sent to **Antwerp** to supervise loading. Ships arrived full of **rubber & ivory**; sailed back carrying **army officers, firearms, ammunition — and manacles/chains** (ledgered as military/recruitment supplies). **~80% of imports were "remote from trade purposes"**; no trade goods went in to pay for what came out. ### ✅ Claim (MECHANISM + LEGAL SHELL) — VERIFIED (NLM 2026-07-13; Casement + Hochschild + Ewans + Nzongola)
**The legal shell:** the CFS was **Leopold's personal private property**, legally distinct from Belgium — in Brussels a constitutional monarch, in the Congo the absolute **"proprietor."** Foundation = an **1885 *terra nullius* decree** declaring all land not physically occupied by houses/crops to be state property, so the king reserved all rubber & ivory to himself via the **domanial system**: the ***domaine privé*** (state monopoly on the rubber forests) + the secret ***domaine de la couronne*** (~250,000 km², >a tenth of the territory, around Lake Leopold II — Leopold's exclusive personal estate funding his Belgian building projects).
**The machine:** extraction "outsourced" to **concession companies — ABIR (Anglo-Belgian India Rubber), the Anversoise, Compagnie du Kasaï** — in which the state held ~50% of shares and granted "rights of police." Compulsion = **rubber quotas** (~3–4 kg dried rubber per man per fortnight) enforced by **hostage-taking** (women/children held until quota met), the ***chicotte*** (hippo-hide whip), village burning, and the **cartridge/severed-hand accounting rule** (a right hand per bullet — Casement primary, pp. 21/29/34/43).

### ⚖️ CONTESTED — THE NUMBER (the referee beat) — DOCUMENTED (NLM 2026-07-13, both camps confirmed verbatim)
**Status:** CONTESTED — present as a genuine scholarly dispute; NEVER assert a single figure. Camps grounded in notebook `0b7dd2b2`.
**MAXIMALIST camp:**
- **Hochschild** ~10M: 1924 census = 10M vs a 20–30M baseline → "cut by at least half" over ~30 yrs. Corroborated by the **1919 Belgian government commission** ("reduced by half") and Major Liebrechts (1920).
- **Nzongola-Ntalaja:** "as high as 10 million" (20–30M → 8.5M in 1911 → 10M in 1924); names the ABIR & Compagnie du Kasaï concession companies + the *chicotte*.
**SKEPTIC / "AGNOSTIC" camp (the referee balance — CONFIRMED):**
- **Vanthemsche (verbatim):** *"Hochschild's estimate remains conjectural."* / *"No one knows the exact figure… the first, rather unreliable, population statistics only go back to 1924."* / *"it is risky to suggest a single percentage… valid for the entire… territory."* BUT: *"no one contests the fact that… millions of people perished under white power."*
- **⚠ CRUCIAL NUANCE — Vansina walked back his own "half":** Hochschild cites Vansina's "cut by at least a half," but **Vansina later revised it** — the **Kuba population was actually *rising*** in the first two colonial decades; ~25% decline came **1900–1919, mainly from disease** [Vanthemsche citing Vansina]. Do NOT present "Vansina says half" as settled.
- **Roes (verbatim):** "maximalists" vs "agnostics"; *"a firm estimate of the number of victims must indeed remain elusive"* (most deaths never recorded); argues for a "precise geography of the impact" over "sterile polemic about aggregated numbers."
**Causation nuance:** decline = murder + starvation/exhaustion + disease (sleeping sickness, smallpox) + collapsed birth rate — the epidemics were *facilitated by* the forced-labour regime (Roes), so "it was just disease" (the CFS's own rebuttal in the Casement Report, blaming sleeping sickness) is the deflection to name and answer.
**THE REFEREE'S VERDICT (grounded):** the exact toll is *unknowable — no census, and Leopold burned the archives* — but every camp, skeptics included, agrees the dead number in the **millions**. The unknowability is itself the signature of the crime.
