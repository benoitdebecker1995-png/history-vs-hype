## KEY PRIMARY DOCUMENT: The Beaumont Memo

Christopher Beaumont (Radcliffe's private secretary) gave testimony to journalist Simon Scott Plummer in 1992, published as:

**Simon Scott Plummer, "How Mountbatten Bent the Rules and the Indian Border," *Daily Telegraph*, February 24, 1992**

- This is a newspaper article, not a primary document
- It is the only published account of Beaumont's allegation that Mountbatten changed the Punjab award
- Upload the article text to NotebookLM as a note or text file
- Cite in script as: "In a 1992 *Daily Telegraph* account, Christopher Beaumont — Radcliffe's own private secretary — alleged that..."
- Do NOT treat as definitive proof; frame as corroborating evidence alongside the Abell letter
