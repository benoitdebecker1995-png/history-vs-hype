## Topic

How Britain partitioned India in 5 weeks — the Radcliffe Line, Mountbatten's rushed timeline, and the 15 million displaced. Framed as a British Empire logistics/mechanism story, not an India-centric history lesson.

## Angle

"How they drew it" — the logistics crowd trigger. Focus on the mechanism: one British lawyer (Cyril Radcliffe) who had never been to India, given 5 weeks to draw a border for 400 million people. Document-heavy, map-heavy.

## Key Research Questions

1. Exactly how was Cyril Radcliffe chosen and briefed? What were his instructions?
2. What was the actual timeline — when did he arrive, when was the line finalized?
3. What primary documents exist? (Radcliffe Award, Mountbatten papers, Boundary Commission records)
4. How many died in partition violence? (Verified academic range, not inflated popular claims)
5. What does the border look like today — any active disputes from the original line?
6. What did Radcliffe himself say about the process afterward?
7. Kashmir — how does the incomplete partition connect to today's dispute?
