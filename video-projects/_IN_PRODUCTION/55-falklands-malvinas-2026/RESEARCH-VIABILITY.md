## Check B: Specificity-bomb count

Requirement ≥3. Identified 7, each with a credible source path:

1. **The 1850 Arana–Southern Treaty** — the treaty that "restored perfect relations" and the question
   of whether it names the islands at all. *(Treaty text — direct read)*
2. **Argentina's first flag was raised by an American.** David Jewett, 1820, an American-born
   privateer, not an Argentine national. *(Goebel 1927)*
3. **The USS *Lexington* wrecked the Argentine settlement in 1831 — two years before Britain arrived.**
   What Onslow displaced in 1833 was already a ruin. *(US naval records; Goebel)*
4. **Britain left a plaque instead of a garrison** when it withdrew in 1774. *(Goebel)*
5. **The 1771 agreement let Britain return while both sides formally reserved sovereignty** — the
   template for 250 years of non-resolution. *(Diplomatic text)*
6. **34 years of Argentine silence, 1850–1884.** *(Diplomatic record; Laver 2001)*
7. **99.8% — three votes against**, and Argentina's answer that the voters don't count.
   *(FIG record; Argentine "implanted population" doctrine)*

- **Result: PROCEED**
