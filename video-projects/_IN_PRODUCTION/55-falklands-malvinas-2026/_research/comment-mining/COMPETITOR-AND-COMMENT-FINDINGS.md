### Arguments both camps are already making (the steelman is pre-written)
Argentine side: *uti possidetis* / Spanish inheritance · 1767–1833 continuous administration · the
1833 expulsion · UN Res. 2065 and the Decolonization Committee · "implanted population" doctrine
voiding the 2013 referendum · continental shelf · Belgrano outside the exclusion zone.
British side: 1690 Strong landing / prior claim · self-determination since Wilson · 2013 referendum
99.8% · part of the islander population is of Argentine descent and still voted to stay · the
territory is already self-governing, so "decolonised" ≠ "handed to Argentina."

### Toxicity: real but bounded — and categorically safer than Herero
Nationalist trash talk, football abuse, Nazi jibes at Argentina, one nasty anti-immigration line.
But **no genocide denial, no Holocaust-denial recruitment funnel**, and a large share of the heat is
substantive argument about dates and law. Herero's comment sections were a denial funnel; this is a
pub argument. Survivable, and the referee posture is built for it.

## 4. The gap, stated precisely

The English-language shelf sells the **war**. The Spanish-language shelf carries the **claim**
(Rosendo Grobo, 278K, *how to recover the Malvinas*). Geography By Geoff knew the legal question was
the demand and **pushed it off YouTube to a Substack post**. Nobody in English puts the two cases
side by side and lets the documents decide.

Meanwhile a 7,180-sub channel just did 1.12M views on this topic in six days.
