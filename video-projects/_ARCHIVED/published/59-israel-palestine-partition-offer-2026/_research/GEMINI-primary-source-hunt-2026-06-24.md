## ITEM 1 — LIBERIA "high pressure electioneering"
- **VERDICT: ARCHIVAL-ONLY.** Minute of the 9 Dec 1947 meeting with the Liberian secretary of state, NARA RG59, Central Decimal File 1945–49, **501.BB Palestine/12-947**.
- **NOT printed in FRUS 1947 v.V**; FRUS prints neighbouring files from the same index but skips this minute. Adjacent docs viewable at history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1947v05/ch11.
- The Firestone / President Tubman pressure (threat to a ~$4.5M development loan) is reproduced from the raw RG59 records in **Michael J. Cohen, *Palestine and the Great Powers 1945–1948*, p.298** (our card uses p.297). Confidence: high.
- **→ Keep S→P via Cohen.**

## ITEM 2 — PHILIPPINES telegram (Murphy & Frankfurter → President Roxas)
