## THE CLAIM
"Two US Supreme Court Justices — Frank Murphy and Felix Frankfurter — sent a telegram to the Philippine president warning that his country would 'isolate millions and millions of American friends and supporters' if it kept voting against the 1947 partition of Palestine."
As carried in-project: cited to **Cohen, *Palestine and the Great Powers, 1945–1948*, p.297** (notebook `d7ba048e`), fn97.

## THE LADDER (top = as-cited, bottom = attempted bedrock)

**1. Cohen, *Palestine and the Great Powers*, p.297** — TIER 3 secondary (in notebook `d7ba048e`). Raw-read char-exact (off `source_get_content`, incl. Cohen's soft hyphens):
> "A certain amount of documentation on the lobby has now come to light. Supreme Court Justices Frank Murphy and Felix Frank­furter pressed the Philippines ambassador in Washington to sup­port partition. The two justices also sent a telegram to the Phil­ippine President Carlos Rojas, warning that his country would 'isolate millions and millions of American friends and supporters if they continued in their effort to vote against partition.' Ten American senators cabled the president in a similar vein. By Friday, November 28, the Philippine delegate was ready to vote in favor of partition.⁹⁷"

Two things off this exact page matter:
- **Cohen asserts documentary basis ("documentation … has now come to light") but footnotes only secondary retellings** — the classic footnote-laundering pattern (`feedback-attribution-audit` §provenance). He does not cite the "documentation."
- Cohen writes the president as **"Carlos Rojas."** The president was **Manuel Roxas**; "Carlos" appears to bleed in from delegate **Carlos P. Romulo**. NAME ERROR — do not propagate.

**2. Cohen's footnote 97** — raw-read char-exact:
> "97 Urofsky, We Are One, pp. 145 ff.; Donovan, Conflict and Crisis, pp. 329-330."

The same pair anchors the neighbouring footnotes of this passage (also raw-read char-exact):
> "93 Henderson interview, HST; also Urofsky, We Are One, pp. 145 ff.; and Donovan, Conflict and Crisis, pp. 329-330."
> "87 Urofsky, We Are One, pp. 145 ff." · "101 Donovan, Conflict and Crisis, p. 331."

So Cohen's **entire** Murphy/Frankfurter → Philippines paragraph rests on: **Melvin I. Urofsky, *We Are One! American Jewry and Israel* (Anchor/Doubleday, 1978), pp.145 ff.**, and **Robert J. Donovan, *Conflict and Crisis: The Presidency of Harry S. Truman, 1945–1948* (Norton, 1977), pp.329–330** — plus a **Loy Henderson oral-history interview (Truman Library)** cited for the *ambassador*-pressing sentence, not the cable. All three are TIER 3 secondary / TIER 2 hostile-witness testimony; **none is a document, and none reproduces the telegram in Cohen's citation.**

**3. Urofsky p.145 ff. and Donovan pp.329–330 — the next rungs. Their own footnotes could NOT be opened via any free route (see §ACCESS below); I therefore have NOT read what they cite.** Honest status: *unread reachable-but-gated rungs.* Neither is in the notebook or the owned `library/` (grepped: absent). Both are 1970s **narrative** syntheses, not archival monographs, which lowers the prior that either prints an archival locator — but that is an inference, not a verified read.

**4. Attempted bedrock — the archival original.** If a Murphy/Frankfurter cable to Roxas survives, it is a private document, most plausibly in the **Frank Murphy Papers, Bentley Historical Library, University of Michigan** (finding aid `umich-bhl-86734`) and/or the **Felix Frankfurter Papers, Library of Congress**. The archival-grade published work that would carry the exact citation is **Sidney Fine, *Frank Murphy: The Washington Years* (Univ. of Michigan Press, 1984)** — Fine wrote directly from the Murphy Papers. It, too, is gated (archive.org `frankmurphy0003fine`, printdisabled). **No showable primary was reached.**

## CROSS-CHECK AGAINST THE NOTEBOOK'S OTHER RIGOROUS HISTORIANS (corroborates SECONDARY-ONLY)
- **Benny Morris, *1948* (notebook `234bb4fe`)** — meticulous footnoter, covers this exact 48-hour pressure episode, and **does NOT mention the Justices' telegram at all** (zero hits on "Frankfurter"/"Murphy"/"two justices"). Morris instead writes: *"Senators and congressmen also interceded. Twenty-eight senators cabled a dozen wobbling governments to vote for partition…"* — footnoted to **Cohen p.296 and "Comay to Gering, 3 December 1947"** (a Jewish-Agency letter, ISA). A historian of Morris's rigour, working the same sources, silently dropped the Justices' cable.
- **Victor Kattan, *From Coexistence to Conquest* (notebook `94a0ed73`)** — details the pressure campaign but attributes it to **Truman's memoirs** and **Zafrullah Khan's memoirs**; **no Justices' telegram** (zero "Murphy" hits).
- **FRUS 1947 v5 d910** (the contemporaneous US-government primary, already traced) — records Roxas's *own* stated reasons for the switch: a **ten-Senators** "high-pressure telegram" + a report via **Ambassador Elizalde** that a "United States representative" warned of harm to relations. **The Justices' telegram is absent from the primary record**, and the State Dept there formally *denied* pressuring anyone.
- **HathiTrust full-text search:** Cohen's exact quoted string **"millions of American friends and supporters" returns NO results** across all digitized volumes → Cohen's on-screen "quote" is very likely a **compression/paraphrase**, not a verbatim transcription of any published or archival text. Do not treat it as a char-exact quotation.

## ATTRIBUTION MISMATCHES
- **PROVENANCE / FOOTNOTE-LAUNDERING (primary flag):** Cohen frames the passage as documentary ("documentation … has now come to light") but footnotes only two 1970s secondary histories. On screen this must **not** be rendered as "documents show" — it is a historians' account whose own sourcing is presently unverified.
- **QUOTE-DRIFT:** Cohen's "isolate millions and millions of American friends and supporters" has **no verbatim match** in HathiTrust full text → treat as Cohen's paraphrase, not a transcribed quotation. Do not put it in quotation marks on screen as if lifted from the cable.
- **NAME ERROR (do not propagate):** Cohen "**Carlos Rojas**" → the president was **Manuel Roxas** (FRUS spells it correctly).
- **CORROBORATION GAP:** two independent rigorous historians in the notebook (Morris, Kattan) do **not** carry the Justices' telegram; the contemporaneous US primary (FRUS d910) does not contain it. The claim is thinly attested and single-stranded (Cohen←Urofsky/Donovan).
