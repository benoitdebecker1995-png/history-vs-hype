## TIER 1 — PRIMARY DOCUMENTS (upload first; the format reads these on screen)

### [P1] UN General Assembly Resolution 181(II) — full text + partition map
- **Type:** Primary document (UN GA resolution + annexed map)
- **Origin:** UN General Assembly, 29 Nov 1947
- **Access:** Avalon Project (Yale) `avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/res181.asp`; UN official record (free, digitized, English)
- **Provenance:** The document itself — Parts I (governance/UN Palestine Commission), II (boundaries), III (Jerusalem corpus separatum), IV (economic union). The map is the on-screen centerpiece.
- **Bias/proximity/intent:** Contemporaneous instrument; drafted by the Ad Hoc Committee. Read it as the text under audit, not a neutral narrator.
- **Carries claims:** allocation %, fragmentation, economic-union clause, Commission powers, non-binding status, Jerusalem.
- **Status:** Pending upload

### [P2] UNSCOP Report (UN Doc A/364)
- **Type:** Primary document (committee report)
- **Origin:** UN Special Committee on Palestine, 3 Sep 1947
- **Access:** UN archives / UNISPAL (free, English)
- **Provenance:** The 11-member committee's majority partition report + the minority federal-union plan; population analysis and boundary rationale.
- **Bias/proximity/intent:** Investigative committee with known internal split (7–3–1); the minority report is itself evidence of the contested viability question.
- **Carries claims:** demographics inside zones, the 45% figure, the federal alternative, Bedouin-correction context, committee process/boycott.
- **Status:** Pending upload

## TIER 2 — ACADEMIC (free-access only for the pilot; carry the interpretive forks)

### [A1] Morris, Benny. *1948: A History of the First Arab-Israeli War*. Yale University Press, 2008.
- **Provenance:** University-press monograph; the standard Anglophone military-political history of 1948.
- **Bias/proximity/intent:** Mainstream Israeli historian (revised from earlier "New Historian" positions); useful for the "reluctant compromise" framing of Zionist acceptance and the run-up to war.
- **Fork it serves:** Zionist acceptance genuine-vs-tactical (the mainstream pole).
- **Access:** Library/NLM only — do NOT purchase for pilot. Status: deferred unless free.

### [A2] Flapan, Simha. *The Birth of Israel: Myths and Realities*. Pantheon, 1987.
- **Provenance:** Post-Zionist revisionist monograph.
- **Bias/proximity/intent:** Explicitly revisionist; argues Zionist acceptance was tactical (stepping-stone). Partisan pole — label as such on screen.
- **Fork it serves:** the tactical-acceptance counter-pole (vs Morris). Needed for an honest split, not as truth.
- **Access:** Library/NLM only. Status: deferred unless free.

### [A3] Louis, Wm. Roger. *The British Empire in the Middle East 1945–1951*. Oxford University Press, 1984.
- **Provenance:** Oxford-press diplomatic history; authoritative on Bevin/British policy.
- **Fork it serves:** Britain neutral-withdrawal vs deliberate-non-cooperation.
- **Access:** Library/NLM only. Status: deferred unless free.

### [A4] Quigley, John. *Palestine and Israel: A Challenge to Justice*. Duke University Press, 2012. (legal)
- **Fork it serves:** legal status of Res 181 (non-binding recommendation vs allocation). Confirms the Charter-based "recommendation" mechanism word.
- **Access:** Library/NLM only. Status: deferred unless free.
