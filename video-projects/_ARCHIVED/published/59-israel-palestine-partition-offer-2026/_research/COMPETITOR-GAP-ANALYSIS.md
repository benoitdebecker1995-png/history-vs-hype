## THE GAP (our edge)
> Resolution 181 is treated everywhere as a **moral** question ("Jews accepted a fair compromise, Arabs rejected it") — never an **administrative** one. Nobody asks: what did the UN actually require to happen institutionally, and was it ever implementable? Part I established a 5-member UN Palestine Commission with no enforcement powers; Britain refused to cooperate; the plan required a functioning joint economic authority within ~2 months. That's a procedural/legal gap that fits HOW > WHY exactly — and the document (Avalon/UN) can go on screen.
