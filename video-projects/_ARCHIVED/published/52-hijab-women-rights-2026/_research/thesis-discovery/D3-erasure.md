## 7 Named-Woman Erasures Surfaced

### 1. 'Aisha bint Abi Bakr ⭐⭐⭐
- **Original role:** military commander (Battle of the Camel), supreme intellectual authority who corrected hadith
- **Eraser:** **Ibn Sa'd (d. 845)** + **Sa'id al-Afghani (1946)**
- **Specific change:**
  - Ibn Sa'd's **17-page biography of 'Aisha** mentions NEITHER the Battle of the Camel NOR her leadership role
  - Al-Afghani (1946) used her "regret" to argue *"woman was not created for poking her nose into politics"*
- Source 4, p. 426; Source 7, p. 643-645; Source 9, p. 1003
- **Modern relevance:** still cited today against women in politics

### 2. Umm Salama
- **Original role:** political advisor — gave crucial wisdom at **Treaty of Hudaybiyya**, witness to Khaybar
- **Eraser:** **Ibn Sa'd (d. 845)**
- **Specific change:** biography reduced to lineage, jealousy, and that she *"labored for hours to prepare a meal"* — omitted her status as primary traditionist and strategist
- Source 4, p. 439-440

### 3. al-Shifā' bint 'Abd Allāh ⭐⭐⭐
- **Original role:** literate healer; **Caliph Umar put her in charge of the market**
- **Erasers:** **Ibn 'Abd al-Barr (d. 1070)** + medieval scribes
- **TWO specific changes:**
  - Market role reattributed to her son Sulayman ("doubts ʿUmar would give such a task to a woman")
  - **Scribes changed ONE LETTER in a hadith: *al-kitāba* (writing) → *al-kitāb* (The Book)** — to obscure that the Prophet encouraged her to teach **writing** to women
- Source 1, p. 11, 21-22, 27
- **THE SPECIFICITY BOMB:** *"They changed one letter to keep women illiterate."*

### 4. Sukayna bint al-Husayn ⭐⭐⭐
- **Original role:** Prophet's great-granddaughter; refused the hijab; dictated her own marriage contract terms
- **Eraser:** **a Pakistani editor of an Islamic journal in London, 1984**
- **Specific change:** publicly accused researchers of lying, shouted **"Sukayna died at the age of six!"** at Karbala — to erase her entire adult history
- Source 7, p. 795; Source 9, p. 1076
- **THE LIVING MECHANISM:** erasure is not 9th century only — it's 1984

### 5. Fatima bint Muhammad
- **Original role:** defiant orator who **addressed Caliph Abu Bakr in the mosque** demanding her Fadak inheritance
- **Erasers:** later Sunni metanarrative lists
- **Specific change:** historical meaning shifted into *"ten things God gave women"* list — assigned "learning" and "sinlessness," dehumanized into symbol of quiet piety
- Source 10, p. 1157, 1162, 1165

### 6. Safiyya bint Huyayy
- **Original role:** defied siege to deliver food to **Caliph Uthman** in distress
- **Eraser:** **Ibn Sa'd (d. 845)**
- **Specific change:** omitted her aid; instead reported moment on mule saying *"Take me back lest I get exposed"* — fear-of-exposure over political courage
- Source 4, p. 430-431

### 7. Rayḥāna bint Zayd
- **Original role:** **self-determinedly refused the Prophet's marriage proposal**, chose to remain in *milk* because "easier" for her
- **Erasers:** **al-Ṭabarānī (d. 971)** + **al-Ḥākim al-Naysābūrī (d. 1014)**
- **Specific change:** omitted proposal and refusal entirely; recast solely as *concubine (istasarra)* to serve later **"legitimization of concubinage"**
- Source 4, p. 501-502, 513
