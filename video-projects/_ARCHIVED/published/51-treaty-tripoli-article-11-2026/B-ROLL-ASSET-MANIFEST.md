# B-Roll Asset Manifest — Real Sources Only

**Principle:** the channel's pitch is primary sources on screen. Recreating period documents in HTML/CSS reads as AI slop and erodes the credibility advantage. This manifest locates the actual public-domain scans for every visual the script references.

**Two lists:**
1. **Real sources** — public-domain scans/portraits with URLs. These go on screen as-is.
2. **Modern graphics (build new)** — clearly-modern infographics that do NOT pretend to be old documents. Channel-branded, modern typography, citation footers visible. These are honest graphic interventions, not fakes.

## 1. Real sources to fetch

### 1.1 Hunter Miller, *Treaties and Other International Acts of the United States of America*, Vol. 2 (1931) — the central document of the video

**Where:** HathiTrust Digital Library — full public-domain Google scan of the 1931 Government Printing Office volume.
**URL:** https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=uiug.30112104106221
**Catalog:** https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/008699166
**Status:** Public Domain, Google-digitized. Full view.

**Pages to capture as B-roll:**
- **Cover / title page** — "Treaties and Other International Acts of the United States of America. Edited by Hunter Miller. Washington: Government Printing Office, 1931."
- **Page 368 area** — Hunter Miller's introduction to the Tripoli treaty notes; identifies Hurgronje's 1930 examination
- **Page 371** — Hurgronje's verbatim line: *"The eleventh article of the Barlow translation has no equivalent whatever in the Arabic. The Arabic text opposite that article is a letter from Hassan Pasha of Algiers to Yussuf Pasha of Tripoli."* + the "stupid secretary"/"bombastic words" passage
- **Page 384** — Hunter Miller's verbatim comment: *"Most extraordinary (and wholly unexplained) is the fact that Article 11 of the Barlow translation, with its famous phrase, 'the government of the United States of America is not in any sense founded on the Christian Religion,' does not exist at all."*
- **The Arabic facsimile page** — Miller's volume reproduces the actual Arabic manuscript page where Article 11 should be. Locate within the Tripoli section (1796/1797 treaty, Documents 1–40).

**How to capture (HathiTrust blocks automated fetch — manual workflow):**
1. Open the URL above in browser.
2. Use the page navigator to jump to the Tripoli section (Document number for 1796 treaty).
3. Use HathiTrust's built-in PDF download for individual pages, OR full-screen + browser screenshot at maximum zoom.
4. Image quality is sufficient for 1080p slow-zoom B-roll; for 4K you may want to stitch two screenshots.

**Alternative full-text-only source** (if you want to verify the quotes without the scan): Avalon Project at Yale Law School has Hunter Miller's notes verbatim — https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/bar1796n.asp
