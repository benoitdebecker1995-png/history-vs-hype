### Issues to Fix Before Filming

**1. ❌ "a quarter of a continent" — FACTUALLY INCORRECT**
- Congo Free State was ~2.34M km²
- Africa is ~30.37M km²
- That's ~7.7%, not 25%
- **Fix options:**
  - "gave one man a territory the size of Western Europe" (accurate — Congo ≈ France + Germany + Spain combined)
  - "gave one man seventy-seven times the size of Belgium as personal property"
  - "gave one man two and a half million square kilometres as personal property"

**2. ⚠️ "three months" — MINOR**
- Conference was Nov 15, 1884 – Feb 26, 1885 = ~3.5 months
- "Three months" is close enough for spoken delivery and sounds better
- No fix required, but be aware
