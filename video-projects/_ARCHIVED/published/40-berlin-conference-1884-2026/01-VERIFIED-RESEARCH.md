### Contested: Rwanda Genocide and Berlin Conference
**Claim:** The Rwanda genocide was caused/enabled by colonial ethnic manipulation at Berlin
**School A says:** The Hutu/Tutsi distinction was hardened by Belgian colonial policy (ID cards), which flows from Berlin Conference; colonial policy created the ethnic binary
**School B says:** Hutu/Tutsi distinctions pre-date colonialism; genocide had multiple complex causes
**Scholarly consensus:** Colonial policy significantly hardened the distinction but did not create it from nothing
**How to present:** "Colonial administrators didn't invent the Hutu/Tutsi distinction — but they hardened it into a legal category, which directly shaped the conditions for 1994."

### Contested: "No Africans in the room"
**Claim:** No Africans were present at the Berlin Conference
**Nuance:** Technically accurate for the conference itself; BUT some African leaders signed later bilateral agreements; some African elites were aware and protested (Sultan of Zanzibar)
**How to present:** "No African government, king, or representative was invited to Berlin. The continent was divided by people who, in many cases, had never set foot in Africa."
**Status:** ✅ VERIFIED — nuance documented above

### Contested: Conference as Primary Cause
**Claim:** The Berlin Conference caused the Scramble for Africa
**Nuance:** Historians argue it did NOT initiate the scramble — it provided rules for one already in progress. Most territory decided bilaterally. Conference was a "safety valve" not a starting gun.
**How to present:** "The Berlin Conference didn't start the scramble — but it created the rules that turned a scramble into a race."
**Status:** ✅ VERIFIED — Wesseling, Förster et al.
