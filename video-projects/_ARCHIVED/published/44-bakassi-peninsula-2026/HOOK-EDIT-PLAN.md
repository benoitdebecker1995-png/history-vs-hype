## General Tips

- **Slow zoom/pan = free production value.** Keyframe static images from 100% to 108-112% over 5-7 sec
- **Hard cuts, not dissolves** for the hook
- **Text overlays:** simple, white or off-white on dark, same font throughout. Cut in/out, don't animate
- **Cut on pauses** — the gaps between sentences in the SRT are your natural cut points. Cut to B-roll on the pause, not mid-sentence
- **Audio continuity:** voice runs continuous under all B-roll. Never cut the audio just because you cut the video
