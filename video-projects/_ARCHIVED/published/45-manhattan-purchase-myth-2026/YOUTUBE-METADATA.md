Every element of the myth was invented afterward: the $24 figure by a journalist in 1844, the beads by a painter in 1853, the Canarsee trickster narrative by a magazine in 1959. The bronze plaque in Inwood Hill Park — the one that calls the story a legend — was put up by the American Legion in 1954.

This video traces the fabrication element by element, using the Schagen Letter, historians Russell Shorto and Robert Grumet, and the recorded words of Lenape elder Hitakonanu'laxk.

CHAPTERS
0:00   The plaque that calls itself a legend
