## POST-FILM ATTRIBUTION DEFECT — cağferiye (logged 2026-06-02)

**Defect:** Beat 2 (delivered SRT seg 70–81, `01:02:27.199–01:02:50.966`) attributed a *word-argument* to Hapgood and Hancock: "the strongest piece of the theory comes down to one word, cağferiye… Hapgood read that word, and Hancock after him, as the name of an ancient, mysterious kind of map… that one word, to them, is the fingerprint of the lost civilization." **Both attributions false.**

**Why it passed the original gate:** every component quote was verbatim-true (Kahle, Hancock's "4th century BC," Hapgood-as-Alexandria-originator). The error was the *connective attribution* — welding true facts into a claim about what these men argued. The quote/claim-level checklist above has no test for attribution integrity. This is the "validated-quote ≠ validated-attribution" failure mode. New gate added (see `feedback-attribution-audit.md`, `/verify`, historian skill).

**Verification (2026-06-02):**
- **Hancock — false.** `01-VERIFIED-RESEARCH.md` Claim 15, "what stayed constant" pt 3: Hancock "cites the source-maps inscription as evidence of 'older, lost' sources **without engaging the named-source breakdown (Ca'feriyes…)**." None of the five 1995–2024 framings reads the word.
- **Hapgood — false.** NLM query of his own book (sources `bb894979` + `dec8a807`): "Hapgood **never names or discusses the word 'Jaferiye'**… it appears in his book only within the translations he reproduced." Word is from the Afet İnan 1954 translation (his Note 3). Hapgood's real anchor = the inscription's "time of Alexander the Great" phrase → "Alexandrian science / Library of Alexandria" (verbatim).
- The word *cağferiye* is **Piri Reis's own** (mis-transliteration of *jughrafiya* = geography, per Kahle). The "fingerprint" framing was the script's own dramatization, credited to no one who said it.
