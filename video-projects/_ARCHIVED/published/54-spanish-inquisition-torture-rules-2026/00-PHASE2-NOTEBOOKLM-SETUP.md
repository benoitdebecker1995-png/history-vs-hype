## Sources to acquire (priority order)

### Tier 1 — Primary documents (the artifact itself)

1. **Torquemada, *Compilación de las Instrucciones del Oficio de la Santa Inquisición*** (1484, with 1488 + 1498 additions; 1561 Toledo compilation)
   - PRIORITY 1 — the document the entire video is anchored on
   - **Free download:** [archive.org/details/BRes14068130Despacho](https://archive.org/details/BRes14068130Despacho) (1561 Toledo compilation, original Spanish)
   - **Critical edition (preferred):** Sierra Corella, ed., *Las Instrucciones de la Inquisición Española* — search libgen.is, archive.org
   - **English translation:** None known to be widely available — language gap is the moat
   - **Key article to verify:** Article XV (torture rules), other articles on procedure
   - **User language verification:** Spanish ✓

2. **Bernard Gui, *Practica Inquisitionis Heretice Pravitatis*** (1323) — earlier inquisitor's manual showing acquittal procedures
   - Used as secondary exhibit if torture rules need a procedural counterpart
   - **Available:** BnF Ms. Lat. 14930; Latin text in Mollat ed. (1926) or Douais ed. (1886)
   - **Free download:** archive.org search "Practica Inquisitionis"
   - **User language verification:** Latin ✓

### Tier 2 — Authoritative scholarly revisionists

3. **Henry Kamen, *The Spanish Inquisition: A Historical Revision*** (Yale University Press, 4th ed. 2014)
   - PRIORITY 1 — the canonical revisionist scholarship; sources the 1-2% torture rate statistic
   - **Free download:** [archive.org/details/spanishinquisiti00henr](https://archive.org/details/spanishinquisiti00henr) (1998 ed.)
   - **Preferred edition:** 4th ed. 2014 — likely on libgen.is
   - **Use:** verify torture-rate statistic (Phase 1 claim 3.1), gaol-conditions claim (3.5), execution totals (3.4)

4. **Edward Peters, *Inquisition*** (Free Press, 1988; reissued UC Press 1989)
   - PRIORITY 1 — the second canonical revisionist; procedural-history framing
   - **Free download:** [archive.org/details/inquisition00pete](https://archive.org/details/inquisition00pete)
   - **Use:** verify "Black Legend" myth-formation, secular-vs-ecclesiastical procedural comparison (Phase 1 claim cluster 4)

### Tier 3 — Specialized recent scholarship

5. **Ron E. Hassner, *Anatomy of Torture*** (Cornell UP, 2022) — extends his 2020 *Security Studies* paper
   - Quantitative analysis of Inquisition torture records using AHN archives
   - **Free download:** [gwern.net/doc/history/2020-hassner.pdf](https://gwern.net/doc/history/2020-hassner.pdf) (article version)
   - **Use:** modern empirical re-analysis of torture statistics; cross-validates Kamen

6. **Lu Ann Homza, *The Spanish Inquisition 1478-1614: An Anthology of Sources*** (Hackett, 2006)
   - Primary-source documents in English translation
   - Use: English-text excerpts of decrees, trial records, edicts of grace
   - **Find on:** libgen.is, archive.org

### Tier 4 — Optional context

7. **Helen Rawlings, *The Spanish Inquisition*** (Wiley-Blackwell, 2006) — clean textbook overview, useful for cross-validation of dates/structure
8. **Henry Charles Lea, *A History of the Inquisition of Spain*** (4 vols., 1906-1907) — the 19th-century OG comparative study; ⭐ Tier 4 because it's secondary to Kamen but provides the original statistical work that Kamen built on
   - **Free download:** archive.org search

Q1 — Article XV Torture Rules

Which procedural protections are listed? Is there a time limit, a

```
Q2 — Statistical reality of torture rate
What does Henry Kamen state, with page number and direct quote, about
the actual rate of torture use in Spanish Inquisition trials? Is the
"1-2% of cases" figure accurate? What's the source archive Kamen used?
Which period does the statistic apply to?
```

```
Q3 — Comparison with contemporary secular courts
According to Kamen and Peters, how did Spanish Inquisition torture
procedure compare to contemporary Spanish secular courts of the same
era? Was the Inquisition more or less restrictive procedurally? Quote
direct passages with page numbers. Was it true that secular courts
under "judício sumarísimo" had no procedural torture limits?
```

```
Q4 — The "15-minute cap" claim
Search Kamen, Peters, and Hassner for any statement about a maximum
duration per torture session. Did the Instrucciones specify a time
cap (15 minutes or any other duration)? If yes, quote with page. If
no scholarly source supports this claim, flag it as Phase 1 ❓ to be
EXCLUDED from the script.
```

```
Q5 — Verbatim phrase for the on-screen exhibit
What is the single most quotable Spanish-language phrase from
Torquemada's Instrucciones that names a specific procedural torture
limitation? The line that, displayed on screen with a translation
overlay, would serve as Exhibit A. Provide: original Spanish, English
translation, article number, folio number, and source archive (PARES
or archive.org).
```

```
Q6 — Walk-Away test

```
