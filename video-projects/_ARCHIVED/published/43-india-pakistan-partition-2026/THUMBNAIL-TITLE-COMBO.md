## The Principle

Thumbnail and title must **complement, not repeat**. Together they tell a complete story — the title provides one piece of information, the thumbnail provides the missing piece.

**But complement ≠ cryptic.** The thumbnail must be instantly comprehensible at 160x90px in under 2 seconds. If it requires context to understand, it fails — no matter how clever the complement is.

## Why "ELIMINATE SALIENT" Was Wrong as #1

The original analysis over-indexed on "zero information overlap" and "niche uniqueness" while ignoring the framework's own rules:
- **12-char limit:** "ELIMINATE SALIENT" is 17 chars. Fails.
- **2-second comprehension:** "Salient" is military jargon. 99% of scrollers won't know what it means. Fails.
- **Phone-size readability:** Dense text + dark background + small map icon = noise at 160x90px.

"MOVED" on a map passes every test. It's not clever. It doesn't need to be.
