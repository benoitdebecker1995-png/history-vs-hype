## 4. Confounds to acknowledge

- **Topic asymmetry vs #52.** Slave trade has a streamer modern-hook (Asmongold/Destiny) that hijab doesn't. Comment volume and engagement may be elevated regardless of script edits. Quality signal needs comment *content* not count.
- **Higher search anchor volume.** *atlantic slave trade* 4,354/mo means more potential cold-start viewers without subscriber context. Cold viewers may bounce faster — affects retention floor independent of script edits.
- **Pre-existing rebuttal pre-empts in script.** Per PROJECT-STATUS, this video already includes anti-rebuttal lines for Latin-pedantry, Zurara-as-propaganda, Afonso-as-Christian-puppet, Britain-ended-it. These are independent of the Fig Tree edit pass and may also affect retention.
- **Format A/B vs Format C.** #52 was Format C; #56 is Format A/B per script metadata. Different baseline expectations. Format A/B typically runs faster pacing — face-hold for slow quote performance is more divergent from baseline here than in #52.
- **Filming + delivery variance.** Same as #52. If pointer beats are read flatly, test is of delivery, not edit.
