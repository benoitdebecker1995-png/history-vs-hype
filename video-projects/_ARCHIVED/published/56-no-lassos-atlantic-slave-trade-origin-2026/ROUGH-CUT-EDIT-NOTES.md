## LESSONS LEARNED (candidates for memory promotion)

1. **User's on-the-fly cut instinct = reinforcement + lead-in lines.** Pattern matches script-tightening pass. Worth trusting in future shoots; could pre-flag these in teleprompter as "optional skip."
2. **Rhetorical pivots get dropped on long takes** because they don't carry information — they carry irony. Flag load-bearing pivot lines in teleprompter (bold/color).
3. **Numbers drift on the second number of a range.** "800 to a thousand" → "800 to 2000." Highlight numerical ranges in teleprompter or film as standalone pickups.
4. **Duplicate phrases ("He takes his royal fifth" x2)** survive long takes — classic mid-take re-do nobody catches without script-compare. Worth auto-running this comparison on every rough cut.
