## Prompt 3: Author Bias Analysis

```
For each of the three primary sources on Thermopylae, I need an AUTHOR PROFILE:

HERODOTUS:
- Who was he? Where was he from? When did he write?
- How many years after Thermopylae was he writing?
- What were his sources? (Did he interview eyewitnesses? Travel to the site?)
- What was his political/cultural agenda? Who was his audience?
- What do modern scholars say about his reliability on Thermopylae specifically?

DIODORUS SICULUS:
- Same questions as above
- Critically: he drew on Ephorus, a LOST source. What do we know about Ephorus?
- Where does Diodorus clearly diverge from Herodotus, and what does that tell us about his different source?

CTESIAS OF CNIDUS:
- Same questions as above
- He was a Greek physician at the PERSIAN court. How does that affect his account?
- What Persian records might he have accessed?
- Why do only fragments survive?

For each author, give me one QUOTABLE LINE that captures their bias — something I can read on camera to introduce them.

Use [1], [2] citation markers. Include a SOURCES section formatted as:
Author Last, First. "Full Title." Publisher, Year. p. XX. [Translator: Name]
```
