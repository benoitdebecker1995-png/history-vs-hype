### [P1] 1878 Sulu-Overbeck/Dent Agreement — Jawi Original + Side-by-Side Translations
- **Type:** Foundational colonial document — the source of the entire dispute
- **Original language:** Jawi (Arabic-script Malay)
- **Translations:** Conklin/Beyer 1946 ("GRANT OF A PERMANENT LEASE"); Maxwell-Gibson 1924 ("grant and cede"); Saleeby 1908 ("to grant and cede")
- **Repository:** Philippine Official Gazette (Conklin vs Maxwell-Gibson side-by-side); British National Archives (BNBC transfer records)
- **Access:** FREE — https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1968/12/01/1878-grant-of-lease-by-the-sultan-of-sulu-to-britain-profession-conklin-translation-vis-a-vis-maxwell-and-gibson-translation/
- **Key content:** The word "pajak" in the cession/lease clause — the entire dispute hinges on this one word
- **On-screen potential:** VERY HIGH — side-by-side translations on official government letterhead; ideal Rule 31 document reveal

### [A9] Conklin, Harold C. (1946) — Translation of the 1878 Deed + Related Ateneo Scholarship
- **Type:** The originating "permanent lease" translation
- **Confirmed context (2026-04-20 research):** In August 1946, Harold C. Conklin — then a 20-year-old research assistant to Dr. H. Otley Beyer at the University of the Philippines — translated the 1878 Deed from its Arabic-script (Jawi) original. The manuscript had been "found by Mr. Quintero in Washington." Conklin rendered "padjak"/"pajakkan" as "lease" — specifically "a lease in perpetuity" or "arrendamiento."
- **Publication pathway:** Conklin's 1946 translation did not appear in a standalone academic publication — it circulated via U.P. and Bureau of Public Affairs channels, then received authoritative reproduction in the **Philippine Official Gazette, December 1968** (see [P1]). The Conklin translation thus effectively lives in [P1] — but its 1946 provenance is a separate narrative point.
- **Best scholarly discussion of the Conklin translation:** Philippine Studies journal (Ateneo de Manila) — "Legal Aspects of the North Borneo Question" — https://archium.ateneo.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=4478&context=phstudies (paywalled to WebFetch but publicly accessible)
- **Companion article:** "The Philippine Claim on North Borneo: Another Look" — *Philippine Studies* — https://archium.ateneo.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1213&context=phstudies
- **Priority:** HIGH — the 1946 provenance narrative + Philippine Studies scholarly discussion
- **Status:** ✅ IN NOTEBOOK (both Philippine Studies articles from Ateneo archium)
