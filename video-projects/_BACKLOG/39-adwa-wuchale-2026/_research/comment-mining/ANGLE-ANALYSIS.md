## What this changes about the locked angle

**The script's locked angle (forensic close-read of Article 17) is a low-signal audience demand.** Comments do not ask "what did Article 17 actually say?" or "did Italy mistranslate on purpose?" — that's a *channel choice*, not an *audience pull*.

This isn't necessarily a problem. The channel's edge is forensic primary-source close-read; that's what differentiates HvH from Armchair Historian's military-tactics narration or History Journo's mainstream-doc style. But running a low-demand angle without augmentation means we're shipping into a quieter market for the same effort.

**The Italian political fallout angle the grill flagged as a differentiation lane has zero organic demand** — drop it from the script outline. Don't expand §6/§7 in that direction.

## Path A vs B vs C — script revision options

### PATH A — Keep forensic angle, add Taytu thread (RECOMMENDED)

Make Empress Taytu the **agent who discovers the discrepancy** — grounded in Extra History [8/9] organic comments + Vid 4 [42]. Script becomes:

> Italy rigged the treaty. Empress Taytu read it in both languages. She told her husband to prepare for war.

**Why this works:**
- Forensic close-read (channel DNA) preserved as centerpiece
- Taytu adds a HUMAN PROTAGONIST that 3 of 4 competitors miss
- Aligns with high audience-demand cluster
- Doesn't change the heist framing — Taytu is the heist's discoverer, not its target
- ADR-0003 still satisfied (mechanism word "rigged" leads)

**Title adjustment:** B-Taytu candidate — *"Italy Rigged the Wuchale Treaty. Empress Taytu Caught It Before the Battle of Adwa."* — 12 words, anchor at end (still indexable), mechanism leads, Taytu as protagonist bridge.

**Script work:** 1-2 new beats in §1 (cold open establishes Taytu's bilingualism) + §2/§3 (Taytu reads both versions, alerts Menelik). Existing rough structure already mentions Taytu indirectly; promote her to named protagonist. Adds ~30-45 spoken seconds.

### PATH B — Strict forensic, lean into Menelik disinformation

Stay with current rough structure but expand on Menelik's documented disinformation/weapons-procurement campaign as the "counter-rigging" — Italy rigs the treaty; Menelik counter-rigs with French/Russian weapons + diplomatic deception. Mechanism-symmetric.

**Audience signal:** Vid 3 [7, 21, 49] explicitly praise Menelik's "disinformation campaign that would make WWII British Counterintelligence blush." Moderate signal.

**Risk:** Forfeits the Taytu cluster. The video stays in the channel-DNA lane but doesn't capture the unmet-demand audience.

### PATH C — Hybrid

Centerpiece is forensic close-read of Article 17 (Path A core). Taytu as agent of discovery (1-2 beats, Path A bonus). Menelik counter-rigging as Act 2 (Path B bonus). Drop Italian political fallout entirely.

**Risk:** runtime overrun. The current script-draft is already targeting 10-12 min. Adding Taytu beats + Menelik counter-rigging beats while keeping forensic close-read could push to 13+ min, blowing the channel's 12-min hard cap.

**Recommendation:** **Path A.** Compress on Italian political fallout (drop from script entirely — zero demand), add Taytu as discoverer (~30-45 sec), keep total runtime within target. Path C is too greedy for an 8-12 min video.
