### Angle A: Quran vs. Tafsir — "The Verse They Got Wrong" [RECOMMENDED]

**Thesis:** The Quranic "Verse of the Hijab" (Q. 33:53) was literally a curtain drawn between two men at a dinner party — not a dress code for women. The story of how a household privacy screen became a universal religious obligation is the story of how named medieval scholars made three specific procedural moves that the Quran never authorized.

**Mechanism (HOW):**
1. Q. 33:53 revealed during Prophet's wedding — Anas Ibn Malik overstaying → Prophet draws physical curtain to exclude him. The word *hijab* = curtain/barrier, not headscarf
2. Q. 33:33 ("stay in your houses") explicitly addresses ONLY the Prophet's wives ("You are not like any other women") — medieval exegetes al-Tha'labi, al-Biqa'i extend this to ALL believing women
3. Q. 24:60 gives older women exception to remove garments — Ibn al-Jawzi (d. 1200) overrides: "young or old, a free woman's hair must be covered" (*Zad al-masir* 6:63)

**Specificity bomb hook:** "The Quran verse about the hijab was revealed to remove an overstaying dinner guest. Not a single word in it is addressed to women."

**Verified quotes with page numbers:**
- Mernissi, *Veil and the Male Elite*, p. 85: *"The hijab — literally 'curtain' — 'descended,' not to put a barrier between a man and a woman, but between two men."*
- Reda/Amin: al-Tha'labi applied Q. 33:33 (wives-of-prophet only) to "believing women in general"
- Reda/Amin: Ibn al-Jawzi — *"young or old, a (free) woman's hair must be covered"* (Zad 6:63)

**Modern relevance:** Every fatwa on hijab today descends from these 3 named scholars' extensions — not from the Quran itself. The debate is about medieval jurisprudence masquerading as scripture.

**Source confidence:** HIGH — corroborated by Mernissi (two books), Reda/Amin, Karen Bauer (Gender Hierarchy in the Quran). All in notebook.

**Thesis:** Before Islam, veiling was FORBIDDEN for slaves and prostitutes under Assyrian law. Islam inherited a pre-existing social technology, not a religious invention.

**Mechanism (HOW):**
1. Middle Assyrian Laws (c. 1200 BCE): noble wives/daughters MUST veil; slaves/harlots FORBIDDEN to veil — penalty for illegal veiling: flogging + pitch on head + ears cut off (Ahmed p. 14)
