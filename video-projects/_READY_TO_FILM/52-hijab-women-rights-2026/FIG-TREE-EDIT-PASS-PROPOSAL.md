## 1. Framework — What ports, what doesn't

| Fig Tree pattern | Port? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Default to talking head; cut only on **named artifact / instructional cue / direct evidence / map** | **PORT** | Already HvH's stated rule. Fig Tree's discipline is sharper — codify "earn the cut." |
| **Spatial pointer language** while reading a document ("up here," "this part," "the line at the bottom") | **PORT** | Pure win. Calm Prosecutor compatible. Turns text-block VO into evidence narration. |
| Read **OVER** the document; don't cut back to face mid-quote | **PORT** | Already implied; make explicit in edit guide. |
| Verbal-only section transitions (no music stinger) | **PORT** | Fig Tree's restraint is closer to Prosecutor voice than aggressive packaging. Drop "Music brief stinger" annotations. |
| Long talking-head holds during context/backstory (2–5 min before first cut) | **PORT (calibrated)** | HvH already does this in Format C. Fig Tree's data validates it — don't break for the sake of cutaway. |
| **Rhetorical question as section beat** ("How are you supposed to know what the gods want from you?") | **PORT** | Hijab §VI already does it. Add one more in §V. |
| Parasocial register ("howdy," "bye bitch," "love you lots," "i'm in love with literally all of you") | **SKIP** | Breaks Prosecutor voice. The whole point of HvH's differentiator. |
| Modern slang ("trust fund baby," "shit slaps," "John locked in") | **SKIP** | Same. |
| Casual sourcing without page numbers | **SKIP** | HvH requires page numbers. Already enforced. |
| Environmental signature close ("the sun has departed") | **SKIP** | N/A for controlled-lighting talking head; also Prosecutor doesn't have a "casual exit." |
| "Welcome home" handoff after the hook | **TRANSLATE** | HvH equivalent already exists structurally: thesis statement after the cold open (§I closer line "States have always used clothing to mark social class. Islam wasn't unique…"). Don't add new ritual. |
| Philosophical zoom-out before factual close | **TRANSLATE** | HvH version = the document-callback closer, which §VIII already does. Sharpen the spatial framing on the side-by-side. |
| Anticipation phrase before a section ("what comes next, you're not even ready") | **TRANSLATE** | Prosecutor version: "And then the system tells on itself." Drop into §VI handoff. |

### HvH differentiators Fig Tree LACKS — preserve at all costs

- **Page-number citations on every quote** (Fig Tree: rarely)
- **Steel-manning the opposing position** (§VIII personal-spiritual-practice disclaimer)
- **Symmetric treatment** (Iran 1936 secular BEFORE Iran 1983 Islamist)
- **Mechanism-over-narrative tone** (no "trust fund baby" character beats)
- **Acknowledged ambiguity** ("The jurisprudential rules are 800 years old. The states enforcing them — in either direction — are just the latest ones.")

These are the moat. Fig Tree's editing rhythm doesn't conflict with them, but her *voice* does. Port the rhythm; keep the voice.

### §VI — PLAGUE TEST (5:50 – 6:40)

**Already Fig-Tree-shaped.** The rhetorical question structure (lines 207–208) is exactly her primary pacing device:

> *"If the veil was genuinely about female modesty, a plague killing everyone should have veiled everyone or veiled no one. Instead it cuts cleanly on the class line…"*

**Single port — sequence:**

Currently the al-Maqrizi document leads, then the rhetorical question follows. Fig Tree's stronger order: rhetorical question FIRST (held on face), then document as evidence answering it.

**Proposed reorder:**
1. Setup: "Cairo, 1438. Bubonic plague…"
2. **Pose the test on face:** "If the veil really was about modesty, what would a plague do? It would veil everyone, or veil no one."
3. **Cut to al-Maqrizi document:** "Instead the Sultan banned women from the streets on pain of death — except slave women, who could leave for household supplies, under one condition…"
4. Reveal: spatial pointer to operative clause "...so long as they did not veil and could easily be distinguished from *free women*."
5. Verdict: "Free women hidden, slave women visible. The variable being managed wasn't women's bodies. It was which bodies could be distinguished at a glance."

**Why:** Earns the document reveal as the *answer to a posed question* rather than narration that happens to have a quote in it. Fig Tree's Cythians-leather-samples sequence does exactly this. Slight rewrite, no material added.

## 4. Candidate rules for promotion (HOLD until 2nd worked example confirms)

If these edits land well on #52 and a second video (#56 Slave Trade — Format C, document-heavy) replicates the win, candidates for promotion:

1. **Spatial-Pointer Document Reveal (Rule candidate)** — Format C reveals of multi-clause primary documents must use pointer language ("the first line…the line that matters…on the left…on the right"). One pointer per operative clause. Codify into `script-writer-v2` and `WRITING-VOICE-AND-STYLE.md` PART 4 (Document Treatment).

2. **Earn-the-Cut Discipline (Rule candidate / extension of existing rule)** — Cuts away from talking head require one of four triggers: named artifact, instructional cue, direct primary-source evidence, geographic orientation. Connective tissue stays on face. Codify into EDIT-GUIDE template.

3. **Question-First Evidence Sequencing (Rule candidate)** — Rhetorical-question beats pose the test on face, then cut to document as the answer. Don't bury the question after the document. Sibling to existing baseline-before-exception rule.

4. **Stinger-Free Chapter Cards (Project-level convention)** — Silent text cards for YouTube chapter nav; no music stings. Belongs in `WORKSPACE_RULES.md` §9 or per-channel playbook.

5. **Quote Performance Stays on Face (Rule candidate)** — When delivering a verbatim primary-source quote with emotional weight (insult, defiance, command), stay on talking head; put citation as on-screen text overlay. Cut to B-roll only AFTER the quote, for the structural reading. Distinguishes "performing the text" from "analyzing the artifact."

**Do not promote until validated.** This proposal is a single worked example; n=1 is not a rule.
