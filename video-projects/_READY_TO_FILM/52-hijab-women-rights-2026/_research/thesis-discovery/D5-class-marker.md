## 5 Pre-Islamic Veiling Codes Surfaced

### 1. Middle Assyrian Laws (c. 1200 BCE)
- **Must veil:** wives/daughters of seigniors, concubines, ex-"sacred prostitutes"
- **Forbidden to veil:** harlots, slaves
- **Penalty for illegal veiling:** **flogging, pitch on head, ears cut off**
- **Signal:** "respectable" under male protection vs "publicly available"
- **Islamic transformation:** Q. 33:59 explicitly maintained the distinction — free women cover so they're "recognized and not annoyed"
- Source 8, p. 14, 854

### 2. Sasanian Persia (224–640 CE) ⭐
- **Must veil:** upper-class and imperial women
- **Class signal:** harem size and seclusion = owner's wealth/power
- **Penalty:** "certificates of disobedience" for wives flouting authority
- **Islamic transformation:** post-conquest, Muslims **assimilated Sasanian domestic organization** (eunuchs, harem expansion) — "veil became commonplace for new Muslim upper class"
- Source 8, p. 5, 19-20, 843, 864-868; Source 10, p. 81-82, 1134-1136

### 3. Byzantine Empire (5th–6th c.)
- **Must veil:** "honest" women, noblewomen
- **Forbidden to veil:** prostitutes — absence of veil was their identifying marker
- **Signal:** women "cloistered as prisoners"; Psellos commended noblewoman who covered even her hands
- **Islamic transformation:** Mediterranean mores of female invisibility "patriarchalized" Islamic society
- Source 8, p. 26, 877-878; Source 4, p. 108, 156-157

### 4. Classical Athens (5th–4th c. BCE)
- **Must veil:** respectable married wives
- "Respectable" wives expected invisible — not even going to market
- **Signal:** preservation of *oikos*
- Source 8, p. 28-29, 881-882

### 5. Jewish/Talmudic (3rd–6th c.) ⭐⭐
- **Must veil:** free Jewish women ("Daughters of Israel")
- **Forbidden to veil:** enslaved women — legally distinguished by lack of head covering
- **DIRECT BRIDGE:** **Ibn al-Jawzi (d. 1200) used Talmudic free/slave distinctions to override Quranic Q. 24:60 exception** — "free woman's hair must always be covered"
- Source 4, p. 162-163; Source 6, p. 11, 127, 157, 409, 651
