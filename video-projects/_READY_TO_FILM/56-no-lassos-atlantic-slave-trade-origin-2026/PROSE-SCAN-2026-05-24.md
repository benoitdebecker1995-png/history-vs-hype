## PART B — Corpus-level observations

Three vocabulary/style clusters appear repeatedly in our script but are absent from Fig Tree:

1. **Industrial/engineering metaphors for human systems** — "engineered," "rewiring," "mechanism," "retooled," "architecture." Fig Tree uses these words only for literal physical objects (clocks, flutes, buildings).
2. **Corporate-design verbs for historical processes** — "scaled," "iterative," "top-down." Fig Tree uses biological/narrative verbs ("diverged," "morphed," "broke conventions").
3. **Meta-logical signposting** — "acknowledge the baseline truth," "proves European demand wasn't passive." Fig Tree's meta is environmental/personal ("the sun's like really getting in my eyes," "my cat fell asleep on the chair").

**Implication:** if you decide cluster #1 is real (and you accept the Fig Tree register as the target), expect ~5+ surface edits across the back half of the script (EVIDENCE 3 + CLOSING are densest). Cluster #2 likely overlaps with cluster #1.

**Counter-consideration:** Fig Tree register isn't the only legitimate human register. Your "Calm Prosecutor" voice intentionally trades some Fig Tree warmth for analytical precision. Don't strip ALL design — pick the strongest 3-5 fixes.
