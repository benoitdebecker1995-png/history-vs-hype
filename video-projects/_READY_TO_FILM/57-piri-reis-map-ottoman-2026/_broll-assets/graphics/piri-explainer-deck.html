Exhibit · The Word
جغرافيا
jughrafiya
Arabic for “geography”
→drop one dot
جعفريا
cağferiye
a word that means nothing
In Arabic, ghayn (غ) and ʿayn (ع) are the same letter — separated by a single dot. Drop the dot, swap one more letter, and “geography” turns into a word no one can translate.
Kahle 1933 · Pinto · McIntosh

THE WORD
whose error · Charles Hapgood, 1966
He read a misspelled word — “cağferiye” — as the name of an ancient, mysterious kind of map.
It just means “geography.” The eight maps were ordinary Ptolemaic atlases, reprinted across Renaissance Europe.
McIntosh · Kahle, “A Lost Map of Columbus,” 1933

Exhibit · Mistake 2 of 2
2
THE DATE
whose error · Piri Reis himself
He thought his maps reached back to Ptolemy I — to Alexander’s day. They didn’t.
That one slip is the only place “the time of Alexander” on the source list comes from — the line Hapgood built his Library of Alexandria on.
McIntosh, p. 18 · Inscription no. 6
