request, no cluster asking for the same thing. Topic selection cannot come from the audience because
there isn't one yet.

## The working reading

What separated the videos that moved is not format. It is whether **an audience for the subject
already existed before the video did, and whether anyone else was answering it.**

The open bet: aim claim provenance at subjects with **mass existing recognition**. The small
successes came from small subjects — that limits the ceiling, not the method.

Caveat honestly. "Existing demand" is inferred from ~50 uploads, most under 300 views. Enough for a
direction, not a formula. And mass recognition brings competition.

**The test is not "is this famous." It is "is this famous AND unanswered."**

## The kill test — apply before recommending anything

This is what nearly cost a production cycle last time. For the exact angle, not the general topic:

1. **Named competing videos with real view counts.** Not "there is some competition."
2. **Which bet is this?** "Nobody has thought of this" or "nobody has done it properly." Say which.
3. **Does demand exist before the video?** Search volume, a live news event, a cultural moment.
4. **Can the central reveal survive contact with the sources?** Trojan Horse failed here.
5. **Is the payoff a succession of discoveries, or one correction?** One correction is a Short.

---

## What a recommendation must contain

One recommendation, not a menu. With:

- The decisive evidence.
- The strongest counterargument.
- What would reverse it.
- How it could fail.
- **A written prediction of the outcome, recorded before publishing.**
