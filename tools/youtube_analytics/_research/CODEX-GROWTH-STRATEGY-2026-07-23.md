## Verified constraints — advice that violates these is worse than none

- **You cannot earn a push with post-publish tuning.** Distribution is decided upstream (the initial impression test, topic, packaging). Don't propose "improve retention to get more views" — retention is a HOLD lever, ρ≈0 with impressions.
- **No topic formula exists in our data.** Territorial is a weak hint (median 3,330 vs 2,240, not significant), NOT a law. Don't tell us to "make territorial disputes." Topics pass a demand gate (`/greenlight`), then win on recognizability + packaging.
- **Verified packaging levers already in doctrine:** search-anchored head term, name the exact entity, evidence-promise title, one clean focal thumbnail (not a document, red isn't a lever), protect the 5–10% post-hook seam (no preamble/recap/roadmap). Don't re-derive these — assume them and build past them.
- **Evergreen identity is non-negotiable:** "history with modern relevance," not current-affairs punditry. A news hook is an optional accelerant, never the identity.
- **Small-channel reality:** native A/B can't reach significance; findings from n<30 are directional. Moves must be testable by a solo creator, not "run a 10-cell experiment."
