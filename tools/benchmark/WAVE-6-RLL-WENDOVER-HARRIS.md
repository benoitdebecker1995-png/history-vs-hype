### Rule 46 Update — Breathing Room After Evidence

**Current Rule 46** says "So What?" after every quote — explain the implication. But doesn't specify HOW MUCH breathing room.

**Wave 6 finding — Post-Evidence Absorption:**

| Channel | After Major Evidence | Sentences Before Next Fact | Assessment |
|---------|---------------------|--------------------------|------------|
| **Harris** | 3 full sentences of interpretation | 3 | BEST — viewer absorbs |
| **Wendover** | 0 — immediately piles on next data point | 0 | Too fast for devastating evidence |
| **RLL** | 0 — immediately piles on next data point | 0 | Too fast — data dump fatigue |

**Proposed Rule 46 addition:**

After devastating evidence (death tolls, betrayal quotes, legal injustice stats), give the viewer **2-3 sentences of interpretation** before the next NEW fact. This is NOT padding — it's the "So What?" expanded to include emotional processing.

Pattern: [Devastating quote/stat] → [1 sentence: what this means] → [1 sentence: why this matters / how this connects] → [1 sentence: transition to next evidence]

Example (Harris model):
> "As a student of history, one's prediction would have it be that the political evolution is likely to be in the direction that the PRC wants."
> This is a tactfully vague way of saying that Taiwan will probably be reunited with the PRC.
> Kissinger is kind of throwing Taiwan under the bus here.
> But his goal is to get leverage over the Soviets, so he's willing to give something up.

**HvH-adapted:**
> "The boundary shall follow the course of the river Akwayafe."
> That single sentence transferred 665 square kilometers and 300,000 people from one country to another.
> Not because of any survey. Not because anyone asked the residents. Because that's what the map showed.
> And the map was wrong.

**New finding — Narration During Visual Reveals:**

| Channel | During Reveals | Effect |
|---------|---------------|--------|

**Proposed Rule 44 addition:**

**"Let the Document Breathe" — Silence After Reveal:**

Pattern: [Staging phrase: "Here's what Article 7 actually says."] → [3-5 seconds: document appears, viewer reads] → [Active reading: "Notice this clause..."]

This leverages HvH's competitive advantage (on-screen documents) by treating each document reveal as a visual pattern interrupt.
