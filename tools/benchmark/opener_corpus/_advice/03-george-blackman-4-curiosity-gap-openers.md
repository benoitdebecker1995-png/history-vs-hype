# [George Blackman] 4 Curiosity Gap Openers for Video Scripts

Source: https://www.georgeblackman.com/write-on-time/steal-these-4-curiosity-gap-openers

## Framework
Each video segment should open with a curiosity gap and close with a payoff that resolves viewer questions.

## The 4 Templates

### 1. Aprilynne Alter — Underrated Importance
**Opening:** "[X] is by far the most important… and yet also the most overlooked."
**Questions raised:** What is [X]? Am I overlooking it? What negative effects result from my ignorance?
**Resolution:** Information that proves X is genuinely critical and explains why it gets ignored.

### 2. Mike Shake — First-Person Verification
**Opening:** "I want to see for myself [whether "X" is true]."
**Questions raised:** Is [X] true? What capabilities does it have? How will this be tested?
**Resolution:** First-person experiment proves/disproves X.

### 3. Cleo Abram — Direct Travel to the Subject
**Opening:** "I'm [travelling directly to] [the thing you're curious about]."
**Questions raised:** What will it look like? What will I discover? What purpose does it serve?
**Resolution:** Close-up footage + explanation.

### 4. Mrwhosetheboss — Pattern Revelation
**Opening:** "The first thing to understand is that [thing of interest] does [surprising statement needing clarification]."
**Questions raised:** How is this possible? Why does this behavior occur? Is there a conspiracy?
**Resolution:** Explanation of the underlying mechanism.

## All 4 Templates Summarized
- "[X] is by far the most important… and yet also the most overlooked."
- "I want to see for myself [whether "X" is true]."
- "I'm [travelling directly to] [the thing you're curious about]."
- "The first thing to understand is that [thing of interest] does [surprising thing needing clarification]."
