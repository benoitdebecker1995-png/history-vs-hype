# Forensic Audit: Rule Files vs. Source Authors

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Audited files:** ARTICLE-WRITING-STYLE-BIBLE.md, article-writer.md, STYLE-GUIDE.md, VOICE-PROFILE.md
**Audited against:** Knowledge of Pinker, Zinsser, Minto, Axios/Smart Brevity, Harari, Diamond, Bryson, Graeber, Mukherjee, Frankopan, Dawkins, Rosling techniques

## Rules That Oversimplify or Misrepresent

### A. "Zero Metadiscourse" goes further than Pinker recommends
Pinker argues against EXCESSIVE metadiscourse, not all of it. He acknowledges some is essential for reader orientation in complex arguments. The blanket "kill on sight" ban doesn't reflect this. Voice Profile already caught this (allows "It's important to mention..." for paradigm-shifting facts).

**Fix:** "Kill unless the phrase does genuine navigational work in a complex argument shift."

### B. "Strip ALL Qualifiers" contradicts Rule 11C
Rule 2 says kill all hedges. Rule 11C provides a ladder of appropriate hedging. Pinker's actual position: hedging is wrong when you're confident, appropriate when evidence is uncertain.

**Fix:** Rule 2 should say "Strip hedges on confident claims. For uncertain claims, use Rule 11C's hierarchy."

### C. "8-Word Verdict" is a heuristic, not a mechanical limit
Harari's devastating lines are often 10-15 words. The principle is brevity relative to surrounding prose.

**Fix:** "Short enough to feel like a gavel strike" rather than word-counting.

### D. "No Em Dashes" isn't a writing craft rule
Pinker, Bryson, and Mukherjee all use em dashes. The ban is an anti-AI perception tactic, not a style principle.

**Fix:** Recategorize from "style scrub" to "anti-AI tactical choice."

## Techniques Missing From the Rules

1. **Harari's Zoom Ladder** — shifting between micro and macro time scales within the same paragraph. Rules capture the Necker Cube flip but miss mid-paragraph zoom as a technique.

2. **Bryson's Ignorance Cascade** — showing how one person's ignorance cascades into centuries of wrong assumptions. The epistemological point, not just the narrative delivery.

3. **Mukherjee's Temporal Bookending** — the same person appears at 3-4 points in the piece, with their story advancing each time. Rules only capture opening/closing loop.

4. **Graeber's Genealogy of the Obvious** — tracing something taken for granted back to its arbitrary, invented origin. Rules capture moral dissonance but not the genealogy technique.

5. **Diamond's Natural Experiment framing** — treating historical comparisons as scientific experiments with control groups. Rules capture causal chains but not the experimental framing.

6. **Rosling's Bias Naming** — naming the specific cognitive bias before debunking it (gap instinct, negativity instinct, etc.). Rules capture the trend/drama distinction but not the naming technique.

## Overengineered Rules

1. **Rotation system tracks 9 dimensions** — by article 10, combinatorial constraints will make writing nearly impossible. Reduce to 4: openings, endings, limitation phrases, evidence delivery style.

2. **Rule 10C** ("at least one section under 100 words") forces structural choices that should emerge from content.

3. **Rule 16** (mid-article re-engagement at ~midpoint) — should happen wherever energy dips, not at a predetermined point.

4. **"Pinker's law" / "Howes's law" / "Bryson's law"** presented as universal — they're heuristics with exceptions (lead with person when person IS the surprise).
